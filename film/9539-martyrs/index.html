Pain and salvation. Rejection and worship. Despair, and then ecstasy. The world's fastest theatrical release—you will become a "witness" to film history. Since its French release in 2008, 'Martyrs' has sparked controversy with its brutally intense depictions, while leading audiences to an unpredictable conclusion that borders on the sublime, igniting fierce debate worldwide. The pinnacle of the "French horror" movement that dominated an era, and the masterpiece of the eccentric genius director Pascal Laugier, now revived in the Reiwa era with a 4K digital remaster in the world's fastest theatrical release. The origin of Laugier's consistent portrayal of women with the conviction to face despair, this film pushes terror and philosophical depth to their absolute limits, venturing into realms beyond human comprehension. Even 18 years later, it remains undimmed, continuing to exert immense influence on contemporary French filmmakers including Coralie Fargeat's 'The Substance' and Julia Ducournau's 'TITANE'. What awaits beyond the pain swirling with conflicting emotions is an unknown realm no other film has ever reached. *Please note: This theater will screen the 2K version. France in the early 1970s. A missing girl named Lucie is found wandering the streets, battered and emaciated. She had been imprisoned, tortured, and abused by someone for a long time, and escaped on her own. However, there were no signs of sexual abuse, and the motive for the crime remained unknown... 15 years later, one morning, a doorbell suddenly rings at a peaceful family home surrounded by forest. When the homeowner opens the door, Lucie (Mylène Jampanoï) stands there with a hunting rifle. Convinced she has finally found the person who abused her, she pulls the trigger of revenge. Anna (Morjana Alaoui), Lucie's best friend, receives a phone call from her and rushes to the house, turning away in horror at the carnage. The two set about disposing of the bodies and preparing to leave. But then, they are confronted with an unimaginable truth.
