キエフ裁判

2022 · 監督: セルゲイ・ロズニツァ · 106分

「ドンバス」「バビ・ヤール」などで世界的に注目を集めるウクライナのセルゲイ・ロズニツァ監督が、第2次世界大戦後にキエフ（キーウ）で行われたナチス関係者15名の国際軍事裁判を描いたドキュメンタリー。 1946年1月、キエフ。第2次世界大戦の独ソ戦において、ナチスドイツと地元警察がソ連領土内で起こしたユダヤ人虐殺事件の首謀者15名が、人道に対する罪で裁判にかけられた。 裁判では、母から幼子を奪って目の前で射殺するなど数々の残虐行為が暴かれる一方で、被告人弁論では自己弁明に終始する者、仲間に罪を擦りつける者、実行しなければ自分が殺されたと同情を得ようとする者など、戦犯たちの凡庸な素顔が浮かびあがっていく。

A documentary by Ukrainian director Sergei Loznitsa, who has drawn worldwide attention for films such as Donbass and Babi Yar, depicting the international military tribunal of 15 Nazi-affiliated individuals held in Kiev (Kyiv) after World War II. In January 1946, in Kiev, 15 masterminds behind the mass murder of Jews committed by Nazi Germany and local police on Soviet territory during the German-Soviet War of World War II were put on trial for crimes against humanity. The trial exposed numerous atrocities, such as tearing infants from their mothers and shooting them before their eyes, while in the defendants' statements, the banal true faces of the war criminals emerge—some devoting themselves entirely to self-justification, some shifting blame onto their comrades, and some seeking sympathy by claiming they would have been killed had they not carried out the orders.
