The first installment of the 2026-27 season of "MET Live Viewing," which records the latest opera performances from New York's Metropolitan Opera (MET) and screens them in cinemas. This recording captures Mozart's masterpiece opera "Così fan tutte," which vividly depicts the beauty of the moment when women release their hearts through the course of a romantic game that tests the fidelity of lovers (recorded from the October 3, 2026 performance). The elderly philosopher Don Alfonso, who declares that "there is no such thing as lasting love," proposes a wager to the young officers Guglielmo and Ferrando: to test the fidelity of the sisters Fiordiligi and Dorabella, to whom they are respectively engaged. Guglielmo and Ferrando pretend to go off to war and disguise themselves as foreign men, swapping partners to court the sisters.
