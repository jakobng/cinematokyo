負けへんで

Never Guilty

2026 · 監督: 本木克英 · 130分 · ドラマ

大阪の不動産デベロッパー社長・山岸忍が自身の身に起きた実際の事件をつづった書籍「負けへんで！　東証一部上場企業社長 vs 地検特捜部」を、「空飛ぶタイヤ」の本木克英監督のメガホンで実写映画化。身に覚えのない罪で逮捕・勾留され、一代で築き上げた会社も失った主人公が、最強弁護士軍団と共に司法の闇に立ち向かう姿を描く。 不動産デベロッパーの社長を務める藤堂繁は、社員に慕われながら多忙な日々を過ごしていた。2019年、藤堂は3年前の学校土地の買収について、関西地検から事情聴取の呼び出しを受ける。検事の鈴鳴はフレンドリーに藤堂に接するが、48日後、彼は30億円の業務上横領の罪で逮捕されてしまう。すでに逮捕されている学校の理事長と、藤堂の部下・木下と共謀した容疑がかけられたのだ。拘置所に収監された藤堂は、自らが一代で築き上げた会社を守るため、社長を退任する。絶望する藤堂のもとに届いた恋人・美奈からの手紙には、彼を支える覚悟と「負けないで！」という言葉がつづられていた。冤罪を晴らすべく戦う覚悟を決めた彼は、元検事・元判事・刑事弁護の専門家・企業法務の専門家が集まった最強の弁護士チームと共に逆襲を開始する。 内野聖陽が藤堂繁役で主演を務め、藤堂を追い詰める検事・鈴鳴通子役で松本若菜、藤堂の無実を信じて支え続ける友人・君塚大作役で北村一輝、冤罪を晴らすため奔走する弁護士役で竜星涼、石丸幹二、藤本隆宏、藤堂の恋人・林美奈役で美村里江が共演。

A live-action film adaptation, directed by Katsuhide Motoki of The Flying Tire, of the book 'Don't Give Up! A TSE First Section Listed Company President vs. the Special Investigation Department' by Shinobu Yamagishi, president of an Osaka real estate developer, chronicling actual events that happened to him. It depicts a protagonist who is arrested and detained for a crime he did not commit and loses the company he built from scratch, as he fights against the darkness of the justice system alongside a team of the strongest lawyers. Shigeru Todo, president of a real estate developer, is admired by his employees and leads a busy life. In 2019, Todo is summoned by the Kansai District Public Prosecutors Office for questioning regarding the acquisition of school land three years earlier. Prosecutor Suzuna treats Todo in a friendly manner, but 48 days later he is arrested for embezzlement of 3 billion yen in the course of business. He is suspected of conspiring with the already-arrested school chairman and his subordinate Kinoshita. Imprisoned in a detention center, Todo resigns as president to protect the company he built from scratch. A letter from his girlfriend Mina arrives at the despairing Todo, expressing her resolve to support him and the words 'Don't give up!' Determined to fight to clear his name, he begins his counterattack together with the strongest team of lawyers, made up of a former prosecutor, a former judge, a criminal defense specialist, and a corporate legal specialist. Seiyo Uchino stars as Shigeru Todo, with Wakana Matsumoto as prosecutor Michiko Suzuna who corners him, Kazuki Kitamura as his friend Daisaku Kimizuka who believes in his innocence and continues to support him, Ryusei Ryo, Kanji Ishimaru, and Takahiro Fujimoto as lawyers working to clear the false charge, and Rie Mimura as Todo's girlfriend Mina Hayashi.
