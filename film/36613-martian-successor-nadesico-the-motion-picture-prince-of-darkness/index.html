機動戦艦ナデシコ The prince of darkness

Martian Successor Nadesico: The Motion Picture - Prince of Darkness

1998 · 監督: 佐藤竜雄 · 87分 · アニメーション / サイエンスフィクション / ドラマ / アクション

A sequel to the TV anime "Martian Successor Nadesico." The story is set three years after the end of the TV series. Compared to the TV series, which had a strong comedic tone, the story is more serious and hard-hitting, with a denser plot (though gags are sprinkled in at key points). This is because the TV series barely depicted the growth of Akito, who was supposed to be the protagonist, and director Sato, feeling this was "too pitiful" for him, decided to tell his story from Ruri's perspective. For this reason, Sato also wrote the screenplay. Additionally, the production was born out of reflection on the TV version's rushed production schedule and its tendency to pander for laughs, feeling it had "played around too much." Producer Toshimichi Otsuki had hoped for a more serious content, and with the request (even among staff, who were okay with comedic parts) for a convincing portrayal of serious scenes, it was produced as a culmination of the series. Yuki Uchida, who was relatively unknown at the time, appeared as a voice actor (Uchida later joked that she spent more time doing interviews than actual voice acting work). A novelization by Sato was planned but later canceled. Most of the manuscript data seems to have been lost, with only a portion remaining, which was included in "Martian Successor Nadesico Novel Te+." Winner of the 30th Seiun Award in 1999 in the Film/Theater and Media categories. Winner of the 21st Anime Grand Prix in the Grand Prix category. A shortened version of the ending theme "Dearest" was broadcast during a special program on Kids Station.
