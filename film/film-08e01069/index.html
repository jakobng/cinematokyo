ごじゃっぺタクシー

2026 · 監督: オガワセイイチ · 85分

死に場所を求めて故郷・茨城に戻った男が、謎のタクシー運転手に導かれて過去に縁を持った女たちのもとを巡りながら、自らの人生と向き合っていく姿を描いたヒューマンドラマ。 東京で内縁の妻と息子と暮らす中村健人は、ある日突然、身に覚えのない借金を背負わされてしまう。家庭は崩壊し、追い詰められた中村は死に場所を求めて故郷・茨城に足を運ぶ。地元の神社で「楽に死ねますように」と願掛けした翌日、謎のタクシー運転手が姿を現し、中村の胸の内を詠む。彼が運転する“終活タクシー”に乗り込んだ中村は、過去に関係を持った女たちを巡る旅に出る。さまざまな再会を通して中村の無責任さや逃げ癖があぶり出されるなか、やがて彼はこのまま「死にたくない」という本音にぶつかっていく。 茨城県出身の同級生である俳優の岩田和浩と草野速仁がダブル主演を務めた。オガワセイイチが監督・脚本・編集、俳優の奥田瑛二が監修を担当。タイトルの「ごじゃっぺ」は茨城県で使用される方言で「でたらめ」を意味する。ゆうばり国際映画祭2026で観客賞を受賞。

A human drama depicting a man who returns to his hometown of Ibaraki seeking a place to die, and, guided by a mysterious taxi driver, visits the women he had relationships with in the past while confronting his own life. Kenji Nakamura, who lives in Tokyo with his common-law wife and son, suddenly finds himself saddled with debt he has no memory of. His family falls apart, and driven to the brink, Nakamura travels to his hometown of Ibaraki seeking a place to die. The day after he prays at a local shrine to 'die easily,' a mysterious taxi driver appears and recites Nakamura's innermost feelings. Boarding the 'end-of-life taxi' he drives, Nakamura sets out on a journey to visit the women he was involved with in the past. Through various reunions, Nakamura's irresponsibility and habit of running away are exposed, and eventually he confronts his true feeling: 'I don't want to die like this.' Actors Kazuhiro Iwata and Hayato Kusano, classmates from Ibaraki Prefecture, star in the dual lead roles. Seiichi Ogawa serves as director, screenwriter, and editor, with actor Eiji Okuda as supervisor. The title 'Gojappe' is an Ibaraki dialect word meaning 'nonsense.' It won the Audience Award at the Yubari International Fantastic Film Festival 2026.
