東のエデン 総集編 Air Communication

Eden of The East Compilation: Air Communication

2009 · 監督: 神山健治 · 123分 · アニメーション / ドラマ / 謎 / サイエンスフィクション

A compilation film of the TV anime of the same name broadcast in 2009. Kenji Kamiyama, director of the TV version, returns to reconstruct the TV series with an original screenplay. On Monday, November 22, 2010, missile attacks occur across Japan with zero casualties. The incident is dubbed 'Careless Monday,' but people gradually lose their sense of crisis. Three months later, Saki Morimi, a member of the university club 'Eden of the East' that develops mobile websites, meets a completely naked man named Akira Takizawa during a graduation trip to the United States.
