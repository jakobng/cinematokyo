ママがもうこの世界にいなくても　私の命の日記

All Our Tomorrows

2026 · 監督: 山戸結希 · 124分 · ファミリー / ドラマ

24歳で他界した遠藤和（のどか）さんが亡くなる10日前までつづり続けた手記「ママがもうこの世界にいなくても　私の命の日記」を、川口春奈が主演を務めて映画化したラブストーリー。 青森県に暮らす遠藤和と将一は、時にぶつかりながらも、かけがえのない日々を過ごしていた。そんなある日、和は医師からステージ4の大腸がんを宣告されてしまう。残された日々は限られていたが、将一は和を一生大切にすることを誓う。将一との子どもに会うことを願う和だったが、それは自身の命をつなぐ抗がん剤治療を止めるという、あまりにも重い選択をともなうものだった。 天真爛漫で芯の強い主人公・和を川口春奈が熱演し、和を献身的に支える夫・将一役で高杉真宙が共演。さらに、和の妹である櫛引遥と結花を松本穂香と中島瑠菜、両親の櫛引慎吾と美智子をデビット伊東と小林聡美、将一の会社の先輩・木本文太を笠原秀幸、和と一緒に回転寿司店で働く友人・澤夏子を清水くるみ、回転寿司店の店長・磯田茂をお笑いトリオ「東京03」の豊本明長、遥の恋人・佐野拓海を一ノ瀬颯、和の担当医・内藤静香を星野真里、和と同じくがん患者の友人・飯島ひかりを森田望智がそれぞれ演じる。監督は「溺れるナイフ」など繊細な感情表現と映像美で評価される山戸結希。

A love story adapted into film starring Haruna Kawaguchi, based on the journal "Even If Mama Is No Longer in This World: My Diary of Life" that Nodoka Endo, who passed away at age 24, kept writing until ten days before her death. Nodoka Endo and Shoichi live in Aomori Prefecture, spending irreplaceable days together even as they sometimes clash. Then one day, Nodoka is diagnosed by a doctor with stage 4 colon cancer. Though her remaining days are limited, Shoichi vows to cherish her for the rest of his life. Nodoka wishes to meet a child with Shoichi, but that comes with an unbearably heavy choice: stopping the chemotherapy that is sustaining her life. Haruna Kawaguchi gives a passionate performance as the innocent yet strong-willed protagonist Nodoka, with Mahiro Takasugi co-starring as her devoted husband Shoichi. Also appearing are Honoka Matsumoto and Runa Nakajima as Nodoka's sisters Haruka and Yuka Kushibiki, David Ito and Satomi Kobayashi as her parents Shingo and Michiko Kushibiki, Hideyuki Kasahara as Shoichi's senior colleague Fumita Kimoto, Kurumi Shimizu as Nodoka's friend and fellow conveyor-belt sushi restaurant worker Natsuko Sawa, Akimasa Toyomoto of the comedy trio Tokyo 03 as sushi restaurant manager Shigeru Isoda, Hayato Ichinose as Haruka's boyfriend Takumi Sano, Mari Hoshino as Nodoka's attending physician Shizuka Naito, and Mochizuki Morita as Hikari Iijima, a friend who is also a cancer patient. Directed by Yuki Yamato, acclaimed for her delicate emotional expression and visual beauty in films such as Drowning Love.
