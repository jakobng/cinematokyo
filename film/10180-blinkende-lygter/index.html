A legendary masterpiece and a historic box-office smash, often called the greatest film in Danish cinema history. Four supremely unlucky rogues, each haunted by absurd childhood traumas. Despite their different ages and backgrounds, they plot a massive heist to turn around their lives of constant failure. After surviving a fierce shootout and making their escape, what lies ahead for them is... This is the role Mads plays! Arne, a gun-obsessed brute who solves every problem with violence and is constantly on edge. A young Mads’s ripped physique and mischievous sex appeal are simply irresistible...!
