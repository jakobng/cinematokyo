Across time and space, the fates of heroes collide—the ultimate chronoverse entertainment is born! From CJ ENM, the studio behind the Oscar-winning "Parasite," comes an unprecedented scale of time-spanning entertainment blockbuster. Five years in the making, 387 days of shooting—a grand adventure that carves a new monument in Korean film history now begins. An ancient cosmic punishment: aliens have been imprisoning prisoners inside human bodies. In modern-day Korea, android guards Gado and Thunder pursue rampaging aliens, while in the late Goryeo Dynasty 630 years ago, Taoist sorcerer Muruk and a mysterious woman named Ian who controls lightning engage in a fateful battle over the legendary "Divine Sword." And at the moment when time and space intersect, a battle beyond imagination begins. Directed by Choi Dong-hoon, the hitmaker behind "The Thieves" and "Assassination," each drawing over 10 million viewers. He has assembled a stellar cast including Ryu Jun-yeol, Kim Woo-bin, Kim Tae-ri, as well as So Ji-sub, Yeom Jung-ah, Jo Woo-jin, and Lee Ha-nui. Transcending time, those guided by destiny now clash for the future of humanity. In 2026, an unprecedented realm of imagination is unleashed on screen.
