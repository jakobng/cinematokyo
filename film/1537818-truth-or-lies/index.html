嘘もまことも

Truth or Lies

2025 · 監督: 磯部鉄平 · 104分 · ドラマ

「凪の憂鬱」「夜のまにまに」の磯部鉄平監督が、代行サービスという「嘘」をつく仕事を通して本当に大切なものを見つけていく女性を描いたヒューマンドラマ。 母が急死し、同僚である恋人・翔也に浮気され、人生のどん底に陥った田中梨沙。仕事を一時休職することになった彼女は、親友の美優から強引に誘われ、代行サービス会社で講演会の客を装うサクラのアルバイトをする。いつもは嘘をつけない梨沙だったが、会社社長の寺村に仕事ぶりを認められ、嘘をつく仕事に没頭していく。 映画・テレビドラマへの出演や多数のCM出演で活躍する朝見心が映画初主演を務め、人生に迷い葛藤しながらも前へ進んでいく主人公・梨沙を自然体で演じた。「ホゾを咬む」のミネオショウが恋人・翔也役、「すばらしき世界」の桜木梨奈が親友・美優役、「はこぶね」の木村知貴が会社社長・寺村役で共演。2025年・第21回大阪アジアン映画祭で観客賞を受賞。

A human drama directed by Teppei Isobe (known for "Nagi no Yuutsu" and "Yoru no Manimani"), depicting a woman who discovers what truly matters through her work in the substitute service business, a job built on lies. After her mother's sudden death and being cheated on by her colleague and boyfriend Shoya, Tanaka Risa hits rock bottom. Taking a temporary leave from work, she is strongly persuaded by her best friend Miyu to work as a paid audience member posing as a lecture attendee at a substitute service company. Risa, who usually can't tell lies, finds her work recognized by company president Teramura and becomes engrossed in this job of lying. Kokoro Asami, active in films, TV dramas, and numerous commercials, stars in her first leading film role, naturally portraying the protagonist Risa, who moves forward despite being lost and conflicted. Sho Mineo (from "Hozo wo Kamu") co-stars as her boyfriend Shoya, Rina Sakurai (from "Subarashiki Sekai") as her best friend Miyu, and Tomoki Kimura (from "Hakobune") as company president Teramura. Won the Audience Award at the 21st Osaka Asian Film Festival in 2025.
