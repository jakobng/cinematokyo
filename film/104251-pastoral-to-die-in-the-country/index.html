田園に死す

Pastoral: To Die in the Country

1974 · 監督: 寺山修司 · 102分 · ファンタジー / ドラマ

かいせつ 多分野で活躍した演劇界の鬼才・寺山の長編映画第二作。時空を越えた寺山の自伝的原風景を、幻想的で儚い鮮烈なイメージの連続で描く。

An autobiographical work written and directed by Shuji Terayama, adapting his own poetry collection of the same name. In a village at the foot of Mount Osore in Aomori Prefecture, a young boy, 'I', lives with his mother in an old house after losing his father. The boy's only solace is having an itako (a spirit medium) summon his father's spirit to talk to him. A beautiful woman who marries into the neighboring house and a circus troupe that comes to the village tempt the boy to run away from home. Later, 'I' grows up, moves to Tokyo, becomes middle-aged, and makes a film. Before this 'I' appears the boy he once was... Kantaro Suga, known for 'Thirteen Assassins' and others, plays the lead, and Kaoru Yachigusa plays the neighbor's wife.
