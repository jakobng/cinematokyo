撃たれた自由の声を撮れ

2024 · 70分

いつだって奪われ踏みにじられるのは小さな私たち。 でも、いつか世界を変えるのも、この小さな私たちの声だ。 米軍が撤退し、タリバンが首都カーブルを含むほぼ全土を掌握。20年にわたる民主政権が崩壊した。タリバン復権によってふたたび女性たちは外で働く場を失い、教育の機会を奪われ、少女たちは親よりも年の離れた男たちと結婚させられ、家に閉じ込められる──。 ラシュミンとナスタランの姉妹は、ほかの女性たちと共に街に出ては声を上げる。「私たちはひるまない」。銃を構えた男たちに言い放ち、この国の現状を発信すべくスカーフにスマートフォンを隠し撮影する。

A documentary recording the daily struggles of women who continue to seek freedom in Afghanistan under Taliban rule. In August 2021, the Taliban seized control of nearly all of Afghanistan, collapsing the two-decade democratic government. With the Taliban's return to power, women were once again barred from working outside the home, deprived of educational opportunities, and girls were forced into marriages with much older men and confined to their houses. Sisters Rashmin and Nastaran, along with other women, take to the streets to raise their voices against countless inequalities. Undaunted even by armed men, they hide smartphones under their scarves to film and broadcast the current situation in Afghanistan. They continue their resistance while moving from one relative's home to another, but in a patriarchal society, their father despises the women participating in demonstrations, and they are forced to live in fear of neighbors informing on them. Despite such harsh circumstances, Rashmin, driven by the desire not to make the next generation experience the same suffering, rushes out into the streets. The director is Zainab Entezar, an Afghan-born female filmmaker, producer, and writer who, while living in exile, continues to broadcast the voices of the women of her homeland to the world.
