#つよくなるとき

Time to Be Strong

2024 · 監督: ナムグン・ソン · 99分 · ドラマ

華やかなK-POPの世界で挫折した元アイドル3人の“その後”をリアルに描いたドラマ。 スミン、テヒ、サランの3人は、競争の激しいK-POPアイドル界での成功を目指し青春のすべてを捧げてきたが、思い描いていたような成功をつかむことができないまま、それぞれのグループは解散し、夢も未来も見失ってしまう。途方に暮れた3人は、学生時代に行くことができなかった卒業旅行をやり直すべく、全財産の10万円を手に済州島へ向かう。さまざまなトラブルに見舞われながらも、みかん畑でのアルバイトや旅先で出会った人々との交流を通して、3人は傷ついた自分自身と向き合い、新たな一歩を踏み出していく。 監督・脚本は「告白ヒストリー」のナムグン・ソン。現役アイドルや元アイドルへの取材を重ね、K-POPアイドル産業が抱える問題に鋭く切り込みながら、自分たちの人生を取り戻していく元アイドルたちの姿を鮮やかに映し出す。出演はテレビドラマ「アンナラスマナラ　魔法の旋律」のチェ・ソンウン、「君と僕の5分」のヒョン・ウソク、「殺人配信」のハ・ソユン。2024年・第25回全州国際映画祭の韓国コンペティションでグランプリを含む3部門を受賞。あいち国際女性映画祭2025および第38回東京国際映画祭では「強くなるとき」のタイトルで上映。

This film, which achieved a triple crown including the Grand Prize at the Jeonju International Film Festival, depicts the "aftermath" of three former idols whose dreams were shattered in the dazzling world of K-POP. The three, who have lost sight of both their dreams and their future, embark on a belated "graduation trip." Facing their wounds, their lives that had come to a standstill begin to move once more. At the helm is director Namgung Sun of "Confession History." Through meticulous research and interviews with current and former idols, the film sharply examines the issues facing the K-POP idol industry—the fierce competition behind the glamorous stage, the financial burdens, and the reality of young people whose minds and bodies are worn down. At the same time, this is not merely a story that indicts the industry's harshness. How do young people who devoted their youth to a dream yet could not become "someone" go on to live? Without sensationally consuming their setbacks and pain, at times with humor and with a warm gaze, the film vividly captures these individuals reclaiming their own lives. The cast brings together a gathering of highly acclaimed young talents: Choi Sung-eun of "Anna: The Magic Melody," Hyun Woo-suk of "Five Minutes Between You and Me," and Ha So-yun of "Murder Livestream." Stopping in their tracks, wounded, yet sharing their vulnerability with others to walk forward once more—the three's belated "graduation trip" gradually transforms into a journey to begin life anew. A moving masterpiece that gently resonates with the paths each viewer has walked. To everyone who has ever stumbled and come to a standstill in life. A story for those of us who could not become "someone," about living as ourselves.
