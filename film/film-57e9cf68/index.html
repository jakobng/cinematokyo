美晴に傘を

2025 · 123分

言葉が心を紡ぐ、家族再生の物語 北の小さな町の漁師である善次は、喧嘩別れをした息子の光雄をがんで亡くす。葬儀にも出席せず四十九日を迎えようとしていたところに、光雄の妻の透子が娘の美晴と凛を連れて訪ねてくる。善次は、彼女たちを通して亡き息子に想いを馳せる。聴覚過敏を持つ自閉症の美晴は、守られてきた世界から一歩でも外に踏み出したいと願うものの、失敗したり不安を感じると、布団を被り夢の中に逃げ込む。そこは、父の光雄が生前病床で書いた『美晴に傘を』という絵本の世界であった——。

A story of family regeneration, where words weave the heart. Zenji, a fisherman in a small northern town, loses his son Mitsuo, from whom he had parted on bad terms, to cancer. As he is about to observe the 49th day after the death without attending the funeral, Mitsuo's wife Toko visits with her daughters Miharu and Rin. Through them, Zenji reflects on his deceased son. Miharu, who has autism and is hypersensitive to sound, wishes to step outside the protected world she lives in, but when she fails or feels anxious, she hides under her futon and escapes into dreams. That place is the world of the picture book 'An Umbrella for Miharu,' which her father Mitsuo wrote while on his sickbed before he died.
