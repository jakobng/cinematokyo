"There's something wrong with this child." Saga feels an inexplicable unease about her newborn baby. Her anxiety gradually turns into fear, and eventually spirals into madness. And what is the terrifying truth that Saga arrives at? When it had its world premiere in the Competition section of the 76th Berlin International Film Festival, it left audiences and critics alike in a state of shock and uproar, with reactions calling it "an unpredictable development that defies all conventions" and "a return of Rosemary's Baby." Director Hanna Bergholm, who gained worldwide attention for her beautiful yet grotesque world-building and original storytelling in Hatching, once again uses serene nature and mysticism as a backdrop to expose the terror lurking within the happiness of an ideal family and to probe the depths of human psychology. Seidi Haarla of Compartment No. 6 delivers a hauntingly intense performance as the mother, Saga. Her husband John is played by Rupert Grint of the Harry Potter series. What exactly did the mother give birth to? A terrifying Nordic child thriller from Finland's unique talent is born. Newlyweds Saga and John, who long for a child, move to a remote, forested town in Finland, Saga's homeland, dreaming of ideal parenting. Embraced by the mysterious forest where the atmosphere of Norse mythology still lingers, the couple soon conceives a long-awaited child. A happy life with her beloved husband and child—Saga believed in that future without a doubt. Until she heard that first cry.
