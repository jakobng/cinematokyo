つぐない payment

2026 · 監督: 宮部一通 · 39分

幼い少女の失踪をめぐり、善悪では語ることのできない人間の複雑さと弱さと優しさを描いた心理サスペンス。 幼い娘・由美と暮らすシングルマザーの里美の日常は、由美が行方不明になったことで一変してしまう。由美がいなくなる前、母娘が住むマンションの管理人の男は、里美が仕事で留守にしている間に学校帰りの由美を管理人室に招き入れ、親しげに遊んでいた。そして由美の失踪後、管理人の男は里美の前で、母娘しか知るはずのない、あることを口にする。 「偶然と想像」「バッシング」などの占部房子が主演を務め、失踪した娘の帰りを待つ母親の複雑な心情を演じた。物語の鍵を握るマンションの管理人役で「恋人たち」「南極料理人」の黒田大輔が共演。国内外の広告賞を数多く受賞してきた映像ディレクターの宮部一通が監督・脚本・撮影・編集を手がけた。2025年・第26回ハンブルク日本映画祭にてグランプリ、第3回東京インディペンデント映画祭にて準グランプリを受賞。

A psychological suspense film that portrays the complexity, weakness, and kindness of human beings, which cannot be simply judged as good or evil, surrounding the disappearance of a young girl. Satomi, a single mother living with her young daughter Yumi, has her daily life turned upside down when Yumi goes missing. Before Yumi's disappearance, the manager of the apartment building where the mother and daughter lived would invite Yumi, on her way home from school, into the manager's office while Satomi was at work, playing with her in a friendly manner. After Yumi's disappearance, the manager says something to Satomi that only the mother and daughter could have known. Fusako Urabe, known for 'Wheel of Fortune and Fantasy' and 'Bashing,' stars as the mother waiting for her missing daughter's return, portraying her complex emotions. Daisuke Kuroda, known for 'Lovers' and 'Nankyoku Ryorinin,' co-stars as the apartment manager who holds the key to the story. The film was directed, scripted, shot, and edited by Kazuto Miyabe, a video director who has won numerous domestic and international advertising awards. It won the Grand Prix at the 26th Hamburg Japanese Film Festival in 2025 and the Semi-Grand Prix at the 3rd Tokyo Independent Film Festival.
