南極料理人 ４Kリマスター

2009 · 監督: 沖田修一 · 125分

監督・脚本を務めたのは、本作が商業映画デビュー作となった沖⽥修⼀。『横道世之介』『キツツキと⾬』『さかなのこ』など、唯⼀無⼆の温かな視点で多くの観客を魅了してきた沖⽥監督の原点ともいえる⼀本です。今年9⽉には、⾃⾝が完全オリジナル脚本を⼿がける最新作『さとこはいつも』の公開も控えており、時代を超えて愛される沖⽥作品の魅⼒に、改めて注⽬が集まります。さらに、作品を彩る料理の数々を⼿掛けたのは、『かもめ⾷堂』などで知られるフードスタイリストの飯島奈美と榑⾕孝⼦。映画⾳楽はユニコーンのABEDON が担当し、主題歌にはユニコーンの「サラウンド」が起⽤され、南極という極限の地で繰り広げられる、温かくユーモラスな物語を包み込みます。 南極観測隊の料理⼈・⻄村を演じるのは、当時すでに確かな演技⼒で⾼い評価を受けながら、その後、国⺠的俳優としてさらなる⾶躍を遂げることになる堺雅⼈。飄々とした佇まいの中に繊細な感情をにじませる唯⼀無⼆の表現⼒で、料理を通して仲間たちの⼼をつなぐ料理⼈・⻄村の温かな⼈柄を⾒事に体現しています。彼が胃袋と⼼を満たす観測隊員たちには、⽣瀬勝久、きたろう、⾼良健吾、古舘寛治、⿊⽥⼤輔、⼩浜正寛、豊原功補ら、個性豊かな実⼒派キャストが集結。ヒトクセもフタクセもある、愛すべき"最強のチーム"が、本作ならではの味わい深い⼈間ドラマを⽣み出しました。 南極観測隊の料理人として、ドームふじ基地へやってきた西村淳。極限の環境で暮らす隊員たちにとって、食事は何よりの楽しみ。手間ひまかけた料理で、みんなの顔がほころぶ瞬間は一番の喜びだ。しかし、日本には妻と8歳の娘、生まれたばかりの息子が待っている。これから約1年半、14,000km彼方の家族を思う日々がはじまる──。

The director and screenwriter was Shuichi Okita, for whom this work marked his commercial film debut. This is a film that can be called the starting point of director Okita, who has captivated many audiences with his singularly warm perspective in films such as "Yokomichi Yonosuke," "The Woodsman and the Rain," and "The Fish Tale." This September, his latest work "Satoko Is Always" with a completely original screenplay by himself is also scheduled for release, and attention is once again gathering on the appeal of Okita's works, loved across eras. Furthermore, the many dishes that color the film were handled by food stylists Nami Iijima and Takako Kureya, known for works such as "Kamome Diner." The film music was handled by ABEDON of Unicorn, and Unicorn's "Surround" was used as the theme song, enveloping the warm and humorous story unfolding in the extreme land of Antarctica. Playing Nishimura, the cook of the Antarctic observation team, is Masato Sakai, who was already highly praised at the time for his solid acting ability and would later go on to further success as a nationally beloved actor. With his one-of-a-kind expressive power that lets delicate emotions seep through his nonchalant demeanor, he splendidly embodies the warm personality of Nishimura, the cook who connects his comrades' hearts through food. The observation team members whose stomachs and hearts he fills are played by a gathering of uniquely talented actors including Katsuhisa Namase, Kitaro, Kengo Takara, Kanji Furutachi, Daisuke Kuroda, Masahiro Kohama, and Kosuke Toyohara. A lovable "strongest team" full of quirks and double quirks creates the richly flavorful human drama unique to this film. Nishimura Jun has come to Dome Fuji Station as the cook for the Antarctic observation team. For the team members living in an extreme environment, food is the greatest pleasure. The moment everyone's faces break into smiles thanks to dishes made with time and effort is his greatest joy. However, in Japan his wife, his 8-year-old daughter, and his newborn son are waiting. For about a year and a half from now, days of thinking of his family 14,000 km away begin──.
