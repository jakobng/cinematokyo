明けまして、おめでたい人

2022 · 監督: ウトユウマ · 83分

NHK連続テレビ小説「らんまん」などに出演する俳優の山脇辰哉が自身の体験をもとに書き起こした2021年上演の同名舞台を映画化。 俳優の山脇辰哉は最近“彼女みたいな人”ができ、仕事も恋も順調だ。年末、元恋人との縁を切るため帰省するという彼女を送り出した山脇は、地元の仲間たちと毎年恒例のテレビゲームをしながらの年越しにいそしむ。その後、彼は家族と一緒に祖母のもとを訪れ、いつも通りの年明けを過ごす。 山脇が原案・企画・脚本・主演を務め、ヒロイン役で「海の夜明けから真昼まで」の羽音、妹役で「17歳は止まらない」の片田陽依が共演。新進気鋭の若手監督を輩出する音楽と映画の祭典「MOOSIC LAB」の新作競作部門として制作された作品で、劇団ミルズズ主宰のウトユウマが長編映画初メガホンをとり、俳優としても活動するミュージシャンの山岸健太が音楽を担当。

A film adaptation of the 2021 stage play of the same name, written by actor Tatsuya Yamawaki — who appears in the NHK morning drama series "Ranman" and other works — based on his own experiences. Actor Tatsuya Yamawaki has recently found someone "like her," and both his work and love life are going well. At year's end, he sees off his girlfriend, who is returning home to cut ties with her ex, and then devotes himself to the annual New Year's Eve tradition of playing video games with his hometown friends. Afterward, he visits his grandmother together with his family and spends New Year's just as he always does. Yamawaki serves as original concept creator, planner, screenwriter, and lead actor, with Hane — known for "From the Night's Dawn to Broad Daylight" — co-starring as the heroine and Yori Katada — known for "17 Is Not Stopping" — as his younger sister. Produced as part of the new-works competition section of MOOSIC LAB, a music and film festival that nurtures promising young directors, the film marks the feature directorial debut of Yuma Uto, head of the theater troupe Millsz, with music by musician and actor Kenta Yamagishi.
