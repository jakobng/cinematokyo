ロンガの海

Distant Shores

2026 · 監督: Haruko Konishi · 83分 · ドキュメンタリー

In 1942, Japan occupied the Dutch East Indies, present-day Indonesia. The local people were forced into harsh labor and subjugation, and many lives were lost to starvation and disease. When Japan was defeated, 1,000 of the Japanese soldiers who had occupied Indonesia deserted the military. They threw themselves into the Indonesian War of Independence, and more than half lost their lives; those who survived chose to live quietly in this land. In July 2023, young Japanese-Indonesian people establish the 'Remaining Japanese Soldiers Historical Museum' on the outskirts of Jakarta. Mario Kuroiwa, who is involved in its activities, has a grandfather, Toru Kuroiwa, who during the Japanese occupation had been in Aceh on Sumatra, exposing anti-Japanese spies as a special police captain. After Japan's defeat, when the Allied forces that refused to recognize Indonesian independence began their invasion again, Kuroiwa personally led a unit and took command in the war of independence. He married a woman living in Aceh and was blessed with children, but in 1955, only six years after Indonesia achieved independence, he returned to Japan, leaving his family behind. Mario's father Hendri, carrying the emotional wounds of being abandoned by his father, still lives in Aceh today. Along with tracing the family memories buried in the gaps of history, Mario sets out on a journey to confront his own roots.
