Turbulence

Turbulence - Fear takes flight

2025 · 監督: クラウディオ・ファエ · 91分 · スリラー / アクション / アドベンチャー / ドラマ / ファンタジー

A situational survival thriller set in the inescapable space of a floating hot air balloon, depicting the threats of nature and human conflict. To repair his relationship with his wife Amy, who is grieving a miscarriage, ambitious CEO Zach joins a hot air balloon tour crossing the Italian Dolomites with her. A mysterious woman named Julia joins them, and the flight with the three passengers begins. But high in the sky, Julia suddenly reveals that she had a secret meeting with Zach the previous day, accusing him of infidelity. Zach denies it, but suspicion swirls, and Julia suddenly pulls a knife. During the struggle, the pilot falls out, and the balloon goes out of control. With the radio dead and the balloon reaching an oxygen-thin altitude of 16,000 feet, battered by turbulence and storms, the three are forced into extreme choices with no escape. Directed by Claudio Fae of 'Air Lock: Underwater Emergency Shelter,' with a screenplay by Andy Mason, who wrote and produced 'Air Lock: Underwater Emergency Shelter' and '47 Meters Down.' The cast includes Hera Hilmar of 'Mortal Engines' as Amy, Jeremy Irvine of 'War Horse' as Zach, and Olga Kurylenko, known as a Bond girl from 'Quantum of Solace,' as Julia.
