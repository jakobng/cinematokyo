わんぱく王子の大蛇退治

The Little Prince and the Eight-Headed Dragon

1963 · 監督: 芹川有吾 · 86分 · アニメーション / ファミリー / アドベンチャー / ファンタジー

美術の小山礼司が平面性を重視したキャラクター造形と背景美術を提案し、日本神話の幻想的な世界が創造された。新東宝の助監督だった芹川有吾の初演出作で、森康二が作画監督を務めた。作画監督は絵柄の統一を図る重要な役職であり、本作以降、日本のアニメーションに定着していく。大塚康生と月岡貞夫が作画に半年を費やした大蛇退治の見せ場は、傑出したカメラワークとアクションで知られる。

The sixth feature-length anime from Toei Animation, this adventure fantasy humorously explores Japanese mythology. In the age of the gods long ago, Prince Susanoo, born to Izanagi and Izanami, enjoys happy days on Onogoro Island. One day, his mother Izanami dies. Unable to accept her death, Susanoo sets out with a rabbit named Akahana on a journey to Yomi-no-Kuni, the land of the dead where his mother is said to be. After adventures in the Night Land ruled by his brother Tsukuyomi and Takamagahara ruled by his sister Amaterasu, Susanoo arrives in Izumo Province. There, to save the princess Kushinada, he decides to fight the Yamata-no-Orochi. This was the directorial debut of Yugo Serikawa, later known for the 'Cyborg 009' series, with original animation direction by Koji Mori. The Orochi battle scene, which took Yasuo Otsuka and Sadao Tsukioka six months to complete, is celebrated as one of the greatest moments in Japanese animation history. The music was composed by Akira Ifukube, known for 'Godzilla'.
