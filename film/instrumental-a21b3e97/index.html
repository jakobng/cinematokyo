2020 · 監督: 宮坂一輝 · 64分

ある新米新聞記者の青春と喪失を、現在と過去を行き来しながら描いたドラマ。東京大学大学院で理論物理学を専攻しながら映画制作をおこなう宮坂一輝の初長編監督作品。新米新聞記者の以有真知子は、コインランドリーで男子中学生の岡孝汰と知り合う。自然と心を通わせていく中で、真知子は孝汰が幼いころの親友・佳子と同じ雰囲気を持っていると感じ始める。真知子役を「サイキッカーZ」の秋田ようこ、中学生の孝汰役を本作が映画初出演となる黒澤凜士、幼少期の真知子役を「アイネクライネナハトムジーク」の八木優希、佳子役をアイドルグループ「さくら学院」の元メンバーで「麻希のいる世界」で主演を務めた新谷ゆづみがそれぞれ演じる。

This film intricately weaves between present and past, depicting the youth and loss of a novice newspaper reporter. It is based on a script written by director Miyasaka during his high school years, which he began producing after entering university by gathering members of his film circle. Yoko Akita, who has been expanding her roles in recent years with works such as 'Psychicker Z' (2022, directed by Akiyoshi Kimura) and 'Unforgettable Memories' (scheduled for 2022, directed by Shin Sonoda), plays the protagonist Michiko Iu. Ryoji Kurosawa, making his film debut, plays the junior high school boy Kota Oka whom Michiko meets. Yuki Yagi, who has attracted attention since her child actor days and recently appeared in the morning drama 'Hiyokko' and 'A Little Night Music' (2019, directed by Rikiya Imaizumi), plays Michiko's childhood self. Yuzumi Shintani, a former member of the idol group Sakura Gakuin who starred in 'The World of Maki' (2022, directed by Akihiko Shiota) and recently appeared in 'The End of the Pale Hour' (2022, directed by Ryutaro Nakagawa), plays Michiko's childhood friend Yoshiko Atsuo. Kazuki Miyasaka, who was 19 years old at the time of production and served as director, screenwriter, cinematographer, and editor, made his feature-length debut with this film. He continues to make films while currently studying theoretical physics in a doctoral program at the University of Tokyo.
