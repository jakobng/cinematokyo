Story: To reclaim the lost house of cherished memories, Van reluctantly follows the plan devised by her sister Kieu and best friend Thien, and approaches two wealthy suitors who are pursuing her. One is a man with calm, mature charm and warmth. The other is young, quirky, and a playboy. But when Van realizes her true feelings, she discovers that both of them had "their own reasons" for approaching her... Whose lips will Van choose for the "million-dollar kiss"?
