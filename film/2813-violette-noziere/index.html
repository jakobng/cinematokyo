Violette Nozière

1978 · 監督: クロード・シャブロル · 124分 · 犯罪 / ドラマ / スリラー

Claude Chabrol, a master representing French cinema, directs this suspense drama based on the true story of Violette Nozière, a girl who poisoned her parents. Violette Nozière, sentenced to death in 1933 for poisoning her biological parents but pardoned and released, became a muse for the Surrealists. The film portrays the precarious and delicate psychology of an 18-year-old girl suffocating at home, engaging in dissolute behavior outside, and ultimately resorting to violence. A young Isabelle Huppert delivers a commanding performance as the titular Violette, winning the Best Actress award at the 31st Cannes Film Festival in 1978. Stéphane Audran of "La Femme cerf" and "Le Charme discret de la bourgeoisie" co-stars as Violette's mother. In August 2026, the digitally remastered version will be shown in Japan for the first time as part of the special screening series "Claude Chabrol Masterpieces vol. 2."
