A horror film directed by Yoshizo Sakai of "Fictional" and "Counselor," planned and produced by Tokio Omori, a TV Tokyo producer who brought forth "The Missing Exhibit" and "Searching for Kikue Ishinaga." Rio Yamashita, who starred in the 2025 long-running hit "Yukiko a.k.a." primarily in mini-theaters over about a year, plays the lead role of a woman tormented by paranoid fear through caring for her mother. After her father's death, Kana returns to her childhood home and begins caring for her mother, who has dementia. She devotes herself to caregiving as if trying to reclaim time with her mother, but her mother's inexplicable behavior gradually becomes increasingly bizarre, and Kana begins to feel uneasy about strange occurrences around her. Eventually, possessed by the sensation that something has entered her mother's inner being, Kana is driven mad by the presence of an unearthly "something." In addition to Rio Yamashita as the protagonist Kana, An Ogawa, who has starred in successive films including "There Is a Stone" and "Song of the Beyond," plays Kana's younger sister Anri, who rushes to the family home after hearing Kana's pleas, and Sports Makita plays psychiatrist Kumagai, who becomes acquainted with Kana and Anri's family through a certain incident.
