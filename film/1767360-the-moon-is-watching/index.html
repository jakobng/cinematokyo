月がみている

The Moon Is Watching

2026 · 監督: 渡邉直子 · 101分 · ドラマ

「失楽園」「愛の流刑地」など数々のベストセラーで知られる小説家・渡辺淳一の長編小説「シャトウルージュ」を、渡辺淳一の娘で映画プロデューサーの渡邉直子が長編初監督を務めて映画化。妻から性的な拒絶を受け続ける夫がフランスの城で彼女に“性のレッスン”を受けさせようとするという設定が話題を集めた原作を、女性監督ならではの視点を加えて再構築。主人公・月子の視点を丁寧に織り込むことで、夫婦それぞれの感情を丁寧につむぎ出す。 エリート医師の原田克彦は、大企業オーナーの娘である月子と入籍する。しかしお嬢様育ちの月子は克彦の性的な求愛を受け止めきれず、克彦は不満を募らせていた。克彦は一計を案じ、妻の留学先だったフランスでの休暇を提案する。しかし狙いはバカンスによって親密になることではなく、性的なレッスンを施すとされる謎の城に彼女を幽閉することだった。 優秀な医師でありながら妻との関係に満たされない思いを抱える夫・克彦を「『桐島です』」「ケンとカズ」の毎熊克哉、夫に翻弄されながらも自身の心と身体を見つめなおしていく妻・月子を「人狼ゲーム　プリズン・ブレイク」の小島梨里杏が演じた。撮影の約7割をフランスで敢行し、濃密で幻想的な映像世界を構築。原作のタイトルにもある「城」という象徴的なモチーフも、本物の古城を使用して可視化した。

Junichi Watanabe's novel Château Rouge, known alongside bestsellers such as Lost Paradise and The Garden of Love's Exile, is adapted into film by his daughter, producer Naoko Watanabe, in her feature directorial debut. The original work, whose premise—a husband who continues to face sexual rejection from his wife attempts to have her undergo "sex lessons" at a French château—drew attention, is reconstructed with a perspective unique to a female director. By carefully weaving in the viewpoint of the protagonist Tsukiko, the emotions of both husband and wife are delicately spun out. Elite doctor Katsuhiko Harada registers his marriage to Tsukiko, the daughter of a major company owner. But Tsukiko, raised as a pampered young lady, cannot accept Katsuhiko's sexual advances, and his dissatisfaction grows. Katsuhiko devises a plan and proposes a vacation in France, where his wife once studied abroad. His aim, however, is not intimacy through a holiday but to confine her in a mysterious château said to administer sexual lessons. Katsuhiko, an excellent doctor yet unfulfilled in his relationship with his wife, is played by Katsuyuki Maku of The Kirishima Thing and Ken and Kazu, while Tsukiko, tossed about by her husband yet reexamining her own heart and body, is played by Riri Kojima of Werewolf Game: Prison Break. About 70 percent of the shooting was done in France, building a dense, fantastical visual world. The symbolic motif of the "château" from the original title is also made visible using a real old castle.
