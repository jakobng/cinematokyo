This tour is the fan-awaited solo concert held approximately 6 years and 4 months after the previous "EXO PLANET #5 - EXpℓOration -." It toured 14 regions worldwide including Japan, recording sold-out seats everywhere. As the concert name always carries the series name "EXO PLANET," EXO is a group named after "exoplanet," meaning a planet outside the solar system, with the meaning of "new stars who came from an unknown world." Each member possesses a unique superpower, unfolding a fantastical and grand worldview and story. Since debuting in Korea and China in 2012, they have released numerous hit songs such as "Growl," "Love Shot," "Monster," and "The Eve," captivating fans around the world. In January of this year, they made a comeback with their eighth full album "REVERXE," topping album and music charts in Korea and abroad and achieving five music show wins, proving their enduring popularity. The encore performance and final day of the world tour, the Seoul concert, will be broadcast live to movie theaters across the country with Japanese subtitles and attendee gifts! EXO has captivated fans worldwide on countless stages. Their overwhelming performances and the stage woven from their grand worldview will be delivered with full immersive power on the big screen. Please enjoy the special stage of the final day of the encore performance at the movie theater.
