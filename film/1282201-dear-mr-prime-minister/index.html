Dear Mr. Prime Minister

1964 · 監督: 野村芳太郎 · 89分 · コメディ

The final chapter of the "Dear Sir" trilogy, a collaboration between master director Yoshitaro Nomura and Kiyoshi Atsumi. Kakumaru, a kind-hearted but unsuccessful comedian, resolves to go to Tokyo after his master's death, but he only ends up doing menial chores. When he reunites with a childhood friend, a drama unfolds.
