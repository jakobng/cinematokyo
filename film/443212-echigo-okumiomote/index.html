越後奥三面　山に生かされた日々

Echigo Okumiomote

1984 · 監督: 姫田忠義 · 147分 · ドキュメンタリー

日本各地の生活や民族を記録する作品を数多く手がけた記録映画作家・映像民俗学者の姫田忠義率いる民族文化映像研究所が、新潟県の最奥で自然に寄り添う暮らしを続けてきた山村・奥三面（おくみおもて）の最後の姿をとらえたドキュメンタリー。 新潟県北部の朝日連峰に位置する奥三面では、山の恵みを隅々まで利用する生活が昭和の終わりまで奇跡のように保たれてきた。冬の深い雪に覆われた山では、ウサギなどの小動物や熊を狩る。春には山菜採りや、慶長2年の記録が残る古い田での田植え。夏の川では仕掛けやヤスでサケ・マス・イワナを捕らえ、秋になると木の実やキノコ採り、仕掛けや鉄砲による熊狩りが行われる。 ダム建設による閉村を前に、映画スタッフは1軒の家と畑を借り、1980年から4年間にわたって村の暮らしを撮影。村人たちが連綿と続けてきた山の生活を、四季を通じて丹念に映し出す。2024年4月、デジタルリマスター版でリバイバル公開。

A documentary by the National Museum of Japanese History's folk-culture film unit—led by documentary filmmaker and visual folklorist Tadayoshi Hikita, who produced numerous works recording the lives and ethnic customs of various regions of Japan—capturing the final days of Oku-Miomote, a mountain village in the farthest reaches of Niigata Prefecture that sustained a life close to nature. In Oku-Miomote, located in the Asahi mountain range of northern Niigata Prefecture, a way of life that used every last blessing of the mountains was miraculously preserved until the end of the Showa era. In the mountains blanketed by deep winter snow, they hunted small animals such as rabbits and bears. In spring came the gathering of wild vegetables and rice planting in ancient paddies with records dating back to the second year of Keicho. In summer rivers they caught salmon, trout, and char with traps and spears, and in autumn they gathered nuts and mushrooms and hunted bears with traps and guns. Before the village's closure due to dam construction, the film staff rented a house and fields and filmed the village's life over four years beginning in 1980, carefully capturing through the four seasons the mountain life the villagers had continued unbroken for generations. In April 2024, a digitally remastered version was re-released.
