アギーレ／神の怒り

Aguirre, the Wrath of God

1972 · 監督: ヴェルナ-ヘルツォ-ク · 94分 · 履歴 / アドベンチャー / ドラマ

A masterpiece adventure film by Werner Herzog, the brilliant talent of New German Cinema, depicting the harrowing fate of a 16th-century Spanish expedition that set out for the golden land deep in the Amazon. In 1560, a Spanish expedition was about to cross the last pass of the Andes on its way to the legendary golden land of El Dorado. A detachment is sent to scout the surrounding area, and several dozen men including commander Ursua, his second-in-command Aguirre, and Aguirre's daughter set out. But harsh nature, tropical fever, and attacks by indigenous people claim the lives of the party one after another, and the detachment collapses from within. Aguirre, having seized control of the group, pushes on through the jungle driven by madness—but then...
