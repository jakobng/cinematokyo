恒星の向こう側

2025 · 監督: 中川龍太郎 · 92分

国内外から注目を集める中川龍太郎監督が「走れ、絶望に追いつかれない速さで」「四月の永い夢」に続いて喪失と再生をテーマに撮りあげた3部作の最終章で、余命わずかな母のもとに帰郷した女性が、母と衝突しながらも彼女を理解していく姿を描いた人間ドラマ。 母の余命を知り故郷に戻った未知は、母・可那子に寄り添おうとするものの拒絶され衝突を繰り返す。未知は夫・登志蔵との子を身ごもっているが、亡き親友への思いに揺れる夫の姿に不安を募らせる。やがて母の遺したテープから“もうひとつの愛”を知った未知は、初めて母を理解し、母から託された愛を胸に前へと進んでいく。 「あの娘は知らない」の福地桃子が主人公・未知、「あん」「朝が来る」などの監督として知られる河瀨直美が母・可那子を演じ、寛一郎、朝倉あき、南沙良、三浦貴大、久保史緒里、中尾幸世が共演。劇場アニメ「ルックバック」のharuka nakamuraが音楽を手がけた。2025年・第38回東京国際映画祭コンペティション部門に出品され、福地桃子と河瀨直美がそろって最優秀女優賞を受賞した。

The final chapter of a trilogy on the theme of loss and rebirth by director Ryutaro Nakagawa, who has attracted attention in Japan and abroad, following "Running at a Speed That Won't Let Despair Catch Up" and "The Long Dream of April." It is a human drama depicting a woman who returns home to her mother, who has little time left to live, and comes to understand her mother despite their conflicts. Michi, who learns of her mother's prognosis and returns to her hometown, tries to stay close to her mother Kanako but is rejected and repeatedly clashes with her. Michi is pregnant with her husband Toshizo's child, but grows anxious at the sight of her husband wavering over thoughts of a deceased close friend. When Michi learns of "another love" from a tape her mother left behind, she understands her mother for the first time and moves forward with the love entrusted to her by her mother. Momoko Fukuchi of "That Girl Doesn't Know" plays the protagonist Michi, and Naomi Kawase, known as director of "An" and "True Mothers," plays her mother Kanako, with Kanichiro, Aki Asakura, Sara Minami, Takahiro Miura, Shiori Kubo, and Koyo Nakao co-starring. haruka nakamura, who scored the animated film "Look Back," handled the music. It was entered in the Competition section of the 38th Tokyo International Film Festival in 2025, where Momoko Fukuchi and Naomi Kawase both won the Best Actress Award.
