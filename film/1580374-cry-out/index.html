Cry Out

2026 · 監督: 瀬々敬久 · 130分 · ドラマ

複雑な過去を抱えた教師・真唯子は過酷な現実に追い詰められていく教え子・章子を救おうとするが…。湊かなえの集大成と評された小説の映画化。

A mystery drama based on the acclaimed novel by best-selling author Kanae Minato, considered her magnum opus, directed by Takahisa Zeze ('The Last 10 Years', 'Those Who Were Not Protected'). Shinomiya Mayuko fulfills her grandmother's expectations and becomes a teacher despite her complicated family background. One day, her student Saeki Akiko receives a letter from someone claiming to be 'Me, 20 years from now'. Half-doubtful, Akiko writes back, enduring the grief of losing her father and the lonely days with her withdrawn mother. But violence from her mother's boyfriend, bullying, and an unbelievable truth push her to the edge. In deep despair, Akiko and her only friend Arisa hatch a forbidden plan: 'kill the parents'. Mayuko tries to save Akiko, reaching out even as she herself is crushed by society's injustices. Yuna Kuroshima plays Mayuko, the teacher who tries to support children despite her own complicated past. Nanami Yamazaki plays Akiko, the girl struggling to survive harsh reality. Tori Matsuzaka and Kitagawa Keiko play Akiko's parents Ryota and Fumino. Ryuta Bando plays Mayuko's lover Haruki Yuki. Kanata Hosoda and Hana Kondo play Hirota Higuchi and Shinju Morimoto, who deeply influence the lives of Mayuko and Akiko.
