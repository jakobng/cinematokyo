気候変動で荒廃した近未来に生きる少女イリスと“空から降ってきた”虹色の少年アルコの冒険。フランスの新鋭が鮮やかな色彩で詩情豊かに描いたアニメ。

The adventure of Iris, a girl living in a near future devastated by climate change, and Arco, a rainbow-colored boy who 'fell from the sky.' A poetically rich animation by a rising French director, depicted in vivid colors.
