CHURA VITA チュラ・ヴィータ

Chura Vita

2026 · 監督: 岸本司 · 105分

沖縄を拠点に作品を撮り続けてきた「HAPPY SANDWICH」の岸本司監督が、世界有数の長寿地域「ブルーゾーン」として知られる沖縄・やんばるを舞台に、スウェーデンから訪れた1人の女性の目を通して「よりよく生きるとは何か」を見つめるヒューマンドラマ。沖縄北部・やんばるの豊かな自然や、伝統の木造帆船サバニ、琉球染織に息づく「生きる知恵」を織り込んで描く。 長寿地域「ブルーゾーン」沖縄の秘密に惹かれたニコリーナは、特別な生き方を探し求めてスウェーデンから島へ渡る。だが理想郷のイメージの奥で、古くから受け継がれてきた食文化や祈り、繊細な手仕事が近代化の波の中で少しずつ姿を消しつつあった。守られてきた営みが薄れていく姿を前に、彼女は自分をこの地へ導いた問いを見失っていく。そんな折、海岸に打ち上げられた1頭のイルカと出会い、声なき命を海へ返そうとする時間の中で、「生きること」の重みと自然への畏敬を静かに教えられていく。 主人公ニコリーナを演じるのは、実際に沖縄を訪れたスウェーデン人のニコリーナ・スベンセン。 城間やよい、真栄城興和、満名匠吾らが共演する。

A Swedish woman, drawn to Okinawa—one of the world's renowned longevity regions known as a "Blue Zone"—searches for the meaning of a fulfilling life. There, she finds herself confronting a vanishing spiritual landscape. Through encounters with a stranded dolphin and the disappearing culture of the islands, she reflects deeply on humanity's fragile relationship with nature, memory, and survival. Fascinated by the mystery of Okinawa, known as one of the world's foremost longevity regions, Nicolina Svensen travels from Sweden in search of the secret to a special way of living. But what she witnesses on the island is a world quietly transforming beneath the image of paradise. As modernization reshapes the land, the food culture, prayers, and delicate crafts passed down since ancient times are gradually disappearing from everyday life. Faced with the quiet fading of a way of life preserved across generations, Nico begins to lose sight of the very question that led her to this place—"What does it truly mean to live richly?" Amid this confusion, she encounters a dolphin washed up on the shore. The time she spends accompanying that voiceless life and trying to return it to the sea quietly teaches her the weight of "living" and a deep reverence for nature. In that moment, what awakens within Nico is not knowledge gained from books, but the raw, pulsing sensation of "life" itself. The light, wind, and sea enveloping the island slowly seep into her inner being. As if held by something greater, Nico begins to realize that the answer she sought does not lie in legends of the distant past, but still breathes in the warmth of people and in the act of living alongside nature. This is the story of one woman's prayer and rebirth—cherishing the beauty that is gradually being lost from the world while continuing to seek a path to live sincerely within it.
