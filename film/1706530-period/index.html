2026 · 監督: 宮野ケイジ · 91分 · アクション / ドラマ

「ハイキック・ガール！」「KG カラテガール」など数々のアクション映画で活躍してきた武田梨奈が、暗殺者役で主演を務めたクライムアクション。 天涯孤独のレイは、小さな海辺の町で時計の修理屋として働きながら、ひっそりと暮らしている。しかし彼女の正体は凄腕の暗殺者で、バーの店主から殺しの依頼を受けては確実に標的を仕留めてきた。一方、幼少期をレイと施設で共に過ごした男も同じ町に住んでおり、彼女のライバルとして暗殺業をこなしていた。レイは町にやってきたある男と宿命の出会いを果たし、彼に惹かれていく。 主人公レイ役の武田がCG・ワイヤーなしでバトルアクションとガンアクションを熱演し、レイと宿命の出会いを果たす男をダンス＆ボーカルグループ「FANTASTICS」の瀬口黎弥、レイと同じ裏社会に生きる孤独な暗殺者を萩原聖人が演じる。共演に嶋田久作、有森也実、特別出演に布施博。監督・脚本は「ニート・ニート・ニート」「殺る女」の宮野ケイジ。

This is a hard-boiled opera—a rarity in Japan—brought to you by Rina Takeda, known as one of Japan's foremost action stars and accomplished actresses, FANTASTICS performer Reiya Seguchi, and Masato Hagiwara. Takeda plays a woman who has lived the fate of an assassin. Here Takeda unleashes her full potential, performing battle action and gun action without CG or wires. Co-starring is Seguchi, a performer in the dance-and-vocal group FANTASTICS, who takes on the role of a man tossed about by the threads of fate with Rei, played by Takeda. In addition, Hagiwara plays a lonely assassin who lives in the same underworld as Rei, and these characters fuse with the beautiful sea and nostalgic townscape of Kozushima (in the Izu Islands) to create a noir action film. The supporting cast is also luxurious, with Kyusaku Shimada, Narimi Arimori, and a special appearance by Hiroshi Fuse in key roles. Music is handled by Jaw Maran, a singer-songwriter who produces in New York and Los Angeles and excels at R&B spanning Asia including Japan, Korea, China, and Burma. What greatly enhances the film's noir action is Keiji Tsujii, who has swept through Japan's action world, delivering fight scenes too fast for the eye to follow. Director Keiji Miyano brings all these attractions together into a hard-boiled opera that fuses a story confronting one's outlook on life with action and magnificent locations. Rei (Takeda), a watch repairer, is all alone in the world, quietly living in a small seaside town. But her real profession is assassin. Taking assassination requests from Ito (Shimada), the owner of a certain bar, she is a highly skilled assassin who finishes off her targets. In the same town, Kaji (Hagiwara), a fellow assassin who spent time with her in a facility as a child, also plies the trade as a formidable rival. Rei shadows and takes down members of a group engaged in evil deeds, but one day she finds herself drawn to a man named Shunsuke (Seguchi) who visits the town. It is a fated encounter—and the arrival of a destiny in which one of them must leave this world.
