KOTOKO

Kotoko

2011 · 監督: 塚本晋也 · 91分 · ドラマ / ホラー

Kotoko (Cocco), gripped by an obsession that she must raise her infant son single-handedly and love him completely, begins to suffer from psychological instability. In the end, her bizarre behavior leads to suspicion of child abuse, and her son is entrusted to her sister in Okinawa. One day, a novelist named Tanaka (Tsukamoto Shinya) appears before her. He says he was drawn to her singing voice and appearance when they happened to share a bus to Okinawa, but Kotoko fiercely avoids him. Yet she eventually accepts his persistent devotion and resolves to live together as three, with her son.
