夢見通りの人々

Dream Street

1989 · 監督: 森﨑東 · 95分 · コメディ

かいせつ 宮本輝の小説を松竹喜劇の名匠・森﨑が監督し映画化。大阪の下町にある夢見通り商店街を行き交う人々の悲喜交々を描いた人情喜劇。映画初主演・小倉の素朴な魅力も味わい深く、大阪ならではの多彩なキャストも楽しい一本。

A film adaptation of Miyamoto Teru's novel, directed by Morisaki, the master of Shochiku comedy. A heartwarming comedy depicting the joys and sorrows of the people who come and go along Yumemidori Shopping Street in downtown Osaka. Ogura's humble charm in her first starring film role is deeply moving, and the diverse cast unique to Osaka makes this a delightful watch.
