『顔 -かお-』

Yeong-gyu is a blind seal engraver who carves the most beautiful stamps in South Korea. To his son Dong-hwan, his father was 'a miracle itself.' But one day, the skeletal remains of his mother Yeong-hui, who disappeared 40 years ago, are discovered—and it holds the potential of being a 'murder.' To uncover the truth behind the death of the mother whose face he never knew, Dong-hwan traces the testimonies of those who knew her. Yet, all he hears are strange words: 'She had a face as ugly as a monster.' Why was his mother so disparaged? Is it the truth, or a distorted memory? What eventually emerges is not the 'face' of one woman, but the prejudice, malice, and collective madness lurking within humanity. The moment the sealed truth is revealed, a powerful shock that will go down in film history strikes the audience!
