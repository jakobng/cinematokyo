新感染 ファイナル・エクスプレス

Train to Busan

2016 · 監督: ヨン・サンホ · 118分 · アクション / ホラー / スリラー

A Korean survival panic action film depicting the terror and chaos caused by the sudden outbreak of a mysterious virus spreading aboard a high-speed train connecting Seoul and Busan. Seok-woo, a fund manager working in Seoul, is separated from his wife and lives with his young daughter Su-an. Su-an insists on going alone to meet her mother in Busan for her birthday, and Seok-woo reluctantly agrees to escort her. They board the KTX high-speed train from Seoul to Busan, but just before departure, a suspicious commotion occurs near Seoul Station. A woman infected with the mysterious virus also boards KTX train 101, the same train as the father and daughter. Alongside the protagonist Seok-woo and his daughter, various passengers—a pregnant woman and her husband, high school baseball players, and a selfish middle-aged businessman—face a life-or-death struggle for survival in a state of extremity where being caught by the infected means certain death, and their individual human dramas unfold. This is the first live-action feature film by Yeon Sang-ho, a rising director who has garnered attention in the Korean animation scene, and the story preceding this film is revealed in the animated feature 'Seoul Station'.
