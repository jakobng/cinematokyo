2005 · 監督: Anders Thomas Jensen · 95分 · ドラマ / コメディ / 犯罪

. 試練と不条理の果てに予期せぬ“奇跡”が舞い降りる 予測不能なダークヒューマンドラマ 仮釈放されたアダムは更生施設を兼ねた田舎の教会へ送り込まれるが、ネオナチ思想に染まる彼は、指導役の聖職者イヴァンから目標を問われ「庭のリンゴを収穫してアップルケーキを作る」と適当な返事をする。イヴァンの自己欺瞞を執拗に暴こうとするアダムだったが、そんな彼に奇怪な災いが次々と降りかかる...。 . マッツが演じるのはこんな役！ 穏やかながら度を越えた楽観主義者、短パン姿の型破りな牧師イヴァン ポジティブ思考の良識人のようだが、徐々にサイコパス味が増してきて…。 . . .

A human drama written and directed by Anders Thomas Jensen, the acclaimed screenwriter of *Open Hearts* and *Brothers*, starring Mads Mikkelsen (*The Hunt*). Drawing motifs from the Old Testament’s "Book of Job" and the fable of Adam’s apples, the film uses dark humor to depict the eccentric characters inhabiting a rural church. Adam, a neo-Nazi skinhead on parole, is sent to a countryside church that doubles as a rehabilitation center. When his mentor, the priest Ivan, asks him to set a goal, Adam dismissively replies that he will bake an apple cake using fruit from the garden. The residents of the church are all peculiar, including Ivan himself, who uses blind faith to shield himself from a harsh reality. As Adam relentlessly attempts to strip away Ivan’s self-deception, a series of bizarre calamities begins to plague him. Ulrich Thomsen (*In a Better World*) stars as Adam, with Mikkelsen portraying Ivan.
