渚の恋人たち

Nagisa no koibito tachi

2016 · 監督: 大九明子 · 58分 · 音楽

貨物船埼玉丸は3か月の遠洋航海を終え、荷を降ろすため帰国の途にあった。海員たちは家族や恋人に電報でそのことを伝える。しかし彼らはわずか2日後にはまた別の地に向かわねばならない。各々が過ごす限られた時間の悲喜こもごものドラマ。木村は半年にわたり海員たちと生活を共にして取材、海で働く男たちの日常を誇張することなく描きつつ、帰国を待つ女性たちの心情も入念に描き込んでいる。海員のなかに大映で俳優デビュー前の伊丹十三の姿を確認できる。

The cargo ship Saitama Maru, having completed a three-month ocean voyage, was on its way home to unload its cargo. The sailors send telegrams to their families and lovers to let them know. But just two days later, they must set off again for another destination. This is a drama of the joys and sorrows each man experiences in his limited time ashore. Kimura spent six months living alongside sailors to research the film, depicting the daily lives of men who work at sea without exaggeration while also carefully portraying the feelings of the women waiting for their return. Among the sailors, one can spot Juzo Itami before his acting debut at Daiei.
