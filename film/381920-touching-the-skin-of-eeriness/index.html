不気味なものの肌に触れる

Touching the Skin of Eeriness

2013 · 監督: 濱口竜介 · 55分 · ドラマ / 謎

「THE DEPTH」「親密さ」といった作品で注目を集め続ける映画作家・濱口竜介監督が、構想途中の長編映画「FLOODS」の前日譚として撮りあげた中編ドラマ。父を亡くした少年・千尋は、腹違いの兄である斗吾とその恋人・美里のもとで暮らすことに。斗吾と美里は千尋を温かく迎え入れるが、孤独感に苛まれる千尋は同じ年の直也とのダンスの練習にのめり込んでいく。そんな彼らの周囲で、不穏な出来事が起こりはじめ……。キャストには「ヒミズ」の染谷将太、「さまよう獣」の渋川清彦、「カナリア」の石田法嗣ら実力派俳優がそろう。

A medium-length drama shot by director Hamaguchi Ryusuke, who continues to attract attention with works like 'THE DEPTH' and 'Intimacy,' as a prequel to his planned feature film 'FLOODS.' Chihiro, a boy who has lost his father, comes to live with his half-brother Togo and Togo's lover Misato. Togo and Misato warmly welcome Chihiro, but plagued by loneliness, Chihiro throws himself into dance practice with his same-age friend Naoya. Around them, unsettling events begin to occur... The cast includes talented actors such as Shota Sometani from 'Himizu,' Kiyohiko Shibukawa from 'The Wandering Beast,' and Hotsushi Ishida from 'Canary.'
