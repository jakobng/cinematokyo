早乙女カナコの場合は

As for Me

2025 · 監督: 矢崎仁司 · 119分 · ドラマ / ロマンス

Kanako Saotome begins living with a friend when she enters university. At the entrance ceremony, she meets Nagatsuda, an aspiring screenwriter in the theater circle "Charing Cross," and they start dating. She finishes job hunting and lands a position at the major publishing company she had dreamed of. She and Nagatsuda have been together for four years, but lately they argue constantly. Nagatsuda only talks big, never finishes writing a script, and shows no intention of graduating. There are even suspicions that he is cheating with Maiko, a first-year student from a women's university who joined the circle. Just then, Kanako receives a confession from Yoshizawa, a senior colleague at her future employer. As Kanako pursues her dream of becoming an editor, she and Nagatsuda gradually drift apart in their ways of life.
