En la ciudad de Sylvia

In the City of Sylvia

2007 · 監督: José Luis Guerín · 82分 · ドラマ / ロマンス

This film follows a young aspiring painter wandering through places of memory, searching for traces of Sylvia, a woman he loved six years ago. It constructs an obsessive love story reminiscent of Alfred Hitchcock's 'Vertigo' (1958) with meticulously calculated sound and imagery, resulting in a groundbreaking work unlike anything seen before. The film was shot entirely on location in Strasbourg, France, an ancient city where medieval streetscapes and cutting-edge railways coexist without dissonance, creating a stateless atmosphere. The masterful sound design, which orchestrates the city's noises—the murmur of people, the passing of trams—like a symphony, and the beautiful, luminous chain of images captured through glass reflections and tram windows, transport the viewer into an unknown, otherworldly space.
