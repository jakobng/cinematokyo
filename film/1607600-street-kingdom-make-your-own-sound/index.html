ストリート・キングダム 自分の音を鳴らせ。

Street Kingdom: Make your own sound.

2026 · 監督: 田口トモロヲ · 130分

1978年、パンクに影響を受けた若者たちは「東京ロッカーズ」と呼ばれ、1年で日本のロックに革命を起こす。その伝説的なムーヴメントを描いた青春映画。

A youth music film reuniting director Tomorowo Taguchi and screenwriter Kankuro Kudo of *Iden & Tity*. Based on the autobiographical essay *Street Kingdom* by photographer Yuichi Jibiki—who served as the group's photographer and manager—the film depicts the "Tokyo Rockers," a movement of young people who forged Japan's first homegrown punk rock scene. In 1978, Yuichi, a young photographer who moved to Tokyo driven by the sounds of the Sex Pistols on the radio, visits a live house after discovering a small rock zine called *Rockin' Doll*. There, he finds a space overflowing with raw energy where music, bands, and audiences are free from all restraint. Blown away by a performance from the band TOKAGE, led by vocalist Momo, Yuichi captures the moment in a frenzy of shutter clicks. Officially hired as their photographer, Yuichi grows closer to the band. Their music soon ignites a fire among the youth, evolving into the "Tokyo Rockers" movement that would redefine the landscape of Japanese rock. Kazunobu Mineta stars as Yuichi alongside Ryuya Wakaba as Momo. The ensemble cast includes Riho Yoshioka, Taiga Nakano, Shotaro Mamiya, Nao Omori, Shido Nakamura, and Sena Nakajima. Yoshihide Otomo (*Iden & Tity*) provides the film's music.
