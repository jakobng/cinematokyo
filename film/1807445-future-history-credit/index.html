みらいしのほこう

Future History Credit

2026 · 監督: 羽蚋拓未 · 66分 · サイエンスフィクション / ドラマ

廃校間際の高校を舞台に、歴史教師と予知能力をもつ生徒が人生の一発逆転を目指して奮闘する姿を描いたSF青春ドラマ。 人生に迷走している歴史教師・野田柚季は、車を運転中に隕石に直撃されそうになったところを、教え子の村木蒼によって救われる。柚季は廃校が決まった高校で、単位の足りない3年生2人の補習講義を担当しており、そのうちの1人が蒼だった。蒼は未来を予知できる超能力者で、彼の父親も時間を止める能力をもっている。教師の仕事にうんざりしていた柚季は、蒼の予知能力を使って人生を逆転させようと考える。一方、孤独を抱える蒼は、どこかズレているが真っ直ぐに自分と向き合ってくれる柚季に恋心を抱きはじめる。しかし柚季の未来には、ある重大な秘密が隠されていた。 監督・脚本は、東京藝術大学大学院映像研究科の修了作品「Song for Laurel」で注目され、本作が商業映画デビューとなる羽蚋拓未。主人公の高校教師・柚季を「笑顔の向こうに」の安田聖愛、予知能力をもつ生徒・蒼を「STEP OUT にーにーのニライカナイ」の盧礼欧が演じた。

A sci-fi youth drama set in a high school on the verge of closure, depicting a history teacher and a student with precognitive abilities as they struggle to turn their lives around. Yuki Noda, a history teacher adrift in life, is saved by her student Ao Muraki when her car is nearly hit by a meteorite while she is driving. Yuki is teaching supplementary lessons to two third-year students who lack credits at the school slated for closure, and one of them is Ao. Ao is a psychic who can predict the future, and his father also has the ability to stop time. Yuki, fed up with her teaching job, decides to use Ao's precognitive abilities to turn her life around. Meanwhile, Ao, who carries loneliness, begins to develop romantic feelings for Yuki, who is somewhat eccentric but faces him honestly. However, a grave secret is hidden in Yuki's future. The director and screenwriter is Takumi Habu, who drew attention with 'Song for Laurel,' a graduation work at Tokyo University of the Arts Graduate School of Film and New Media, and this film marks his commercial directorial debut. The protagonist, high school teacher Yuki, is played by Seia Yasuda of 'Beyond the Smile,' and Ao, the student with precognitive abilities, is played by Reo Ro of 'STEP OUT: Nini's Nirai Kanai.'
