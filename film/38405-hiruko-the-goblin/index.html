ヒルコ　妖怪ハンター

Hiruko the Goblin

1991 · 監督: 塚本晋也 · 89分 · ホラー / ファンタジー / コメディ

塚本晋也監督がデビュー作「鉄男」に続いて手がけた監督第2作で、諸星大二郎の漫画「妖怪ハンター」を沢田研二主演で実写映画化。新進気鋭の考古学者・稗田礼二郎は学界で注目を集めていたが、ある学説を唱えたことで異端視され、その存在はほとんど忘れ去られていた。そんな彼のもとに、義兄で中学校教師の八部高史から「古代人が悪霊を鎮めるために造った古墳を発見した」という内容の手紙が届く。自分の学説が証明されると考えた稗田は八部の家を訪ねるが、八部は生徒の月島令子とともに謎の失踪を遂げていた。2人を捜すため学校へ向かった稗田と八部の息子まさおの前に、恐ろしい妖怪ヒルコが出現。妖怪退治の武器を手に立ち向かう稗田たちだったが……。1991年の初公開から30年目を迎えた2021年7月、レストア＆リマスター版が公開。

Director Shinya Tsukamoto's second directorial work following his debut film "Tetsuo," a live-action adaptation of Daijiro Morohoshi's manga "Yokai Hunter" starring Kenji Sawada. Up-and-coming archaeologist Reijiro Hieda was attracting attention in academic circles, but after advocating a certain theory he was branded a heretic, and his existence was all but forgotten. Then a letter arrives from his brother-in-law, middle school teacher Takashi Yabe, stating that he has discovered an ancient tomb built by ancient people to pacify evil spirits. Believing his theory will be proven, Hieda visits Yabe's home, but Yabe has mysteriously disappeared along with his student Reiko Tsukishima. As Hieda and Yabe's son Masao head to the school to search for the two, the terrifying yokai Hiruko appears before them. Hieda and the others take up weapons for slaying yokai and face it, but... In July 2021, marking the 30th year since its original release in 1991, a restored and remastered version was released.
