おくりもの

2003 · 80分

This is the feature debut of 2003 by Michelangelo Frammartino, a solitary and distinctive filmmaker in Italian cinema. Starring the director's grandfather, Angelo Frammartino, it quietly depicts the final days of an old man's life with unique humor, set in a small Calabrian village on the verge of abandonment due to depopulation. An old man lives in a depopulating rural village in southern Italy. One day, young men who help him bury his old dog leave behind a cell phone and a scrap of paper—a nude photo taken for personal purposes. Later, the old man notices a woman in the village; she is the person in the photo. Using no professional actors, filming with local residents, the film creates a quiet visual poem exploring universal themes of "tradition and modernity," "life and death," and "community and solitude." It will have its Japanese theatrical premiere in 2026 in a 4K restored version as part of the special screening series "The Amazing World of Michelangelo Frammartino."
