あのこは貴族

Aristocrats

2021 · 監督: 岨手由貴子 · 124分 · ドラマ

山内マリコの同名小説を原作に、同じ都会に暮らしながら全く異なる生き方をする2人の女性が自分の人生を切り開こうとする姿を描いた人間ドラマ。都会に生まれ、箱入り娘として育てられた20代後半の華子。「結婚＝幸せ」と信じて疑わない彼女は、結婚を考えていた恋人に振られ、初めて人生の岐路に立たされる。あらゆる手段でお相手探しに奔走し、ハンサムで家柄も良い弁護士・幸一郎との結婚が決まるが……。一方、富山から上京し東京で働く美紀は、恋人もおらず仕事にやりがいもなく、都会にしがみつく意味を見いだせずにいた。そんな2人の人生が交錯したことで、それぞれに思いも寄らない世界がひらけていく。「愛の渦」の門脇麦が箱入り娘の華子、「ノルウェイの森」の水原希子が自力で都会を生き抜く美紀を演じる。監督は「グッド・ストライプス」の岨手由貴子。

Based on Mariko Yamauchi's novel of the same name, this human drama depicts two women living in the same city but leading completely different lives as they try to carve out their own paths. Hanako, in her late twenties, was born in the city and raised as a sheltered daughter. Believing without doubt that 'marriage equals happiness,' she is dumped by the boyfriend she was thinking of marrying and stands at a crossroads in life for the first time. She rushes about searching for a partner by every means, and her marriage to Koichiro, a handsome lawyer from a good family, is decided—but then... Meanwhile, Miki, who came to Tokyo from Toyama and works there, has no boyfriend, finds no fulfillment in her job, and cannot find meaning in clinging to the city. When the lives of these two women intersect, unexpected worlds open up for each of them. Mitsuki Kadowaki of 'Love's Whirlpool' plays the sheltered Hanako, and Kiko Mizuhara of 'Norwegian Wood' plays Miki, who survives the city on her own. The director is Yukiko Sode of 'Good Stripes.'
