仁義なき戦い 広島死闘篇

Battles Without Honor and Humanity: Deadly Fight in Hiroshima

1973 · 監督: 深作欣二 · 99分 · ドラマ / 犯罪 / アクション

1950 (Showa 25), Hiroshima City. Masaharu Yamanaka, a demobilized soldier who was beaten by the Otomo gang's Katsutoshi Otomo for dining and dashing, is tenderly nursed back to health by Yasuko Uehara, the widow and niece of Muraoka gang boss Tsuneo Muraoka. Through this connection, Yamanaka becomes a member of the Muraoka gang, but a chance encounter leads to a sexual relationship with Yasuko, which incurs the wrath of Boss Muraoka, and Yamanaka flees to Kyushu on the orders of wakagashira Matsunaga. However, the shooting death of Wada gang boss in Kyushu makes Yamanaka's name resound throughout the underworld, and he is permitted to return to Hiroshima, with his relationship with Yasuko also approved by Boss Muraoka.
