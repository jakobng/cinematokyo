身土不二（しんどふじ）

2026 · 監督: 安孫子亘 · 86分

福島県・熱塩加納町の有機農業と、地産地消率100％を実現した学校給食にスポットを当てたドキュメンタリー。 福島県喜多方市の旧・熱塩加納村は、かつて「有機の里」と呼ばれていた。昭和55年から有機農業に取り組み、農薬と化学肥料を極力減らして作った特別栽培米「ひめさゆり米」は村特産の主力となった。有機栽培は野菜作りにも広がり、その米と野菜が学校給食にも使われ「ほぼ100％の地産地消給食」として全国から注目を集めた。しかし少子高齢化の波はこの町にも及び、小中学校の統廃合や地元農家の高齢化が進むなか、学校給食の存続も危ぶまれている。 映画では、「熱塩加納方式」と呼ばれる給食の実態と、野菜生産者農家「まごころ野菜の会」の人々の野菜作りへの想い、そしてその給食を元気に食べる子どもたちの姿を、3年半にわたり記録。有機農業で給食を届け続ける地元農家の人々と子どもたちとの触れ合いを通して、いまの農業が抱える少子高齢化や地球環境の問題、そして有機農業の原点を浮かび上がらせていく。監督は「決断　運命を変えた3.11母子避難」などで知られ、2011年の東日本大震災以降は福島に拠点を移してドキュメンタリー映画を製作している安孫子亘。

A documentary focusing on organic farming in Atsushio Kano Town, Fukushima Prefecture, and its school lunches that achieved a 100% local consumption rate. The former Atsushio Kano Village in Kitakata City, Fukushima, was once called the 'Village of Organic Farming.' Starting organic farming in 1980, their specially cultivated rice 'Himesayuri Rice,' grown with minimal pesticides and chemical fertilizers, became the village's main specialty. Organic cultivation expanded to vegetables, and these rice and vegetables were used in school lunches, gaining national attention as 'nearly 100% locally sourced lunches.' However, the wave of declining birthrates and aging population has reached the town, threatening the continuation of school lunches amid the consolidation of schools and the aging of local farmers. The film documents over three and a half years the reality of the 'Atsushio Kano Method' lunches, the passion of the 'Magokoro Vegetable Association' farmers for growing vegetables, and the children happily eating those lunches. Through the interactions between local farmers who continue to provide lunches through organic farming and the children, it highlights the issues of declining birthrates, aging, global environmental problems facing agriculture today, and the origins of organic farming. Directed by Abiko Wataru, known for 'Decision: The 3.11 Mother-Child Evacuation That Changed Destiny,' who moved his base to Fukushima after the 2011 Great East Japan Earthquake to produce documentary films.
