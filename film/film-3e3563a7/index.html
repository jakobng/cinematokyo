風を継ぐもの　はじまり

2026 · 監督: 河村友宏 · 90分

激動の幕末に生きた新選組の若者たちの青春を新解釈で描くオリジナルテレビアニメ「風を継ぐもの」を、2027年1月の放送に先駆け、劇場先行版としてスクリーン上映。劇場先行版では、記憶喪失の主人公・立川迅助と壬生浪士組の仲間たちとの出会いや、局長筆頭・芹沢鴨とのエピソードを上映する。 文久3年6月。京都・壬生浪士組屯所に、記憶をなくした隊士の青年が運び込まれる。青年は副長助勤・沖田総司から、自分が「立川迅助」という人物であることを教えられ、隊士としての日々を過ごすようになる。新選組は幕末の混迷のなかで次第に存在感を増し、迅助たちは激動の波に飲み込まれていく。やがて迅助がすべての記憶を取り戻したとき、世界は大きな変貌を遂げる。 脚本は、演劇集団キャラメルボックスの創設者のひとりで、同じく新選組を題材にした舞台「風を継ぐ者」をはじめとする「立川迅助シリーズ」でも知られる劇作家・成井豊。「GAMBA ガンバと仲間たち」の河村友宏が、Live2Dとセルアニメーションを融合させた新しい映像表現に挑み、ロックバンド「くるり」の岸田繁が初めてテレビアニメの劇伴を担当。アイドルグループ「Snow Man」の佐久間大介が主人公・迅助の声を務め、声優としてテレビアニメ初主演を果たした。

A theatrical advance screening of the original TV anime Those Who Inherit the Wind, which depicts the youth of young Shinsengumi members who lived through the turbulent end of the Edo period with a new interpretation, ahead of its January 2027 broadcast. The theatrical advance version shows the encounters between the amnesiac protagonist Shunsuke Tachikawa and his Mibu Roshigumi comrades, as well as episodes with the head of the corps, Kamo Serizawa. June of the 3rd year of Bunkyu. A young corps member who has lost his memory is brought to the Mibu Roshigumi barracks in Kyoto. The young man is told by vice-captain assistant Soji Okita that he is someone named 'Shunsuke Tachikawa,' and begins living the days of a corps member. The Shinsengumi gradually increases its presence amid the turmoil of the end of the Edo period, and Shunsuke and the others are swallowed by the waves of upheaval. Eventually, when Shunsuke regains all his memories, the world undergoes a great transformation. The script is by Yutaka Nari, one of the founders of the theater company Caramelbox and known for the 'Shunsuke Tachikawa Series' including the stage play Those Who Inherit the Wind, which also deals with the Shinsengumi. Tomohiro Kawamura of GAMBA: Adventures of Gamba takes on a new visual expression fusing Live2D and cel animation, and Shigeru Kishida of the rock band Quruli handles the background music for a TV anime for the first time. Daisuke Sakuma of the idol group Snow Man voices the protagonist Shunsuke, marking his first lead role in a TV anime as a voice actor.
