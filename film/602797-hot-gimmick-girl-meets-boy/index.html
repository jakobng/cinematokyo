ホットギミック　ガールミーツボーイ

Hot Gimmick: Girl Meets Boy

2019 · 監督: 山戸結希 · 119分 · ドラマ / ロマンス

「乃木坂46」の堀未央奈が映画初主演を務め、「溺れるナイフ」の山戸結希監督が相原実貴原作の同名コミックを実写映画化。平凡な女子高生・成田初は、兄、妹、両親に囲まれたごく普通の家庭で暮らしていた。同じ社宅に住む幼なじみで昔から初の憧れの存在だった小田切梓。口は悪いが傷ついた初を励ましてくれる橘亮輝。初の兄で、ある秘密を持つ凌。初を取り巻く3人の男性との間で、彼女の心が揺れ動いていく。主人公の初役を堀が演じるほか、亮輝役を清水尋也、梓役を板垣瑞生、凌役を間宮祥太朗がそれぞれ演じる。

Nogizaka46's Miona Hori stars in her first film lead role, and director Yuki Yamato of Drowning Love helms this live-action adaptation of the same-name manga by Miki Aihara. Hatsu Narita, an ordinary high school girl, lives in a perfectly normal household with her older brother, younger sister, and parents. There is Azusa Odagiri, a childhood friend who lives in the same housing complex and has long been the object of Hatsu's admiration. There is Rikiki Tachibana, foul-mouthed but always there to encourage Hatsu when she is hurt. And there is Ryō, Hatsu's older brother, who holds a certain secret. Hatsu's heart wavers among these three men around her. Hori plays the protagonist Hatsu, with Jinya Shimizu as Rikiki, Mizuki Itagaki as Azusa, and Shotaro Mamiya as Ryō.
