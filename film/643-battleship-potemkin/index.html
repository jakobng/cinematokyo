戦艦ポチョムキン

Battleship Potemkin

1925 · 監督: セルゲイ・M・エイゼンシュテイン · 75分 · ドラマ / 履歴 / 戦争

Sailors reach the peak of their discontent with the imperial regime and rise up in rebellion. This soon escalates into a massive massacre of the populace by the military. The 'Odessa Steps' scene, where a baby carriage tumbles down the stairs, is too famous to ignore in this masterpiece by the giant of cinema, Sergei M. Eisenstein.
