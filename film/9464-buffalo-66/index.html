バッファロー’66

Buffalo '66

1998 · 監督: ヴィンセント・ギャロ · 110分 · ドラマ / ロマンス / コメディ

Billy is released from prison after serving a five-year sentence. Having been away from home for work and having lied to his parents that he was married, he kidnaps a passing woman named Layla on his way back to his family home... <A love story that shot Vincent Gallo to stardom. It depicts the taciturn love between an anarchic man who knows nothing of love and a woman drawn to him. The gray-toned visuals and unique slow pacing make for an unparalleled artistic style. Co-starring Christina Ricci.>
