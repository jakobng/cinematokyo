パンダコパンダ 雨ふりサーカスの巻

Panda! Go Panda!: Rainy Day Circus

1973 · 監督: 高畑勲 · 39分 · アニメーション / コメディ / ファミリー

Mimiko-chan, Pan-chan, and Papanda lived happily together every day. Then, one day, while Mimiko-chan and the others were out, two suspicious characters showed up... When Mimiko-chan returned, she was delighted, saying, "It's my first time seeing burglars!" but the pair ran away in a flash. Mimiko-chan was a little disappointed that the seemingly interesting guests had gone, but Pan-chan noticed that his food had been eaten. Pan-chan sniffed around the house searching. Crawling into Pan-chan's bed and sleeping soundly was... a tiger cub! Both of them were so surprised they ran around the house making a huge commotion...
