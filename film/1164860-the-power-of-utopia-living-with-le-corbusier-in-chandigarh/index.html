Kraft der Utopie – Leben mit Le Corbusier in Chandigarh

The Power of Utopia: Living with Le Corbusier in Chandigarh

2023 · 監督: Thomas Karrer · 84分 · ドキュメンタリー

A documentary about Chandigarh, the planned city built in India by Le Corbusier, the master of modernist architecture who left behind numerous legendary buildings. In 1947, India and Pakistan gained independence, and Punjab was divided between the two countries. With the state capital Lahore belonging to Pakistan, India needed to build a new capital. Prime Minister Nehru wanted a city symbolizing faith in the future and democracy, entrusting Le Corbusier with designing the planned city of Chandigarh. Born in the wilderness at the foot of the Himalayas, the "Shining City" of Chandigarh aimed to be a human-centered utopia where natural order and modern technology were in perfect harmony, symbolizing a new India. How has this city transformed 70 years after its birth? The film traces the buildings and their history, while residents—architects, urban activists, and artists—discuss the challenges Chandigarh faces and its enduring charm. Screened as part of the special program "Le Corbusier and Doshi: Modernism in India" (from May 1, 2026, at Eurospace in Tokyo and other theaters nationwide).
