Missed You, Left You, Lost You, Miss You

2026 · 監督: 濱田真和 · 114分 · ドラマ

親友の死を知った脚本家とその元恋人が、亡き親友が会いたがっていた母親を探す旅に出る姿を描いたロードムービー。演出家・脚本家・俳優の濱田真和が長編初メガホンをとり、半自伝的なオリジナル脚本をもとに撮りあげた。 川久保恒は東京で脚本家として生計を立てていたが、不条理な現実に耐えられず仕事を辞めてしまう。自堕落な生活を送っていた年末のある日、元恋人・日浦瑞穂からの連絡で、疎遠になっていた親友・祐輔が急死したことを知る。川久保と瑞穂は、祐輔が暮らしていた「名前を亡くした町」を訪れ遺体と対面するが、年末年始で火葬できず、1月4日まで祐輔の遺体と過ごすことになる。そんな中、祐輔を慕っていた不登校の中学生・広瀬歩と出会った2人は、祐輔が自分を捨てた母親に会いたがっていたことを知り、祐輔の遺体と広瀬を連れて、祐輔の母親探しの旅に出る。 主人公・川久保を野村周平、ヒロイン・瑞穂をセントチヒロ・チッチが演じ、2人と一緒に旅をすることになる少年・広瀬役で小川冬晴、川久保の亡き親友・祐輔役で髙橋雄祐、広瀬の母・多恵子役で菜葉菜、祐輔の母・薫役で渡辺真起子が共演。セントチヒロ・チッチが主題歌も担当。

A road movie depicting a screenwriter who learns of his best friend's death and his ex-girlfriend as they set out to find the mother the late friend had wanted to see. Masakazu Hamada—director, screenwriter, and actor—makes his feature directorial debut, shooting from a semi-autobiographical original screenplay. Ko Kawakubo made a living as a screenwriter in Tokyo but quit his job, unable to endure the absurdity of reality. One day at year's end, while living a dissolute life, he learns through a call from his ex-girlfriend Mizuho Hiura that his estranged best friend Yusuke has died suddenly. Kawakubo and Mizuho visit the 'town that lost its name' where Yusuke lived and see his body, but because it is the New Year holiday the cremation cannot take place, and they end up spending time with Yusuke's body until January 4. Meanwhile, the two meet Aya Hirose, a school-refusing middle schooler who looked up to Yusuke, and learn that Yusuke had wanted to see the mother who abandoned him; they set off with Yusuke's body and Hirose on a journey to find Yusuke's mother. Shuhei Nomura plays protagonist Kawakubo, Cent Chihiro Chittiii plays heroine Mizuho, Fuyuharu Ogawa plays Hirose, the boy who travels with them, Yusuke Takahashi plays Kawakubo's late best friend Yusuke, Nanana plays Hirose's mother Taeko, and Makiko Watanabe plays Yusuke's mother Kaoru. Cent Chihiro Chittiii also performs the theme song.
