鯛と雨とゆきおんな

2026 · 監督: 長谷川汐海 · 71分

ショート映画制作支援プログラム「TRIPLEX（トリプレックス）」にて制作された3本の短編を収録したオムニバス映画。 遺産整理中の古い日本家屋を舞台に、鯛のお頭が夜中に突然奇妙な物語を語り出し“世界の終わり”を告げる怪奇譚「鯛のあら」（長谷川汐海監督）、触れたものを急速に乾燥させてしまう異常な雨に見舞われた人々の恐怖を描くパニック映画「白雨」（鈴木龍監督）、雪女の民話をモチーフに、現代に生きる夫婦の言葉にならない心の距離を描いた「凍えるほど近く」（深田隆之監督）の3作品で構成。 多数の応募作品の中から選ばれた企画をもとに、短編映画「repeat in the room」で注目された長谷川汐海監督、短編映画「化身」が世界各地の映画祭で上映された鈴木龍監督、「ある惑星の散文」「ナナメのろうか」で知られる深田隆之監督がそれぞれメガホンをとった。出演は「ブラックホールに願いを！」の星能豊、「椰子の高さ」の田中爽一郎、「早乙女カナコの場合は」の根矢涼香、「カオルの葬式」の蔵本康文、「息を殺して」の谷口蘭、「LONESOME VACATION」の藤江琢磨。

An omnibus film compiling three short films produced through the short film production support program "TRIPLEX." It comprises "Tai no Ara" (directed by Shiomi Hasegawa), a bizarre tale set in an old Japanese house being cleared of an inheritance, in which a sea bream's head suddenly begins telling a strange story at night and announces "the end of the world"; "Shiroame" (directed by Ryu Suzuki), a panic film depicting the terror of people beset by abnormal rain that rapidly dries out anything it touches; and "Kogoeru Hodo Chikaku" (directed by Takayuki Fukada), which uses the folk tale of the snow woman as a motif to depict the unspoken emotional distance between a husband and wife living in the present day. Based on projects selected from numerous submissions, the films are directed by Shiomi Hasegawa, noted for the short "repeat in the room"; Ryu Suzuki, whose short "Keshin" was screened at film festivals around the world; and Takayuki Fukada, known for "A Prose of a Certain Planet" and "Naname no Rouka." The cast includes Yutaka Hoshi of "Wish Upon a Black Hole," Soichiro Tanaka of "The Height of the Palms," Ryoka Neya of "In the Case of Kanako Saotome," Yasufumi Kuramoto of "Kaoru's Funeral," Ran Taniguchi of "Holding My Breath," and Takuma Fujie of "LONESOME VACATION."
