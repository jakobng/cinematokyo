婦系図

Her Hidden Past

1962 · 監督: 三隅研次 · 99分 · ドラマ / ロマンス

かいせつ 書生・主税と芸者・お蔦の身分違いの悲恋を簡潔かつ情感豊かに描いた名作。雷蔵と名匠・三隅の名コンビによる鏡花原作の五度目の映画化。

A masterpiece that concisely and emotionally depicts the tragic love between a student named Chikara and a geisha named Otsuta, separated by class. The fifth film adaptation of an Izumi Kyoka original, featuring the famed duo of Raizo and master director Misumi.
