Miss Oyu

1951 · 監督: 溝口健二 · 94分 · ドラマ / ロマンス

かいせつ 妹の縁談相手の男は、見合いに同席した姉に惹かれ…。数々の傑作を生み出し黄金期となる大映での溝口の第一作。名キャメラマン・宮川とも初めて組み、美しい姉妹と一人の男の揺れ動く三角関係を繊細で夢幻的な映像美で描く。

A commentary: The man who is a match for the younger sister becomes drawn to the older sister who sits in on the arranged meeting... This is Mizoguchi's first film at Daiei, which produced numerous masterpieces during its golden age. Collaborating for the first time with the renowned cinematographer Miyagawa, it delicately depicts the shifting love triangle between two beautiful sisters and one man with a dreamlike visual beauty.
