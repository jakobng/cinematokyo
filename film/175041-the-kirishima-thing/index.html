桐島、部活やめるってよ

The Kirishima Thing

2012 · 監督: 吉田大八 · 103分 · ドラマ

Ryoya Maeda belongs to the film club at a prefectural high school in a rural town. Though he is plain and inconspicuous in class, his passion for film is stronger than anyone else's. The students at his school go about their daily lives—some diligently participating in club activities on Friday afternoons as usual, while others with time on their hands play basketball as members of the go-home club. One day, Kirishima, the captain of the most popular volleyball team in school, quits the club. This triggers a wave of unease that begins to spread through the relationships among the various clubs and classmates.
