Get Undressed

2026 · 監督: 千葉誠治 · 71分 · ホラー

「AVN エイリアンVSニンジャ」「忍者狩り」の千葉誠治監督が、悪霊に追い詰められ、生き延びるためには服を脱がなければならないという状況に追い込まれた若者たちの恐怖を描くエロティックホラー。 過去にあるトラウマを抱えている紬紀は、高校時代の陸上部メンバーたちとキャンプへ向かう。彼女たちの間には高校時代の確執があり、そのムードは険悪だった。彼女たちが訪れたキャンプ場は数百年前に磔刑場だった場所で、当時全裸にされて処刑された村人が夜中に悪霊となり、訪れた人間を襲う呪われた土地だった。夜中になり、紬紀たちは村人の悪霊に襲われるが、服を脱げば悪霊に認識されないということに気づき……。 南野百合、筒井結愛、志武明日香、米沢朋花、Kaitoら若手俳優陣が顔をそろえ、「カラダ探し」「血を吸う粘土」などで知られる特殊メイクアーティストの梅沢壮一が特殊メイクと造型を担当。

An erotic horror film directed by Seiji Chiba of "AVN Alien vs. Ninja" and "Ninja Hunt," depicting the terror of young people driven into a situation where they must strip off their clothes to survive while being pursued by evil spirits. Tsumugi, who carries a certain trauma from her past, heads to a campsite with her high school track-and-field teammates. There is lingering friction among them from their high school days, and the mood is tense. The campsite they visit was a crucifixion ground several hundred years ago, a cursed land where villagers who were executed naked at the time become evil spirits at night and attack visitors. At midnight, Tsumugi and her friends are attacked by the villagers' evil spirits, but they realize that if they take off their clothes, the evil spirits cannot recognize them... Young actors including Yuri Minamino, Yua Tsutsui, Asuka Shibu, Tomoka Yonezawa, and Kaito appear, with Soichi Umezawa, known for "Karada Sagashi" and "Blood-Sucking Clay," handling special makeup and modeling.
