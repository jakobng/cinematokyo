男はつらいよ 寅次郎忘れな草

Tora-san's Forget Me Not

1973 · 監督: 山田洋次 · 99分 · コメディ

The 11th installment in the "Otoko wa Tsurai yo" series. Torajiro, who has traveled to Abashiri in Hokkaido in early summer, meets Lily Matsuoka (Kiyoko Matsuoka), a third-rate traveling singer. Lily is a manly woman who curses at everything, and Torajiro is initially at a loss with her. But once he realizes it is all a bluff, Torajiro feels sympathy for her because his own circumstances closely resemble the environment in which Lily grew up. As Lily listens to Torajiro's story of his wounded heart, she comes to look up to him like an older brother. However, Torajiro's sympathy eventually turns into an overwhelming romantic love.
