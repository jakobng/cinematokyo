RYUJI 竜二

2026 · 監督: 水田伸生 · 91分

金子正次が脚本・主演を務めた1983年公開の大ヒット映画「竜二」を、柳楽優弥が主演を務めてリメイク。「舞妓Haaaan!!!」「謝罪の王様」などの水田伸生監督がメガホンをとり、裏社会に生きながらも愛する家族を守ろうともがく男の姿を、現代的なリアリティとともに描き出す。 昭和59年から平成2年にかけての新宿・歌舞伎町。腕っぷしが強く情に厚いヤクザの竜二は、舎弟の直とひろしを家族のように愛していた。やがてまり子と出会い息子が生まれた彼は、家族を守るために裏社会から足を洗うことを決意し、酒屋の配達員として懸命に働きはじめる。生活は決して楽ではないが、自らが望んだ穏やかな暮らしを手に入れたはずだった。しかし、薬物に溺れた兄弟分のカズや、竜二にとってもうひとつの家族である舎弟たちとの再会が、彼を過去へと引き戻していく。 竜二が愛する女性・まり子を川栄李奈、舎弟の直を萩原利久、もう1人の舎弟・ひろしをダンス＆ボーカルグループ「BE:FIRST」のJUNON、兄弟分のカズを三宅健、竜二が所属する組の幹部・新田を伊藤英明、竜二に因縁を抱える敵対組織の宮内をダンス＆ボーカルグループ「MAZZEL」のRYUKIが演じる。「BE:FIRST」が主題歌を担当し、JUNONが作詞・作曲にも参加した。

A remake of the 1983 smash hit "Ryuji," written by and starring Masatsugu Kaneko, with Yuya Yagira in the lead. Directed by Nobuo Mizuta of "Maiko Haaaan!!!" and "The Apology King," the film depicts a man who lives in the underworld yet struggles to protect the family he loves, with a contemporary realism. Shinjuku's Kabukicho, from 1984 to 1990. Ryuji, a yakuza with strong fists and a warm heart, loves his underlings Nao and Hiroshi like family. Eventually he meets Mariko and has a son, and he resolves to wash his hands of the underworld to protect his family, beginning to work hard as a liquor store deliveryman. Life is by no means easy, but he should have obtained the peaceful existence he wanted. However, reunions with his sworn brother Kazu, who has sunk into drugs, and with his underlings—another family to Ryuji—pull him back into the past. Mariko, the woman Ryuji loves, is played by Rina Kawaei; underling Nao by Toshihisa Hagiwara; the other underling Hiroshi by JUNON of the dance and vocal group BE:FIRST; sworn brother Kazu by Ken Miyake; Nitta, an executive of the gang Ryuji belongs to, by Hideaki Ito; and Miyauchi, of a rival organization bearing a grudge against Ryuji, by RYUKI of the dance and vocal group MAZZEL. BE:FIRST performs the theme song, with JUNON also participating in writing the lyrics and music.
