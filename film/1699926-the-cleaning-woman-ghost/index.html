清掃する女 亡霊

The Cleaning Woman Ghost

2023 · 監督: 七里圭 · 74分

白髪の清掃婦の前に若かりし姿の母の亡霊が現れる…。2019年上演の実験的舞台に、CGパートを加えて映像化した前代未聞の最新作！

A visual work that reimagines "The Woman Who Cleans," a stage piece first performed in 2019 by director Kei Shichiri, known for "Waiting for the Pianist" and "Sleeping Princess." "The Woman Who Cleans," which was Shichiri's first stage work, gained attention as an experimental performance that interwove live bodies with gauze-screen projections (projections onto translucent screens), modeled after the traditional performing art of mugen-noh (dream noh). The work later evolved into a video performance that was among the first to incorporate AI-based motion capture (skeletal extraction) technology, and now a CG segment with non-human choreography has been added to create a new video piece. Before a white-haired woman working as a cleaning lady, the ghost of her mother appears in her youthful form. The mother laments that her daughter lives alone without bearing children, but the daughter strongly refutes her. The cast features accomplished performers in the performing arts. Tomoko Ando, from the legendary troupe "Tenkei Gekijō" and currently leading the theater company "ARICA," plays the white-haired cleaning woman, while dancer and choreographer Ikuyo Kuroda, who has led the dance company "BATIK" and won numerous awards, plays the mother's ghost. Singer Junko Sato handles the vocals and music.
