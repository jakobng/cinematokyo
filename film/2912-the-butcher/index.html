Le Boucher

The Butcher

1970 · 監督: クロード・シャブロル · 93分 · スリラー / 犯罪 / ドラマ

フランス南西部の村で小学校の教師を務めるエレーヌは、孤独を抱えた肉屋のポポールと結婚式で隣あい、急速に親しくなる。一方、村で次々と起こり始める残忍な殺人事件。ある日、生徒たちとピクニックに出かけたエレーヌは惨殺死体を発見するが、傍らに落ちていたライターはエレーヌがポポールにプレゼントしたものとそっくりだった…。のどかな片田舎での日常に出現する異様な事態──花束に似た肉、パンに滴る血──人を愛しきることができない女、そして果てしない闇を抱えた人間がしぼり出す、一瞬の限りない切実さ。奇妙な切なさに放り出されるクライマックスも秀逸な、これぞシャブロル流愛の犯罪劇。（1970年）※全作デジタルリマスター版での上映

Directed by Claude Chabrol, a leading figure of the French New Wave known for numerous suspense and mystery masterpieces, this crime drama is set in a peaceful rural village. It quietly and sharply delves into the darkness of the human psyche through a series of murders committed by a lonely butcher and the female teacher who is loved by him. Hélène, a primary school teacher in a small village in southwestern France, meets Paul, a war veteran burdened with loneliness, and they gradually grow close. Meanwhile, a series of murders of women shakes the village's calm daily life. One day, during a picnic with her students, Hélène discovers a brutally murdered body; beside it lies a lighter identical to one she had given Paul. Stéphane Audran, who was married to Chabrol at the time, stars as Hélène, the same character name as in 'Les Biches'. Jean Yanne plays the butcher Paul. Although never theatrically released in Japan, the film will have its Japanese theatrical premiere as part of the 'Claude Chabrol Masterpiece Selection' (starting February 13, 2026, at Cinema Rise and Morc Asagaya, among others), featuring three of Chabrol's suspense films from the late 1960s to 1970.
