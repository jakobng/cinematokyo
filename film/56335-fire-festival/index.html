火まつり

Fire Festival

1985 · 監督: 柳町光男 · 125分 · ドラマ

かいせつ 熊野で実際に起きた凄惨な事件から着想し中上が脚本を書き上げた衝撃作。北大路が自滅していく男を壮絶に演じ、その情婦役・太地の激情ぶりも圧巻。

A shocking work scripted by Nakagami, inspired by a gruesome real incident in Kumano. Kitaoji delivers a devastating portrayal of a man spiraling toward self-destruction, while Taichi's fiery performance as his mistress is equally overwhelming.
