Hit the Road

2021 · 監督: Panah Panahi · 93分 · コメディ / ドラマ

A car races across the desolate landscape of Iran. In the back seat, the father entertains his exuberant younger son; in the passenger seat, the mother sways to an old pop song; and in the driver's seat, the eldest son stares silently ahead. While the younger son, unaware of the purpose of the trip, frolics innocently, the family arrives near the Turkish border...
