ハートに火をつけて（1989）

Catchfire

1990 · 監督: デニス・ホッパー · 116分 · ドラマ / アクション / スリラー / ロマンス

Ann (Jodie Foster), a contemporary artist in Los Angeles, accidentally witnesses a mafia murder. Her boyfriend Bob (Charlie Sheen) is killed, and Ann goes into hiding. Professional hitman Milo (Dennis Hopper), hired by the mafia, pursues Ann but inexplicably begins to fall in love with her. Milo gives Ann an ultimatum: "Die or become my woman."
