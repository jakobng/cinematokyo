オステンデ

Ostende

2011 · 監督: ラウラ・シタレラ · 85分 · 謎 / ドラマ

The 2011 debut film from Argentine director Laura Citarella, who has garnered significant international acclaim after her four-hour-plus mystery epic *Trenque Lauquen* was named the best film of 2023 by France’s *Cahiers du Cinéma*. The story follows a woman who becomes immersed in the fictions lurking within everyday life. After winning a radio quiz contest, Laura stays at a hotel in Ostende, near Buenos Aires. It is the off-season at the resort, and while waiting for her boyfriend to join her, she begins to observe a middle-aged man staying at the hotel with two young women. Intrigued by the man’s peculiar behavior, Laura lets her imagination run wild as she attempts to unravel the secret narrative that may be unfolding around him... The film makes its Japanese theatrical debut as part of the special screening program "Laura Citarella Retrospective: Resonating Secrets," held in conjunction with the theatrical release of *Trenque Lauquen*.
