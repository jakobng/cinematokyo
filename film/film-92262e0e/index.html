怪奇タクシー　布告を知らぬ者達に

2025 · 監督: 岡本英郎 · 95分

水木しげるに師事し、妖怪ものを中心に活動してきた漫画家・森野達弥の人気漫画「怪奇タクシー」を実写映画化。 正体不明のタクシー運転手・怪⽊焚朗を案内⼈に、高台のデートスポットにあるカーブで、カップルを乗せた車が不可解な事故に遭うという噂を取材するテレビクルーが、恐怖の体験に巻き込まれる「カーブの⼈⾷い⾃動⾞」、違法駐⾞していた⾞の渋滞により救急⾞が病院に間に合わず命を落とした悲劇の真相を描いた「違法駐⾞評論家」、ある秘密を抱えた男が参加したバスツアーで起こったさまざまな異変を描いた「ミステリー・ツアー」の3編で構成される。 出演は岡⽥結実、⽊⼾⼤聖、的場浩司、中⼭エミリ、杉浦太陽、千原せいじ、永⼭⻯弥、⼩川⿇琴ら。同原作を映画化作品「怪奇タクシー　風の夜道に気をつけろ！」でも脚本を手がけ、さまざまな特撮、アニメ作品のデザイナーとしても知られる岡本英郎がメガホンをとった。

A live-action film adaptation of Tatsuya Morino's popular manga "Kaiki Taxi," created by the artist who studied under Shigeru Mizuki and specializes in yokai (supernatural creatures) stories. The film consists of three episodes: "Curve's Man-Eating Car," where a TV crew investigating rumors of a mysterious accident involving a couple's car on a hilltop date spot curve is drawn into a terrifying experience, guided by the enigmatic taxi driver Takerō Kiboku; "Illegal Parking Critic," which reveals the truth behind a tragedy where an ambulance couldn't reach the hospital in time due to traffic caused by illegally parked cars, resulting in a death; and "Mystery Tour," depicting various strange occurrences during a bus tour attended by a man with a secret. The cast includes Yumi Okada, Taisei Kido, Koji Matoba, Emiri Nakayama, Taiyo Sugiura, Seiji Chihara, Ryūya Nagayama, and Makoto Ogawa. Hideo Okamoto, who also wrote the screenplay for the film adaptation "Kaiki Taxi: Beware the Windy Night Road" and is known as a designer for various special effects and anime works, directed the film.
