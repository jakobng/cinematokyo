4アウト　もう一度、プレイボール

4 OUTS: Back to the Ballgame

2026 · 監督: 稲垣壮洋 · 126分 · ドラマ

作家・平山讓のノンフィクション小説「4アウト　ある障害者野球チームの挑戦」を原作に、東京に初めて結成された身体障害者野球チーム「東京ブルーサンダース」の創設にまつわる実話を映画化。 子どもの頃から野球一筋だった矢上雄一は、結婚して父になってからも社会人野球に情熱を注いでいたが、年齢の壁には勝てず戦力外通告を受けてしまう。身体障害者スポーツセンターに再就職した彼は、聴覚障害を持つ泉邦衛から野球チームの監督を依頼され、戸惑いながらも引き受けることを決める。同僚の高橋明日香と共に選手集めに奔走し、やがてチームは全国大会に挑むことになるが、現実は厳しく初めての大舞台で惨敗してしまう。「勝ちたい」と言いながらも悔しそうな様子を見せない選手たちに失望した矢上は、監督を降りると宣言するが……。 相葉雅紀が矢上雄一役で主演を務め、実際の身体障害者野球チームとの交流や練習を重ねて撮影に臨んだ。矢上の同僚でチームマネージャーの高橋明日香役で黒島結菜、聴覚障害を持つ泉邦衛役で松本利夫、矢上の妻・道子役で前田亜季、障害者スポーツセンター所長・阿武隈薫役で斉藤由貴、NPO法人日本身体障害者野球連盟を創設した初代理事長をモデルとした岩橋広司役で佐々木蔵之介が共演。さらに、約200人の障がい者オーディションで選ばれたキャストが多数出演した。監督は、緒方明監督や廣木隆一監督のもとで助監督として経験を積み、本作で長編映画監督デビューを果たした稲垣壮洋。

Based on the nonfiction book '4 Out: The Challenge of a Disabled Baseball Team' by author Jo Hiryama, this film dramatizes the true story behind the founding of the Tokyo Blue Thunders, the first baseball team for people with physical disabilities formed in Tokyo. Yuichi Yagami, who devoted himself to baseball since childhood, continued to pour his passion into corporate baseball even after marrying and becoming a father, but unable to overcome the wall of age, he is told he is no longer part of the team's plans. Reemployed at a sports center for people with disabilities, he is asked by Kuniei Izumi, who has a hearing disability, to manage a baseball team, and though bewildered, he decides to accept. Together with his colleague Asuka Takahashi, he rushes around recruiting players, and the team eventually takes on the national tournament, but reality is harsh and they suffer a crushing defeat on their first big stage. Disappointed by the players who say they 'want to win' yet show no frustrated expressions, Yagami declares he will step down as manager, but... Masaki Aiba stars as Yuichi Yagami, preparing for the role through exchanges and practice with an actual disabled baseball team. Yui Aragaki plays his colleague and team manager Asuka Takahashi, Toshio Matsumoto plays the hearing-impaired Kuniei Izumi, Aki Maeda plays Yagami's wife Michiko, Yuki Saito plays sports center director Kaoru Abukuma, and Kuranosuke Sasaki plays Hiroshi Iwahashi, modeled on the founding first chairman of the NPO Japan Physically Disabled Baseball Federation. In addition, many cast members selected through an audition of about 200 people with disabilities appear. The director is Takehiro Inagaki, who gained experience as an assistant director under directors Akira Ogata and Ryuichi Hiroki and makes his feature film directorial debut with this work.
