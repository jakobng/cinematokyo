サクラマスのラストワルツ

A Trout and the Last Waltz

2026 · 監督: 坂本麻人 · 95分 · ドキュメンタリー

森と海をつなぐ流域を行き来する魚・サクラマスを通して、自然界と人間社会の関係性を問い直すドキュメンタリー。 日本を中心に分布するアジア固有のサケ科魚類・サクラマス。ヤマメとして川に生まれた後に海へと旅立ち、春の到来とともにサクラマスとなって川に帰ってくる。その旅路は森と海をつなぎ、長い時間をかけて流域の暮らしと文化を育んできたが、近代社会の発展はその往還を次第に阻んでいった。防災のために建設された数万基のダムは川と海を分断し、放棄された森林は災害のリスクを高めている。地球温暖化対策として進められている再生可能エネルギーの開発もまた、自然と社会のあり方を揺るがしていく。全国各地の川に残されたヤマメたちの姿は、連続性を絶たれてしまった流域の自然を象徴しているともいえる。 本作では、サクラマスと向き合い続けてきた研究者や水産関係者らの証言を交えながら、サクラマスの行き来する流域が織りなす世界を立体的に描き、経済発展の陰で失われつつある自然と文化の軌跡をひもといていく。ドキュメンタリー映画「ミルクの中のイワナ」で源流域に生息する幻の魚・イワナを描いた坂本麻人監督が、同作からの問いを引き継いで完成させた。

A documentary that reexamines the relationship between the natural world and human society through the sakura masu, a fish that travels back and forth through the watershed connecting forests and seas. The sakura masu is a salmonid fish native to Asia, distributed mainly in Japan. Born in rivers as yamame, it journeys to the sea and returns to the river as sakura masu with the arrival of spring. Its journey connects forests and seas and, over a long time, has nurtured the livelihoods and culture of the watershed, but the development of modern society gradually blocked this migration. Tens of thousands of dams built for disaster prevention have divided rivers from the sea, and abandoned forests have increased the risk of disasters. The development of renewable energy being promoted as a measure against global warming is also shaking the relationship between nature and society. The yamame remaining in rivers across the country can be said to symbolize the watershed's nature, which has been severed from its continuity. In this film, while incorporating testimony from researchers and fisheries officials who have continued to engage with sakura masu, the world woven by the watershed through which sakura masu travel is depicted in three dimensions, unraveling the traces of nature and culture being lost in the shadow of economic development. Director Mato Sakamoto, who depicted the phantom fish iwana living in headwater streams in the documentary "Iwana in the Milk," completed this work by carrying forward the questions from that film.
