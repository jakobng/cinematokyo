Junichiro Tanizaki's masterpiece, considered the starting point of his aesthetic literature, is boldly adapted into an original story as the film "Tattoo," set to open at Cinema Mart Shinjuku from November 13! Tanizaki's masterpiece "Tattoo," regarded as the starting point of his aesthetic literature, probes the essence of a beauty-seeking literature where the pursuit of beauty and human desire, pleasure and pain, intertwine. This pinnacle of sensuality and fetishism, previously brought to screen by masters such as Yasuzo Masumura, Chusei Sone, and Toshio Sato, is revived in the Reiwa era as an original story with a contemporary interpretation. Seiji, a tattoo artist, meets Chisato, a woman walking barefoot along the edge of the night district. Captivated by Chisato, who lives desperately, Seiji—as if resisting his body wasting away from illness—burns with the desire to tattoo her back. Just then, Seiji reunites with his childhood friend Takashi, who leads a dissolute life. Seiji, together with Takashi and Chisato, races toward a flight that abandons everything. What they see at the end of their journey of perversion and pleasure—? Here is born a story that depicts the essence of Tanizaki's literature amid the darkness and light of the present day. The protagonist Chisato is played by Megumi Kanno, marking her second collaboration with director Shinji Imaoka following "Key," based on Junichiro Tanizaki's original work. With overwhelming expressive power, she embodies a dangerous and beautiful heroine who steps into a new life together with the tattoo etched into her skin. The tattoo artist Seiji, who has little time left to live, is played by Taishi Fuchigami, active widely in film, TV dramas, and stage. He portrays with a weighty presence the obsession and sorrow of a man fixated on the immortal beauty of tattooing. The master tattoo artist who supports Seiji is played by the renowned actor Takehiro Murata; the woman Seiji admires whom he met in childhood is played by Noriko Ohara; and Seiji's childhood friend is played by Daichi Saeki. In addition, a diverse cast including Yoko Kamon and Butchaa assembles. They bring blood to the world of sensuality and perversion. Directing is the prodigy Shinji Imaoka, who works freely and boundlessly across genres and continues to depict human foolishness and lovability. While inheriting the perverse aesthetics of Tanizaki's literature, he sublimates it into a contemporary story of lonely people leaning on one another. The unveiled poster vividly shows the tattoo on Chisato, played by Kanno. It is a visual steeped in overflowing fetishism and unfathomable passion. A teaser trailer has also been released, beginning with a depiction of Seiji tattooing Chisato, along with his line, "The last thing I want to do—this is all I have."
