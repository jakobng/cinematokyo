BU・SU

Bu su

1987 · 監督: 市川準 · 95分 · ドラマ

A story depicting a girl who is "ugly" in personality as she moves to Tokyo, accumulates various experiences, breaks out of her shell, and grows. Turning eighteen, Mugiko Morishita was born and raised in the countryside and was a twisted, gloomy girl. To cure this "ugliness of the heart," she moves to Tokyo, where she is given the name Suzume and, while apprenticing as a geisha at the okiya run by her aunt Kocho, attends high school.
