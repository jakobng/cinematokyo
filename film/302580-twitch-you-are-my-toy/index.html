Twitch: You Are My Toy

2004 · 監督: 田尻裕司 · 64分 · ドラマ

Minori, a photographer, has been carrying on an affair with Kushida, a magazine editor-in-chief. One day, Minori goes to interview Ageha, a female adult manga artist. Not only does she arrive late to the meeting, but her blunt attitude angers Ageha and causes trouble for Shinichi, an editor. Kushida is furious at Shinichi for botching the interview and tells him to just publish a fabricated article to push Ageha into committing to drawing a new work. Shinichi begins writing the manuscript as instructed. His girlfriend Midori gently watches over him. Minori, held in Kushida's arms, wavers inside over her affair with him. When she visits Shinichi's apartment with the finished photos of Ageha, Midori answers the door. She was Minori's junior in a college club.
