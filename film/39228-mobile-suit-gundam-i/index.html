The first of a three-part theatrical compilation re-editing the TV anime "Mobile Suit Gundam," broadcast from 1979 to 1980. In Universal Century 0079, the majority of humanity lives in space colonies built in outer space, and only a limited number of people are able to live on Earth, where humanity was born and raised. Against the Earth Federation government, which rules humanity from such a peaceful Earth, the frontier group of space colonies known as "Side 3" declares independence. Calling itself the Principality of Zeon, it launches a war of independence against the Federation using humanoid mobile weapons called mobile suits (MS). Amuro Ray, a shy boy living in Side 7, is caught up in the battle between the Zeon and Federation forces that erupts over the state-of-the-art MS "Gundam," which his father Tem Ray, a Federation engineer, helped develop, and he ends up boarding the Gundam by circumstance. Despite it being his first battle, Amuro destroys a Zeon MS and becomes the Gundam's pilot, evading pursuit by the Zeon ace known as the "Red Comet," Char Aznable, and racing across the battlefield while feeling confusion and fear about fighting.
