私がまだ知らない沖縄

2026 · 監督: 猪股東吾 · 110分

沖縄の島々に暮らす人たちの声から、沖縄と日本の関係を見つめ直したドキュメンタリー。 豊かな自然と温暖な気候に恵まれ、琉球王国時代から受け継がれてきた独自の文化・風習をもつ沖縄の島々。その一方で、戦争では20万人以上が犠牲となり、戦後も日本国内における米軍基地の約7割がこの地に集まり、県民所得は全国最下位で3人に1人の子どもが貧困状態にある。戦後81年、本土復帰54年を経た現在、沖縄では何が起こり、そこに暮らす人々は何を見つめ、どのような問題と向き合っているのか。10代から80代までの市井の人々の語りの集積から、沖縄の実像を重層的に浮かび上がらせていく。 監督は、大袈裟太郎という芸名でも知られるジャーナリストでラッパーの猪股東吾。東京から沖縄に移住して取材を続けるなかで、沖縄が抱えるさまざまな問題の根幹に「本土」側の無関心と構造的差別があることを実感した猪股監督が、「やまとんちゅ（本土出身者）」という立場から初メガホンをとり、4年の歳月をかけて完成させた。

A documentary that reexamines the relationship between Okinawa and Japan through the voices of people living on Okinawa's islands. Blessed with rich nature and a warm climate, the islands of Okinawa possess a unique culture and customs inherited from the era of the Ryukyu Kingdom. On the other hand, more than 200,000 people lost their lives in the war, and even after the war, about 70% of the U.S. military bases in Japan are concentrated in this region, prefectural income is the lowest in the country, and one in three children lives in poverty. Now, 81 years after the war and 54 years after the reversion to the mainland, what is happening in Okinawa, what are the people living there gazing at, and what problems are they facing? Through the accumulation of testimonies from ordinary citizens ranging from their teens to their eighties, the true image of Okinawa is brought to light in multiple layers. The director is Togo Inomata, a journalist and rapper also known by the stage name Daikisho Taro. While continuing to interview after moving from Tokyo to Okinawa, Inomata came to realize that at the root of the various problems Okinawa faces lies the indifference and structural discrimination of the "mainland." From his position as a "Yamatonchu" (person from the mainland), he directed his first feature film, completing it over the course of four years.
