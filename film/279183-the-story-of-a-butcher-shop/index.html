ある精肉店のはなし

The Story of a Butcher Shop

2013 · 監督: 纐纈あや · 108分 · ドキュメンタリー

大阪貝塚市で代々、育てた牛を家族で食肉処理し、販売している精肉店を営む一家を、温かなまなざしで見つめたドキュメンタリー。大阪貝塚市にある北出精肉店では、7代目の長男が肉質を見極めて切り分け、妻は接客にいそしみ、ガレージで太鼓屋の看板を掲げる次男も自ら牛を解体し、なめした皮を使ってだんじり太鼓の皮の張り替えを行っている。しかし、穏やかに暮らす一家の心の中には、その仕事ゆえにいわれなき差別を受け続けてきた父の姿があった。それでも仕事に対する誇りをもって自らを律して生き、命を食べて人は生きるという生の本質を見つめ続けている一家の1年間を記録した。原発開発計画に反対する山口県祝島の人々を描いて大きな反響を呼んだ「祝（ほうり）の島」の纐纈あや監督の第2作。

A documentary that warmly observes a family running a butcher shop in Kaizuka City, Osaka, who for generations have raised cattle, slaughtered them as a family, and sold the meat. At Kitade Meat Shop in Kaizuka City, Osaka, the eldest son—the seventh-generation owner—assesses the quality of the meat and cuts it, his wife attends to customers, and the second son—who also runs a taiko drum shop under a sign in the garage—butchers the cattle himself and uses the tanned hides to re-skin danjiri festival drums. Yet within the hearts of this peacefully living family lies the image of their father, who endured unjust discrimination throughout his life because of this work. Despite this, the family lives with pride in their craft and self-discipline, continuing to contemplate the essence of life—that people live by eating the lives of others. The film records one year in the life of this family. It is the second film by director Aya Hanabusa, whose 'Island of Dreams' about the people of Suo-Oshima in Yamaguchi Prefecture opposing a nuclear power development plan drew great response.
