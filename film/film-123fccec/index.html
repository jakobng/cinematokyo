岡本万太

監督: 真田宗仁郎

The protagonist, Manta Okamoto (Sojiro Sanada), is a 26-year-old part-timer. Plagued by mounting irritation over life’s minor inconveniences, he lives in constant fear of whether he can suppress his own impulses. For Manta is a man of uncontrollable rage. Once a month, his stress reaches a breaking point—his "Limit Day." To cope, he locks himself away at home, strapped to a bed with restraints. Because these days strike without warning, he frequently misses work, leading to a cycle of being fired and drifting from one job to the next. One day, Nakamura (Masaki Miura), a former senior colleague from a publishing house that let him go, approaches him with a new opportunity. The job is simple: store some cardboard boxes for a few days in exchange for a large sum of money. But...
