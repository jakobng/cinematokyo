1963 · 監督: フェデリコ・フェリ-ニ · 139分 · ドラマ

The undisputed representative work of director F. Fellini. Film director Guido dreams one day of his body falling through the air. To heal the various mental and physical fatigue associated with his daily life, he goes to a spa for rest and recuperation, but even there he cannot escape from work and life. And finally, he begins to hallucinate that he is among the elderly spending their remaining years at the spa... This film is an autobiographical work by Fellini himself, and its depiction is superb, portraying reality and fantasy in parallel! A must-see!!
