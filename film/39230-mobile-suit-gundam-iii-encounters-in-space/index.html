The third installment of the theatrical trilogy re-editing the TV anime "Mobile Suit Gundam" broadcast from 1979 to 1980. The White Base, having returned to space once more, engages in operations as a diversionary unit, but the White Base is relentlessly pursued by its sworn enemy Char Aznable. As the main Earth Federation Forces move to capture the Zeon fortress Solomon, the White Base sets course for the neutral colony cluster Side 6 in order to divert Zeon's attention. Amuro reunites with his father at Side 6, but is devastated by his father's transformed state. Around this time, Amuro encounters the woman of destiny, Lalah Sune.
