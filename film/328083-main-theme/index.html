メイン・テーマ

Main Theme

1984 · 監督: 森田芳光 · 101分 · コメディ / ドラマ / ロマンス

昨日まで幼稚園の先生だった小笠原しぶきは、房総の海岸で手品の修行のために全国を回っている健と出会う。2人は彼の４ＷＤで一緒に旅をすることになるが、ソリが合わずケンカばかり。やがて2人は沖縄へ……。若いカップルの道中に中年の男女の恋を絡めた、ライト感覚の青春映画。森田と薬師丸ひろ子のコンビはこれが初めて。相手役・野村宏伸は新人だった。

Ogasawara Shibuki meets Daito Shima Ken, who is training in magic, and decides to go on a trip in his car. Shibuki's destination is Osaka, and Ken's is Okinawa. The two don't get along and argue constantly during the trip. In Hamamatsu, Ken's uncle has a magic show, and Shibuki ends up helping out. However, during the show, Ken meets with a jazz singer named Ise Masayo. Seeing Ken so smitten with Masayo, Shibuki feels jealous. The car arrives in Osaka, and the two part ways. But Shibuki can't stop thinking about Ken and sets off for Okinawa, where her sister and brother-in-law live.
