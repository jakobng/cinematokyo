TSUMUGU

2016 · 監督: 犬童一利 · 109分 · ドラマ

和紙をはじめとする伝統産業が盛んな福井県丹南地域と、百済時代の面影を残す韓国・扶余（プヨ）を舞台に、頑固な職人と韓国から来た若い娘が次第に心を通わせていく人間ドラマ。妻を亡くして以来、偏屈な性格から誰とも心を通わせることなく生きる、越前和紙職人の剛生。脳腫瘍で倒れ、半身まひで介護が必要な体となってしまった剛生のもとにヘルパーとしてやって来たのが、韓国からワーキングホリデーで福井に訪れたフリーターのヨナだった。頑固な剛生と勝ち気なヨナ、文化も異なる2人は当然のように衝突するが、ヨナの常識にとらわれない介護により、固く閉ざされた剛生の心が次第に開いていく。主人公・剛生役に、役者人生50年にして本作が映画初主演となる石倉三郎。ヨナ役には「息もできない」のキム・コッピ。監督は「カミングアウト」「早乙女4姉妹」などを手がけた犬童一利。

A human drama set in the Tan'nan region of Fukui Prefecture, known for traditional industries such as washi paper, and Buyeo, South Korea, which retains traces of the Baekje era, depicting a stubborn craftsman and a young woman from Korea who gradually open their hearts to each other. Gosei is an Echizen washi craftsman who, since losing his wife, has lived without connecting with anyone due to his cantankerous nature. After collapsing from a brain tumor and becoming hemiplegic and in need of care, the helper who comes to him is Yona, a freeter who came to Fukui from Korea on a working holiday. The stubborn Gosei and the strong-willed Yona, with their different cultures, naturally clash, but through Yona's unconventional caregiving, Gosei's tightly closed heart gradually opens. Saburo Ishikura, in his 50th year as an actor, plays the protagonist Gosei in his first film lead role. Yona is played by Kim Kkob-bi of "Breathless." The director is Kazutoshi Inudo, known for "Coming Out" and "The Four Sisters of Saotome."
