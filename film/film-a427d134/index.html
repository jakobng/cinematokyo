石塚淳（歌とギター）、山本啓太（ベース）、伊奈昌宏（ドラムス）による三人組バンド・台風クラブ。“日本語ロックの西日”と称された、侘び寂びとノスタルジーが滲む人懐っこく耳に残る楽曲と、手刷りのTシャツや手の込んだレコードなど、こだわりと独自の美学が宿る作品は多くの著名人や音楽ファンに愛されている。

The three-piece band Taifu Club consists of Jun Ishizuka (vocals and guitar), Keita Yamamoto (bass), and Masahiro Ina (drums). Called "the setting sun of Japanese-language rock," their catchy, friendly songs imbued with wabi-sabi and nostalgia, along with their meticulously crafted works—from hand-printed T-shirts to elaborate records—reflect a unique aesthetic and dedication that has endeared them to many celebrities and music fans. Over ten years have passed since their first live performance on New Year's Day 2015 in the rain at the Kamogawa Delta in Kyoto. This film, directed by member Keita Yamamoto himself, documents the band's five-year journey, focusing on their second album produced and released after the COVID-19 pandemic, their long-distance band activities after Yamamoto and Ina left Kyoto, and their largest and longest live tour to date. It is a documentary following everyday artists, exploring the meaning of continuing band activities while holding jobs unrelated to music, and the pursuit of creation and expression. From Kyoto, the setting sun of Japanese-language rock. A three-piece band: Jun Ishizuka (vocals & guitar), Keita Yamamoto (bass), and Masahiro Ina (drums). Formed in 2013, they began full-scale activities with their current lineup in 2015. They released their first album 'Early Taifu Club' in 2017, winning the runner-up prize at the 2018 CD Shop Awards. They performed at FUJI ROCK FESTIVAL '18. Their second album 'Album Second Collection' was released in 2023, and their solo concert at Tokyo Kinema Club sold out on the day of release. Their DIY activities based on their unique aesthetic and their timelessly appealing music have garnered support from artists such as aiko, Kazunobu Minegishi (Ging Nang BOYZ), and Asako Miyazaki (SHISHAMO).
