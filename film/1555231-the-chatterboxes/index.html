みんな、おしゃべり！

The Chatterboxes

2025 · 監督: 河合健 · 143分 · ドラマ

ろう者の父と弟がいる古賀家と、その街に新しく越してきたクルド人一家が、些細なすれ違いから対立する。二つの家族の通訳として繰り出されたのは、古賀家で唯一の聴者である娘の夏海と、クルド人一家で唯一日本語が話せるワヒだった。二人は次第に惹かれ合っていくが、両者の対立は深まるばかり。そんなある日、古賀家の弟・駿が描いた謎の文字をきっかけに、小さな対立は街を巻き込む問題へと発展、想像を超えた結末へと向かっていく——。

A comedy about a prideful feud between a deaf Japanese family and a Kurdish family, told through Japanese Sign Language and Kurdish. Natsumi Koga lives with her deaf father and younger brother, who run an electronics shop. One day, their family gets into a minor conflict with a Kurdish family living in the same town. Natsumi, who can hear, and Hiwa, the only member of the Kurdish family who speaks Japanese, are drafted as interpreters for their respective families. As they interpret for each other, a bond of trust grows between Natsumi and Hiwa, but the conflict between the two families only deepens. Then one day, a mysterious drawing by Natsumi's younger brother Shun escalates into an incident that engulfs the entire town. Nagisa Nagashima of "The End of Love" plays the protagonist Natsumi. Kazuyoshi Kezuka, a deaf ramen shop owner in Tokyo's Nishi-Nippori, makes his acting debut as Natsumi's father. Hideaki Nasu of the TV drama "Deaf Voice: Court Sign Language Interpreter" and Akito Imai of "The Two Worlds of My Life" play the father's friends. Shunya Itabashi of "Laughing Kaibutsu" plays a community organizer planning a town revitalization project, and Karin Ono of "Animation x Paralympic: Who Is the Hero?" plays a teacher at a school for the deaf. The film is directed by Ken Kawai, known for works like "Nanno-chan's Second World War" and who is himself a CODA (Child of Deaf Adults).
