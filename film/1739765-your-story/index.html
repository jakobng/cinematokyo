君の話

Your Story

2026 · 監督: 中川龍太郎 · 120分 · ドラマ / ロマンス

「恋する寄生虫」などで知られる小説家・三秋縋による長編小説「君の話」を、主演に北村匠海、ヒロインに出口夏希を迎えて実写映画化した純愛ラブストーリー。 孤独に生きる大学生の千尋は、不幸な過去を忘れるため、記憶を消す治療を受ける。しかし目を覚ましても記憶は消えておらず、会ったこともない幼馴染・灯花との初恋の思い出が残されていた。作られた記憶の中にしかいないはずの灯花の存在は、いつしか千尋の心から離れなくなっていく。そんなある日、現実世界の千尋の前に、灯花が実体をもって姿を現す。 千尋を北村匠海、灯花を出口夏希が演じ、千尋と同じ大学に通う親友・江森役でアイドルグループ「Aぇ! group」の佐野晶哉、千尋の高校時代の同級生・桐本役で「死刑にいたる病」の宮﨑優、千尋の父親役でお笑いトリオ「ネプチューン」の原田泰造が出演。NHK連続テレビ小説「虎に翼」の吉田恵里香が脚本を手がけ、「やがて海へと届く」「四月の永い夢」の中川龍太郎監督がメガホンをとった。2026年・第39回東京国際映画祭「ガラ・セレクション」部門上映作品。

A pure love story adapted into a live-action film from the novel "Kimi no Hanashi" by Miaki Sumi, the author known for "Parasite in Love," starring Takumi Kitamura and Natsuki Deguchi as the heroine. Chihiro, a university student living in solitude, undergoes a memory-erasing treatment to forget his unhappy past. However, when he wakes up, his memories have not been erased, and he retains memories of a first love with a childhood friend named Hika, whom he has never even met. The presence of Hika, who should only exist within fabricated memories, eventually becomes inseparable from Chihiro's heart. Then one day, Hika appears before Chihiro in the real world in physical form. Takumi Kitamura plays Chihiro and Natsuki Deguchi plays Hika, with Masaya Sano of the idol group "Ae! group" as Chihiro's close friend Emori who attends the same university, Yu Miyazaki of "The Hanging Garden" as Kirimoto, Chihiro's high school classmate, and Taizo Harada of the comedy trio "Neptune" as Chihiro's father. The screenplay is written by Erika Yoshida of the NHK serial TV drama "Tora ni Tsubasa," and the film is directed by Ryutaro Nakagawa of "Soon We'll Reach the Sea" and "April's Long Dream." It is an official selection in the Gala Selection section of the 39th Tokyo International Film Festival in 2026.
