TRUE KYOHEI SAKAGUCHI SHOW 坂口恭平生活

TRUE KYOHEI SAKAGUCHI SHOW

2026 · 監督: 小宮雄貴 · 147分 · ドキュメンタリー

作家、画家、音楽家、建築家など、多岐にわたる活動をおこなう坂口恭平の生活と創作を追ったドキュメンタリー。 自身の電話番号を公開して希死念慮を持つ人々からの電話相談を受け続ける傍ら、躁鬱病の当事者であることを公言し、時に深い鬱に潜って自らと向き合う。彼は誇大妄想家か、救世主か。TRUEであり、SHOWでもある生活とは？

A documentary capturing the life and creative output of Kyohei Sakaguchi, a multi-disciplinary artist active as a writer, painter, musician, and architect. Born in Kumamoto Prefecture in 1978, Sakaguchi is known for numerous works, including *0-Yen House*, a photo book documenting the dwellings of the homeless, and *How to Build an Independent State*, which reexamines the role of government and the nation. In 2011, spurred by a lack of trust in the government’s response to the Great East Japan Earthquake, Sakaguchi declared the establishment of a "New Government" in his hometown of Kumamoto and began accepting evacuees at his base, "Zero Center." Since 2012, he has made his personal phone number public to continue "Inocchi no Denwa" (Telephone of Life), a telephone consultation service for those struggling with suicidal ideation. Sakaguchi is also open about his own experience with bipolar disorder, at times sinking into deep depression to confront himself. New director Yuki Komiya, making his feature debut, began following Sakaguchi over a long period after visiting a solo exhibition and filming a live performance as an audience member. The film records Sakaguchi's daily life in Kumamoto, as well as his exhibitions and events across the country. Even as filming was interrupted when Sakaguchi fell into a deep depression during production, the documentary vividly captures the process of him giving birth to new words while facing a fundamental "loneliness" he has carried since before his childhood. Director Komiya personally edited footage previously released on YouTube to complete this theatrical version.
