恋傷

2026 · 監督: 森岡利行 · 116分

漫画家・天堂きりんの同名コミックを実写映画化した恋愛群像劇。恋に傷つきながらも誰かを求めてしまう人々の姿を通して、現代の孤独と承認欲求を描く。 秋華と久志は穏やかな新婚生活を送っていたが、久志が会社の同僚・涼子を気にかけるようになったことで、2人の間に亀裂が生じはじめる。秋華は隣室に暮らす大学生・譲治との出会いをきっかけに、自らの孤独と向き合っていく。一方、久志の兄・啓太の妻である夏美は、学生時代から憧れの存在だった啓太と結婚し家庭を築いたが、その胸の奥には拭いきれない寂しさがあった。ある夜、その寂しさは久志を巻き込み、2人は許されない一線を越えてしまう。離婚によって傷ついた涼子。夫のいる女性への想いに揺れる譲治。親友を見守り続ける真冬。人の孤独に寄り添う風俗嬢のベル。それぞれが抱える孤独や欲望、後悔、罪悪感は、やがて互いの人生を結びつけていく。 アイドルグループ「SKE48」の元メンバーである高畑結希が映画初主演を果たし、幼少期からのコンプレックスを抱え孤独を募らせる主人公・夏美を演じる。夏美の夫・啓太役で内藤秀一郎、啓太の弟・久志役で堀海登、久志の妻・秋華役で新藤まなみ、久志に惹かれる同僚・涼子役で入来茉里、夢を追う風俗嬢・ベル役で三輪晴香が共演。監督・脚本は、「向田理髪店」「純平、考え直せ」の森岡利行。

A live-action romantic ensemble film adapted from Kirin Tendo's manga of the same name. Through people who, though wounded by love, still seek someone, it depicts the loneliness and desire for approval of modern life. Akika and Hisashi are enjoying a peaceful newlywed life, but when Hisashi begins to take an interest in his coworker Ryoko, cracks begin to form between the two. Akika, through meeting Joji, a university student living in the neighboring apartment, begins to confront her own loneliness. Meanwhile, Natsumi—wife of Hisashi's older brother Keita—married Keita, whom she had admired since her student days, and built a family, but deep in her heart lies an unshakeable loneliness. One night, that loneliness draws Hisashi in, and the two cross a line that cannot be forgiven. Ryoko is wounded by divorce. Joji wavers over his feelings for a married woman. Mafuyu continues to watch over her best friend. Bell, a sex worker who attends to people's loneliness. The loneliness, desires, regrets, and guilt each of them carries eventually come to connect their lives. Yuki Takahata, a former member of the idol group SKE48, makes her first film lead appearance, playing protagonist Natsumi, who carries childhood insecurities and growing loneliness. Shuichiro Naito plays Natsumi's husband Keita, Kaito Hori plays Keita's younger brother Hisashi, Manami Shindo plays Hisashi's wife Akika, Mari Iriki plays coworker Ryoko who is drawn to Hisashi, and Haruka Miwa plays Bell, a sex worker chasing her dreams. Directed and written by Toshiyuki Morioka (Mukaida Barber Shop, Junpei, Think Again).
