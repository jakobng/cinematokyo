Men & Chicken

2015 · 監督: Anders Thomas Jensen · 104分 · コメディ / ドラマ

. あなたの“マッツ愛”が試される?! 狂気的な怪演が光る 奇想天外、クセ者たちのルーツ探しの狂想曲 大学教授のガブリエルと、女とトリビアにしか興味がないエリアスの兄弟は、父親の遺品からビデオレターを発見し、父がふたりの生物学的な父親ではないこと、それぞれの母親も違うことを知る。二人は父親の住所を探し出すが、辿り着いたのは、家畜が放し飼いにされ、3人の異母兄弟が暮らす寂れた屋敷だった...。 . マッツが演じるのはこんな役！ 無造作なクセ毛に無精ヒゲ、短気で暴力的、対人関係に難ありの偏屈な男エリアス 女好きで狂気的な役柄を怪演するも、その色気は隠しきれない…?! . .

Will your "Mads love" be put to the test?! A wild, eccentric rhapsody of a roots-seeking journey among oddballs, featuring a crazed, standout performance. Gabriel, a university professor, and his brother Elias, who cares only about women and trivia, discover a video letter among their late father's belongings and learn that their father was not their biological parent and that each of them has a different mother. The two track down their father's address, but what they arrive at is a desolate mansion where livestock roam freely and three half-brothers live... Mads plays a role like this! Elias: a grumpy, difficult man with unkempt curly hair and stubble, short-tempered and violent, with trouble in interpersonal relationships. He gives a crazed, standout performance as a womanizing character, yet his allure cannot be hidden...?!
