塀の中のプレイ・ボール

"Guys Who Never Learn" Part 2

1987 · 監督: 鈴木則文 · 104分 · コメディ

かいせつ 『塀の中の懲りない面々』の脚本を手掛けた鈴木が監督した、原作続編の映画化。休憩中のソフトボールが生き甲斐の受刑者たちが、刑務所内でかつて行われた伝説の看守との対抗試合を夢見るが・・・。受刑者役の顔ぶれが個性的！

A film adaptation of the sequel to the original work, directed by Suzuki, who wrote the screenplay for "The Unruly Guys Behind Bars." Inmates whose sole purpose in life is playing softball during breaks dream of a legendary match against the guards that once took place in the prison... The cast of inmates is wonderfully unique!
