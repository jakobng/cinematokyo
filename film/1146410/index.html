旅人の必需品

여행자의 필요

2024 · 監督: ホン・サンス · 90分 · ドラマ

韓国を代表する映画作家ホン・サンスが「3人のアンヌ」「クレアのカメラ」に続いてイザベル・ユペールと3度目のタッグを組んだコメディドラマ。 ソウルを旅する謎めいたフランス人女性イリス。生活費を稼ぐためフランス語の個人レッスンをしているが、そのあまりにも風変わりな教え方に生徒たちは戸惑いを隠しきれない。レッスンを終えると、彼女は年下のボーイフレンドのアパートへと帰っていく。イリスは何のために韓国へやってきたのか、そしてなぜフランス語を教えているのか。韓国の国民的詩人ユン・ドンジュの詩に触れるなかで、謎に包まれた彼女の日常が徐々に浮かび上がっていく。 共演にもホン・サンス監督作の常連俳優が顔をそろえ、主人公イリスがフランス語を教える生徒ウォンジュをイ・ヘヨン、そのパートナーをクォン・ヘヒョ、イリスのボーイフレンド・イングクをハ・ソングク、その母親をチョ・ユニがそれぞれ演じた。2024年・第74回ベルリン国際映画祭コンペティション部門に出品され、審査員グランプリ（銀熊賞）を受賞。日本では、ホン・サンス監督のデビュー30周年を記念して5カ月連続で新作を上映する企画「月刊ホン・サンス」の第1弾作品として劇場公開。

Winner of the Silver Bear (Grand Jury Prize) at the Berlin International Film Festival. The third collaboration between legendary French actress Isabelle Huppert and Korean master director Hong Sang-soo. Through the footsteps of a traveler who loves makgeolli and poetry, we encounter the humorous and mysterious streets and people of Seoul. Thirty years have passed since his 1996 feature debut, *The Day a Pig Fell into the Well*. Having released over 30 films to date, Hong Sang-soo continues to expand his filmography at an even more prolific pace in recent years. Born in Seoul and having studied art in the United States, Hong has established a production style that keeps its distance from big-budget commercial cinema. With five awards at the Berlin International Film Festival and acclaim at Cannes, Venice, and Locarno, he has maintained a truly singular presence in world cinema. To commemorate the 30th anniversary of Director Hong Sang-soo’s debut, "Monthly Hong Sang-soo" will be held over five months from November 2025 to March 2026, premiering five new films (including his latest) on a monthly basis. The event kicks off with *A Traveler’s Needs*, starring Isabelle Huppert as a mysterious traveler who arrives in Seoul. Additionally, a special retrospective titled "Extra Edition Hong Sang-soo" will run concurrently at select theaters, revisiting past works with themes linked to the new releases. The first installment is *Hill of Freedom* (2014), featuring Ryo Kase as a man who travels to Seoul in pursuit of an older Korean woman. This program offers a unique opportunity to experience the stories of two "strangers" in Seoul through both his newest and classic works.
