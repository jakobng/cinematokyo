平行と垂直

Lives at Right Angles

2026 · 監督: 小林聖太郎 · 118分 · ドラマ / ファミリー

安田章大（SUPER EIGHT）とのんが主演を務め、自閉スペクトラム症の兄と結婚を控える妹の温かくも強い絆を描いたヒューマンドラマ。 自閉スペクトラム症の大貴と、幼い頃から彼を支えてきた妹の希。母を早くに亡くした彼らはネグレクト気味の父から距離を置き、ふたりで懸命に生きてきた。現在、大貴は清掃の仕事に就き、周囲のサポートを受けながら自立した生活を送っている。大貴はほとんど会話をせず、表情もあまり変わらないように見える。独自の規則を持つ彼は、すべてを平行と垂直に並べるほど几帳面でこだわりが強く、机に並ぶ食器もていねいにそろえる。週に1度の希との食事の時間は19時ちょうどで、1分でも過ぎると落ち着かなくなる。カウンセラーとして働いている希は、ある日恋人からプロポーズを受け、一抹の不安を抱えながら大貴とともに恋人の両親に会いに大阪から東京へ向かう。希の結婚話をきっかけに、兄妹は思いがけずお互いの過去と未来に向き合うことになる。 劇団ふくふくや主宰で俳優としても活躍する山野海によるオリジナル脚本に感銘を受けた安田章大が製作を熱望し、企画から参加。自閉スペクトラム症の専門家の監修のもと約2年かけて脚本を練り、安田自身も専門家のレクチャーを受けて撮影に臨んだ。監督は「かぞくのひけつ」「毎日かあさん」の小林聖太郎。

Yasuda Shota (SUPER EIGHT) and Non star in this human drama depicting the warm yet strong bond between a brother with autism spectrum disorder and his sister who is about to get married. Taiki, who has autism spectrum disorder, and his sister Nozomi, who has supported him since childhood. Having lost their mother early, they kept their distance from their neglectful father and lived desperately on their own. Now, Taiki works a cleaning job and lives independently with support from those around him. Taiki rarely speaks and his expressions seem to barely change. He has his own rules and is so meticulous and particular that he arranges everything in parallel and perpendicular lines, carefully lining up the dishes on the table. His weekly meal with Nozomi is exactly at 7:00 PM, and he becomes restless if it's even a minute past. Nozomi, who works as a counselor, one day receives a proposal from her boyfriend, and with a hint of unease, heads to Tokyo with Taiki to meet her boyfriend's parents. Triggered by Nozomi's marriage talk, the siblings unexpectedly come face to face with each other's past and future. Yasuda Shota, deeply impressed by the original screenplay by Yamano Umi—who leads the theater company Fuku Fukuya and is also active as an actress—fervently wished to produce it and participated from the planning stage. The script was developed over about two years under the supervision of an autism spectrum disorder specialist, and Yasuda himself received lectures from the specialist before filming. Directed by Kobayashi Seitaro of "Kazoku no Hiketsu" and "Mainichi Kaasan."
