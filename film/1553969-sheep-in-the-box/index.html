Sheep in the Box

2026 · 監督: 是枝裕和監督 · 126分 · ドラマ / サイエンスフィクション

「怪物」「万引き家族」の是枝裕和監督が、綾瀬はるかとお笑いコンビ「千鳥」の大悟を主演に迎え、 亡き息子の姿をしたヒューマノイドを迎え入れた夫婦の物語をオリジナル脚本で描いた長編映画。 息子を亡くして2年、建築家の音々と工務店の二代目社長を務める健介の甲本夫婦は、 息子・翔の姿をしたヒューマノイドを迎え入れることになる。 彼が到着した日、「おかえり」と駆け寄り喜びを隠さない音々と、戸惑いを隠せない硬い表情の健介。 「パパだよね」と問いかけられた健介は、「おじさんでええよ」と答えるのだった。 少しずつ動き始める家族の時間。静かに広がっていく波紋。 ほどなく予期せぬ事態が起こり、夫婦がそれぞれに抱く息子の死への想いが露わになっていくのだった。 夫婦とは？家族とは？彼らは大きな決断に迫られる。 そんな中、ヒューマノイド翔は密かにヒューマノイドの仲間たちとつながり始める──。

A feature film directed by Hirokazu Kore-eda ("Monster", "Shoplifters"), starring Haruka Ayase and Daigo (from the comedy duo "Chidori"), based on an original script about a couple who welcome a humanoid in the form of their deceased son. Set in the near future. Architect Kōmoto Oto and her husband Kensuke, the second-generation president of a construction company, take in a humanoid resembling their son Sho, who died two years ago. On the day the humanoid arrives, Oto joyfully welcomes him with Sho's same smile and voice, while Kensuke hides his bewilderment behind a stiff expression. Family time gradually begins, but an unexpected event occurs, revealing the couple's hidden feelings about their son's death. Meanwhile, the humanoid Sho secretly starts connecting with other humanoids. The role of the deceased son Sho and his humanoid counterpart is played by Kuwaki Rito, chosen from over 200 auditionees. Kiyono Yasho plays Oto's younger sister Kotaki Arisu; Kanichiro plays Nikawa Gen, an employee at Kensuke's construction company Tamaken; Yo Kimiko plays Oto's mother Nishimura Nobuyo; and Tanaka Min plays Yamagata Akio, a skilled worker at Tamaken. The title "The Sheep in the Box" is derived from a passage in Antoine de Saint-Exupéry's classic novel "The Little Prince". The film was selected for the Competition section of the 79th Cannes Film Festival in 2026.
