冤罪のつくりかた

2026 · 監督: 泊誠也 · 124分

2019年にIR事業をめぐる汚職事件で逮捕され有罪判決を受けた元衆議院議員・あきもと司の手記「事実無根　私はこうして特捜に嵌められた」を原案に、“冤罪をつくる側”の視点から描いた社会派サスペンス。 東京地検特捜部に配属されたばかりの若き検察事務官・早乙女直樹は、指導役を務めるエース検事・如月美麗が1枚の供述調書を“作り上げていく”現場を目の当たりにする。それは事件の捜査ではなく、事件そのものの製造だった。「特捜は正義の組織ではなく、“勝つ”ための組織だ」と語る如月は、微笑みながら被疑者を追い込み、望んだ供述だけを引き出していく。そんな彼女の標的となったのは、秘書から身を起こし40代で副大臣にまでのぼりつめた衆議院議員・塚本明。身に覚えのない罪を着せられた彼は否認を貫くが、証言の買収と捏造された調書によって追い詰められていく。 特捜部のエース検事・如月美麗役で加藤夏希、新人検察事務官・早乙女直樹役でダンス＆ボーカルグループ「BUDDiiS」の小川史記、逮捕された衆議院議員・塚本明役で原田龍二、事件の真相を追う新聞記者・神崎ゴウ役でやべきょうすけが出演。実業家の堀江貴文が本人役で特別出演。

A social suspense film based on the memoir 'Groundless: How I Was Framed by the Special Investigation' by Tsukasa Akimoto, a former member of the House of Representatives arrested and convicted in a 2019 corruption case over an IR project, depicting events from the perspective of those who 'manufacture false charges.' Naoki Saotome, a young prosecutor's assistant newly assigned to the Tokyo District Public Prosecutors Office Special Investigation Department, witnesses his mentor, ace prosecutor Mirei Kisaragi, 'fabricating' a single statement record. It is not the investigation of a case but the manufacture of the case itself. Kisaragi, who says 'The Special Investigation is not an organization of justice but an organization for winning,' smiles as she corners suspects and draws out only the statements she wants. Her target becomes Akira Tsukamoto, a member of the House of Representatives who rose from secretary to deputy minister in his forties. Framed for a crime he did not commit, he maintains his denial but is driven into a corner through bought testimony and fabricated records. Natsuki Kato plays ace prosecutor Mirei Kisaragi, Shiki Ogawa of the dance & vocal group BUDDiiS plays rookie prosecutor's assistant Naoki Saotome, Ryuji Harada plays arrested Representative Akira Tsukamoto, and Kyosuke Yabe plays newspaper reporter Go Kanzaki, who pursues the truth of the case. Businessman Takafumi Horie makes a special appearance as himself.
