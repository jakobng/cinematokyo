そして彼女たちは

Young Mothers

2025 · 監督: リュック・ダルデンヌ · 104分 · ドラマ

An ensemble drama by Belgian masters Jean-Pierre and Luc Dardenne, known for "The Child" and "The Kid with a Bike," depicting five young mothers living in a mother-child support facility. The five girls—Jessica, Perla, Ariane, Julie, and Naima—live together in a facility that supports women who became pregnant young. Without anyone to rely on and facing various problems like poverty and violence, they become mothers while confused, troubled, and unable to find a model for the family they should aim for. Struggling not to be swallowed by the overwhelming loneliness, the girls who still wish "to love" sometimes lean on others and choose their own paths to follow. Lukas Dhont, director of "Close," is listed as a co-producer. Screened in the Competition section of the 78th Cannes Film Festival in 2025, it won the Best Screenplay Award and the Ecumenical Jury Prize.
