ワンダンス

Wandance

2026 · 監督: 草場尚也 · 126分 · ドラマ

講談社「月刊アフタヌーン」にて連載され、2025年のテレビアニメ化も話題を呼んだ珈琲による漫画「ワンダンス」を実写化した青春ダンス映画。 吃音症が原因で目立つことが苦手なカボこと小谷花木（こたにかぼく）は、自分の気持ちを抑え周囲に合わせながら生きてきた。そんなある日、深夜に人目を気にせずダンスに没頭するワンダこと湾田光莉（わんだひかり）と出会ったカボは、心から楽しそうに踊る彼女の姿に衝撃を受ける。心を掻き立てられるままにダンスの世界に飛び込んだカボは、ワンダへの憧れとその才能への嫉妬、なりたい自分への迷いなど葛藤を繰り返しながらも、自分だけのダンスを目指して挑戦を続けていく。 ボーイズグループ「&TEAM」のJOがカボ役で映画初主演を務め、カボの運命を変えるワンダ役で池端杏慈、カボが飛び込むダンスチームのカリスマダンサー・宮尾恩役で山口綺羅（Girls²）、カボの才能を見抜きダンスバトルへの挑戦を後押しする先輩・厳島伊折役でRAN（MAZZEL）、カボたちのライバルとなる壁谷楽役で現役DリーガーのTSUKKI、カボたちの前に立ちはだかる実力派チームのリーダー・巧宇千役で高松アロハ（超特急）が共演。「雪子 a.k.a.」の草場尚也監督がメガホンをとり、「恋は光」の小林啓一が脚本を担った。

A youth dance film adapted into live action from the manga "Wandance" by Coffee, serialized in Kodansha's "Monthly Afternoon" and also a hot topic when it received a TV anime adaptation in 2025. Kabo, real name Kaboku Kotani, who struggles with stuttering and dislikes standing out, has lived while suppressing his own feelings and conforming to those around him. One day, Kabo encounters Wanda, real name Hikari Wanda, who immerses herself in dance late at night without caring about others' eyes, and is shocked by the sight of her dancing with what looks like genuine joy. Drawn by his stirred heart, Kabo dives into the world of dance, and while repeating conflicts such as admiration for Wanda, jealousy of her talent, and uncertainty about who he wants to become, he continues to take on challenges aiming for a dance that is uniquely his own. JO of the boy group "&TEAM" makes his film debut starring as Kabo, with Anji Ikehata as Wanda, who changes Kabo's fate, Kira Yamaguchi (Girls²) as Miyao On, the charismatic dancer of the dance team Kabo joins, RAN (MAZZEL) as Itsuru Itsukushima, the senior who recognizes Kabo's talent and pushes him to challenge dance battles, TSUKKI, an active D-League dancer, as Gaku Kabeya, the rival of Kabo and his friends, and Aloha Takamatsu (Cho Tokkyu) as Chi Kō, the leader of a highly skilled team standing in the way of Kabo and his friends. The film is directed by Naoya Kusaba of "Yukiko a.k.a." and written by Keiichi Kobayashi of "Love Is Light."
