Academy Award nominee for Best Foreign Language Film. An emotional human drama that inspired a Hollywood remake. Jacob, a Danish man dedicated to helping orphans in India, receives an offer for a massive donation from Jørgen, a businessman back in his home country. Returning home, Jacob finalizes the deal with Jørgen, only to be invited to his daughter's wedding. There, an unexpected reunion leads him to a shocking discovery... Mads plays this role: Jacob, a naive idealist with a secret who runs an orphanage in India. Beloved by the children, he is a gentle soul who evokes a sense of tenderness, yet he harbors a hidden past...
