霧のごとく

A Foggy Tale

2025 · 監督: チェン・ユーシュン · 134分 · アドベンチャー / ドラマ

Directed by Taiwan's Yu-Hsun Chen, known for "The Girl Who Leapt Through Time" and "The Tropical Fish," this is a story of hope and rebirth set against the backdrop of the "White Terror" era in 1950s Taiwan, when many citizens were arrested and executed on suspicion of being anti-government. In the 1950s, under martial law in Taiwan. A young girl named A-Yue, living in Chiayi, learns that her brother, captured as an anti-government element, has been executed in Taipei. With a small amount of money and her brother's keepsake watch in hand, she heads to Taipei alone, only to find that a hefty fee is required to claim his body. At her wit's end, she is tricked by a suspicious man and nearly sold into a brothel, but is rescued by a rickshaw puller named Zhao Gongdao. Originally from Guangdong, China, he came to Taiwan as a soldier for the Nationalist army and has been unable to return home, living hand-to-mouth ever since. Having lost his army comrades to the White Terror and finding no direction in life, Gongdao is moved by A-Yue's determination and decides to lend her a hand. Caitlin Fang ("A Girl from America") plays A-Yue, Will Or ("The Staggering Girl") plays Zhao Gongdao, and popular Taiwanese singer and actor 9m88 co-stars. The film won four awards, including Best Feature Film, at the 62nd Golden Horse Awards in 2025.
