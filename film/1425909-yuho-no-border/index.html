遊歩：ノーボーダー

Yuho No Border

2026 · 監督: Asano Yumiko · 104分 · ドキュメンタリー

A documentary tracing the journey and present life of Yuho Asaka, one of Japan's pioneers of peer counseling. Born in Fukushima City, Fukushima Prefecture in 1956, Yuho Asaka has a condition characterized by brittle bones. From a young age, she was forced to give up on learning and grew angry at a society that discriminates and excludes based on disability and gender. Always searching for her place in the world, Asaka connected with the cerebral palsy activist group "Fukushima Prefecture Aoi Shiba no Kai" and, drawing on the independent living movement and feminism she encountered while studying in the U.S., helped establish Japan's first independent living center. She also participated in international conferences and helped spark changes to the Eugenic Protection Law. The film follows Asaka's relentless pursuit of freedom, while also depicting her sister Aiko, who was caught in the turbulent relationship between their father and Yuho; her daughter Sora, who was born with the same condition and works at an overseas institute advocating for the rights of people with disabilities while attending graduate school; and the young caregivers who share daily life with her in a free-spirited relationship. Yumiko Asano, producer of "What Could Have Been?," makes her directorial debut, with Tomoaki Fujino, director of that same film, handling cinematography, editing, and production.
