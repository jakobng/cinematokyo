Das Versprechen - Architekt BV Doshi

The Promise, Architect BV Doshi

2023 · 監督: Jan Schmidt-Garre · 90分 · ドキュメンタリー

A documentary about the world-renowned Indian architect Balkrishna Doshi. Balkrishna Vithaldas Doshi, who in 2018 became the first Indian to win the Pritzker Prize, considered the Nobel Prize of architecture, passed away in 2023 at the age of 95. Beginning his career under Le Corbusier in Paris in the 1950s, he oversaw the implementation of Corbusier's projects in Ahmedabad, western India, and later collaborated with Louis Kahn after India's independence. Influenced by Corbusier and Kahn, Doshi incorporated sustainable and ecological ideas into his architecture early on, establishing a unique style that fused modernism with Indian tradition, climate, and spirituality. He aimed for architecture that contributes to society and the environment, a 'people's architecture' rooted in daily life. This film delves into Doshi's inner world in his final years, visiting the buildings he collaborated on with Corbusier and Kahn, as well as his own works, as he discusses his architectural philosophy, creative process, and career spanning 70 years. Screened as part of the special program 'Le Corbusier and Doshi: Indian Modernism' (starting May 1, 2026, at Eurospace in Tokyo and other theaters nationwide).
