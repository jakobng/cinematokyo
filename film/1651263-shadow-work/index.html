シャドウワーク

Shadow Work

2026 · 監督: 吉野竜平 · 129分

日常的に暴力を振るう夫から逃げてきた紀子。絶望の中、入院先の看護師・路子に救われ、配偶者や親から暴力を受けた女性たちを匿うシェルター〈おうち〉に身を寄せる。そこでは暮らしのすべてが「持ち回り」というルールで成り立っていた。穏やかで守られた日々を取り戻していく紀子だが、少しずつ違和感を覚え始める。やがて〈おうち〉が“交換殺人”によって女性たちを解放している事実を知る。残るか去るか、選択を迫られる紀子。一方、とある事件を追う刑事・薫もまた夫からの暴力に苦しんでいた。疑われることのない夫・晋一と追い詰められていく現実のなかで、薫は事件の先に〈おうち〉の存在へと辿り着く。そして晋一の存在によって、その均衡が崩されていく──。

A crime suspense film adaptation of the novel of the same name by Hiroshi Sano, winner of the 66th Edogawa Ranpo Award, starring Riho Yoshioka and Nao in a double lead, set in a shelter where women suffering violence from spouses or parents take refuge. Directed and written by Ryuhei Yoshino of "You Are Younger Than Those Guys Forever." Noriko, who escaped her husband's violence, loses her place and is saved by nurse Michiko, who brings her to "Ouchi," a shelter in a Japanese-style house on the outskirts of Miura Beach. At that place, which shelters women who have suffered violence from spouses or parents, everything in daily life—cooking, cleaning, and so on—is governed by a rule of "taking turns." Noriko regains a peaceful, protected daily life, but gradually begins to feel something off about that routine. And finally, she learns the truth of the organized crime being carried out at "Ouchi." Meanwhile, Detective Kaoru, pursuing a certain case, also begins to see the existence of "Ouchi" emerge in her investigation. Riho Yoshioka, who won the Best Supporting Actress award at the 48th Japan Academy Awards for "Identity," plays Noriko, and Nao, who won the Best Actress award at the 2025 Tokyo Drama Award, plays Detective Kaoru. Jun Fubuki, Jun Miho, Wakana Sakai, First Summer Uika, Emi Satsuki, and other colorful faces play the women of "Ouchi," while Takumi Kitamura, Yoshitaka Hara, and Naoya Shimizu play the figures who threaten the women.
