Eleanor the Great

2025 · 監督: スカーレット・ヨハンソン · 98分 · ドラマ

親友を亡くしたエレノアは、出掛け先でホロコースト生存者会に迷い込み…。嘘をきっかけに出会ったパワフルな老婦人と母を亡くした学生の友情を描く。

Actress Scarlett Johansson makes her feature directorial debut with this lighthearted drama about the friendship between a powerful elderly woman and a motherless student, sparked by a lie, set against the backdrop of her own Jewish cultural roots. Based on a script by first-time feature screenwriter Tory Kamen, drawing on her own grandmother's and family history, Johansson directs with humor and wit. Eleanor, an elderly woman living in New York, feels a void after losing her longtime friend Bessie. One day, intending to attend a tea party, she accidentally wanders into a support group for Holocaust survivors. There, she improvises a story about Bessie's life, recounting her Holocaust memories. Inspired by the dramatic story, journalism student Nina approaches Eleanor, and a new friendship blossoms. But Eleanor's lie takes on a life of its own, sparking a major uproar. June Squibb ("Thelma") plays the unconventional Eleanor, and Erin Kellyman ("Solo: A Star Wars Story") plays Nina. Rita Zohar, herself a Holocaust survivor, plays Eleanor's late friend Bessie.
