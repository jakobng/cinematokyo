魚影の群れ

The Catch

1983 · 監督: 相米慎二 · 141分 · ドラマ

「セーラー服と機関銃」の相米慎二監督が、吉村昭の短編小説を映画化した人間ドラマ。青森県下北半島最北端の漁港を舞台に、マグロの一本釣りに命をかける男たちと、寡黙だが情熱的な女たちを描き出す。 青森県の漁港・大間のマグロ漁師である小浜房次郎は、娘のトキ子と2人で暮らしている。ある日、房次郎はトキ子が結婚を考えているという青年・依田俊一に会いに行く。マグロ漁に命をかけて生きてきた房次郎は、簡単に漁師になりたいと話す俊一に腹を立て、彼を拒絶する。漁師を目指すため大間に引っ越してきた俊一は、漁を教えてほしいと房次郎のもとに通い続け、ついに彼の船に乗せてもらうことになるが……。 命がけでマグロとの戦いに向き合う漁師・房次郎を緒形拳が熱演し、1984年・第7回日本アカデミー賞で最優秀主演男優賞を受賞。房次郎の娘・トキ子を夏目雅子、トキ子の恋人・俊一を佐藤浩市が演じた。脚本は「ツィゴイネルワイゼン」「セーラー服と機関銃」の田中陽造。2026年・第39回東京国際映画祭「日本映画クラシックス」部門上映作品。

A human drama directed by Shinji Somai (Sailor Suit and Machine Gun), adapted from a short story by Akira Yoshimura. Set in a fishing port at the northern tip of the Shimokita Peninsula in Aomori Prefecture, it depicts men who stake their lives on pole-and-line tuna fishing and the taciturn yet passionate women around them. Fusajiro Kohama, a tuna fisherman in the Aomori port of Oma, lives with his daughter Tokiko. One day, Fusajiro goes to meet Shunichi Yoda, a young man Tokiko is considering marrying. Fusajiro, who has lived devoting his life to tuna fishing, grows angry at Shunichi for casually saying he wants to become a fisherman and rejects him. Shunichi, who moved to Oma to pursue a career as a fisherman, keeps visiting Fusajiro to ask him to teach him fishing, and is finally allowed aboard his boat—but then... Ken Ogata gives a passionate performance as Fusajiro, the fisherman who faces tuna at the risk of his life, winning the Best Actor award at the 7th Japan Academy Awards in 1984. Masako Natsume plays Fusajiro's daughter Tokiko, and Hiroshi Sato plays Tokiko's lover Shunichi. The screenplay is by Yozo Tanaka (Zigeunerweisen, Sailor Suit and Machine Gun). Screened in the Japan Film Classics section at the 39th Tokyo International Film Festival in 2026.
