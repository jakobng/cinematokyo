時給三〇〇円の死神

Part-time Death Angel

2026 · 監督: 酒井麻衣 · 119分 · ファンタジー / ドラマ

西畑大吾（なにわ男子）と福本莉子が主演を務め、小説家・藤まるが2017年に発表したライトノベル「時給三〇〇円の死神」を実写映画化したヒューマンドラマ。 不祥事を起こした父親の逮捕、両親の離婚など、不幸続きの人生に絶望する大学生の佐倉真司は、同級生の花森雪希から「死神」のアルバイトに誘われる。仕事内容は、この世をさまよう死者の心残りに寄り添い、願いを果たす手伝いをして、あの世へ送り出すこと。雇用期間は6カ月で、依頼内容は郵便受けに投函され、時給はたったの300円だが、最後までやり遂げれば特別賞与として願いをひとつかなえてもらえるという。花森とバディを組み、さまざまな死者たちの想いを見届けるなかで、佐倉の止まっていた人生は少しずつ動きはじめる。 佐倉の元恋人・朝月静香役で渋谷凪咲、訳ありの死者の少年役で齋藤潤、物語の鍵を握る死者の少女・四宮夕役で泉谷星奈、出産時に命を落とした広岡加奈役で佐津川愛美、佐倉の父・鷹矢役で山口馬木也、謎めいたホームレス・雨野役で小澤征悦が共演。「ストロベリームーン　余命半年の恋」「美しい彼 eternal」の酒井麻衣監督がメガホンをとり、「366日」の福田果歩が脚本を手がけた。

A human drama starring Daigo Nishihata (Naniwa Danshi) and Riko Fukumoto, adapted into a live-action film from the light novel The Reaper with an Hourly Wage of 300 Yen, published in 2017 by novelist Fujimaru. Sakura Shinji, a university student despairing over a life of misfortune including his father's arrest for a scandal and his parents' divorce, is invited by classmate Yukiki Hanamori to take a "Reaper" part-time job. The work involves accompanying the lingering regrets of the dead who wander this world, helping fulfill their wishes, and sending them on to the next world. The employment period is six months, requests are delivered to a mailbox, and the hourly wage is only 300 yen, but if he sees it through to the end, he will be granted one wish as a special bonus. Paired with Hanamori, as he witnesses the feelings of various dead people, Sakura's stalled life begins to move forward little by little. Co-starring are Nagisa Shibuya as Sakura's ex-girlfriend Shizuka Asatsuki, Jun Saito as a dead boy with circumstances, Seina Izumiya as the dead girl Yū Shinomiya who holds the key to the story, Manami Satsukawa as Kana Hirooka who lost her life during childbirth, Makaya Yamaguchi as Sakura's father Takaya, and Seiyetsu Ozawa as the mysterious homeless man Ame no. Directed by Mai Sakai (Strawberry Moon: A Love with Half a Year to Live, Utsukushii Kare eternal), with screenplay by Kaho Fukuda (366 Days).
