西遊記 女人国の戦い

The Monkey King 3

2018 · 監督: 鄭保瑞 · 114分 · アドベンチャー / ファンタジー / アクション / ファミリー

Continuing their westward journey to India, Sanzo and his companions Son Goku, Cho Hakkai, and Sha Gojyo are attacked by a giant fish while traveling by boat on a river. The four are swept through a mysterious hole in the sky and land in a strange land—the "Land of Women," where only women live. The queen and Sanzo develop mutual affection. Soon, the four are sentenced to execution under the land's laws, but they manage to escape thanks to the queen's intervention. However, they have no idea how to return from the Land of Women to their original world...
