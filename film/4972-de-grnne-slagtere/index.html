フレッシュ・デリ

De grønne slagtere

2003 · 監督: Anders Thomas Jensen · 100分 · コメディ

. 日本では長らく視聴困難となっていた ファン待望のハートフル・カニバリズム・ドラマ スヴェンはビャンと共に精肉店を立ち上げるが、冷凍庫から閉じ込められた電気工の死体が出てきてしまう。困り果てたスヴェンは死体の肉で作ったマリネを客に提供するが、なんとそれが大ヒット。店の評判を失いたくない彼は次々と人を手にかけていき...。 . マッツが演じるのはこんな役！ 常に脂汗を額にたぎらせた、精肉店を営む嫌味な町の嫌われ者スヴェン イメージを覆す衝撃的なヘアスタイルで、新たな魅力が開眼?! . . .

Popular Danish actor Mads Mikkelsen stars in this Danish black comedy about the struggles of two men who find themselves serving human meat to their customers. After opening their own butcher shop, Sven and Bjarne discover the body of an electrician who had been accidentally locked in their freezer. Through a bizarre turn of events, Sven prepares a marinade using the corpse’s meat and serves it to a customer, only for the dish to become an unexpected sensation. Desperate to maintain the shop’s reputation, he begins killing people one after another to procure more "ingredients." Mikkelsen delivers an eccentric performance as the town outcast Sven, sporting a unique hairstyle, while Nikolaj Lie Kaas of the "Department Q" series plays his partner, Bjarne. The film is written and directed by Anders Thomas Jensen, who has collaborated with Mikkelsen on numerous works, including "Adam's Apples." In Japan, the film will receive its first theatrical release in November 2025 as part of the special screening project "The Treasure of Scandinavia: Mads Mikkelsen's 60th Anniversary Festival."
