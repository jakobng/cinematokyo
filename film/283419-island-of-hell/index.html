獄門島（1977）

Island of Hell

1977 · 監督: 市川崑 · 141分 · 謎 / ホラー

Carrying the will of his friend Sentaro Kito, Kindaichi Kosuke descends upon "Gokumon Island." On the night of Sentaro Kito's wake—who died worrying about the fate of his three younger stepsisters—the first incident occurs. The youngest daughter, Hanako, is found murdered, hung upside down from an old plum tree. Next, the second sister, Yukie, is discovered dead inside the temple bell. And finally, even the eldest daughter, Tsukiyo, is left in a cruel state of death. Who is doing this, and for what purpose? As Kindaichi pursues this bizarre case, he encounters the mystery of three haiku left behind by Sentaro's father, Kaemon. What is the meaning behind these copycat serial murders—and who is the true culprit?
