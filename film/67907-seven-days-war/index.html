ぼくらの七日間戦争

Seven Days War

1988 · 監督: 菅原浩志 · 94分 · アクション / コメディ / ドラマ

At Aoba Junior High, where strict discipline is enforced, eleven boys and girls rise up in pursuit of freedom. They bring food and barricade themselves in an abandoned factory, while the teachers try to disband them strictly according to school rules. The students fend off the teachers using various means, but... A lighthearted youth drama based on the original novel by So Munetari. Screened simultaneously with "Flower's Asuka Gang!".
