汝、星のごとく

You, Like a Star

2026 · 監督: 藤井道人 · 135分 · ドラマ / ロマンス

第20回本屋大賞を受賞した凪良ゆうによる恋愛小説「汝、星のごとく」を、「正体」「新聞記者」の藤井道人監督が実写映画化。同じく凪良ゆう原作の「流浪の月」でも共演した横浜流星と広瀬すずが主演を務め、風光明媚な瀬戸内の島で出会った男女の15年にわたる愛と人生の選択を描く。 美しい海と空が広がる、瀬戸内のとある島。京都から島の高校に転校してきた青埜櫂には、漫画原作者になるという夢がある。一方、この島で生まれ育ち母と暮らす井上暁海は、大好きな刺繍を仕事にしたいと願っていた。ともに複雑な家庭の事情を抱える孤独な2人は惹かれ合い、恋に落ちる。やがて櫂は夢を追って上京するが、暁海は家族を支えるため島に残ることになる。離ればなれになった2人の間には、物理的な距離のみならず、心のすれ違いが生じはじめる。いくつもの岐路が無情にも突きつけられていくなか、2人は15年の恋愛の末に、ある決断をくだす。 櫂の母・ほのか役で尾野真千子、暁海の母・志穂役で木村佳乃、暁海が憧れを抱く刺繍作家・林瞳子役で石田ゆり子、櫂と暁海を見守り続ける高校教師・北原草介役で長谷川博己、櫂の相棒として共に夢を追う久住尚人役で松村北斗、文芸編集者・二階堂絵理役で中村アン、漫画編集者・植木渋柿役で濱田岳が共演。「きのう何食べた？」シリーズの安達奈緒子が脚本を手がけ、ロックバンド「RADWIMPS」が主題歌を担当。

A live-action film adaptation of the romance novel "You, Like a Star" by Yui Nagira, which won the 20th Booksellers Award, directed by Michihito Fujii of "The Last 10 Years" and "The Journalist." Ryusei Yokohama and Suzu Hirose, who also co-starred in the Nagira-penned "The Moon" adaptation, star in this story depicting 15 years of love and life choices between a man and a woman who meet on a scenic island in the Seto Inland Sea. On a certain island in the Seto Inland Sea, surrounded by beautiful ocean and sky. Kai Aono, who transferred from Kyoto to the island's high school, dreams of becoming a manga author. Meanwhile, Akemi Inoue, born and raised on the island and living with her mother, wishes to make her beloved embroidery her profession. The two lonely individuals, both carrying complicated family circumstances, are drawn to each other and fall in love. Eventually Kai moves to Tokyo to pursue his dream, but Akemi stays on the island to support her family. Between the two who are now apart, not only physical distance but also emotional rifts begin to emerge. As numerous crossroads are mercilessly thrust before them, the two make a certain decision at the end of their 15-year romance. The cast includes Machiko Ono as Kai's mother Honoka, Yoshino Kimura as Akemi's mother Shiho, Yuriko Ishida as Rinko Hayashi, the embroidery artist Akemi admires, Hiroki Hasegawa as Kisuke Kitahara, the high school teacher who watches over Kai and Akemi, Hokuto Matsumura as Naoto Kuzumi, who pursues dreams alongside Kai as his partner, Anne Nakamura as literary editor Eri Nikaido, and Gaku Hamada as manga editor Shibukaki Ueki. Naoko Adachi of the "What Did You Eat Yesterday?" series writes the screenplay, and the rock band RADWIMPS handles the theme song.
