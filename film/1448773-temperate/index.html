温帯の君へ

Temperate

2025 · 監督: ラブストーリー · 75分 · ドラマ

. “分かり合えなさ”を抱えた全ての人に贈る、 新たな時代の青春恋愛映画。 . 初長編監督作『(Instrumental)』で劇場公開を果たした新鋭・宮坂一輝が放つ最新作は、現代に生きる若者たちの葛藤を描くラブストーリー。 キャストには、本作で第18回田辺・弁慶映画祭俳優賞をトリプル受賞した山下諒、二田絢乃、さいとうなりをはじめ、吉田晴登、関口滉人ら気鋭の若手俳優が集結。 「今」という時代を映す、青春恋愛映画の新たな傑作が誕生した。 . .

A new era youth romance film for all those who carry the burden of 'not being able to understand each other.' The latest work from up-and-coming director Kazuki Miyasaka, who made his theatrical feature debut with '(Instrumental),' is a love story depicting the conflicts of young people living in the modern age. The cast includes Ryo Yamashita, Ayano Nita, and Nari Saito, who each won the Actor Award at the 18th Tanabe Benkei Film Festival for this film, as well as rising young actors such as Haruto Yoshida and Koto Sekiguchi. A new masterpiece of youth romance cinema that reflects the era of 'now' has been born.
