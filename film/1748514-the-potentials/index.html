The Potentials

2026 · 監督: 森本晶一 · 111分

森田剛が主演を務め、マンガの制作現場を舞台に万年アシスタントの中年男性の奮闘を軽やかにつづったヒューマンドラマ。 巨匠マンガ家のアシスタントを長年にわたり務めてきた、42歳の土屋輝一。マンガ家デビューした後輩や人気声優である別居中の妻に対してモヤモヤした思いを抱え、一緒に暮らす高校受験生の娘とも気持ちがすれ違ってばかりいる。そんな彼のもとに、あるマンガ企画が舞い込む。仕事への熱量不足を自覚する29歳の中堅編集者・潮田ユキと、マンガ家を志す猪突猛進な40歳の新人アシスタント・山田ぽてとと共に、マンガ制作に踏み出す土屋だったが……。 編集者・潮田ユキ役で森川葵、新人アシスタント・山田ぽてと役で川上友里、潮田の上司である編集部長・馬場明役で阿部寛、土屋の別居中の妻・莉花役で松本まりか、土屋がアシスタントを務める巨匠マンガ家・吉住はるを役で武田鉄矢、土屋の娘・ひかり役で早瀬憩が共演。監督は、是枝裕和監督作の助監督などを務めてきた森本晶一と、「泣く子はいねぇが」の佐藤快磨。是枝監督率いる映像制作者集団「分福」に所属する両監督が共同監督を務め、森本監督が自身の経験に着想を得て原案・オリジナル脚本を手がけた。

A human drama starring Go Morita that lightly depicts the struggles of a middle-aged man who has been a perpetual assistant in the manga production industry. Koichi Tsuchiya, 42, has spent years as an assistant to a master manga artist. He harbors complicated feelings toward a junior colleague who made his manga debut before him and toward his estranged wife, a popular voice actress, and his feelings constantly miss the mark with his high school exam-taking daughter who lives with him. Then a certain manga project lands in his lap. Alongside Yuki Shioda, a 29-year-old mid-level editor aware of her own lack of passion for the work, and Yamada Potato, a headstrong 40-year-old new assistant who dreams of becoming a manga artist, Tsuchiya sets out on manga production—but... Aoi Morikawa plays editor Yuki Shioda, Yuri Kawakami plays new assistant Yamada Potato, Hiroshi Abe plays Shioda's boss and editorial department head Akira Baba, Marika Matsumoto plays Tsuchiya's estranged wife Rika, Tetsuya Takeda plays the master manga artist Haruo Yoshizumi whom Tsuchiya assists, and Ikoi Hayase plays Tsuchiya's daughter Hikari. The directors are Shoichi Morimoto, who has served as assistant director on Hirokazu Kore-eda's films, and Kaishu Sato of "The Kids Are Fine." Both directors belong to the filmmaking collective "Bunbuku" led by Kore-eda, and serve as co-directors; Morimoto conceived the original story and wrote the original screenplay inspired by his own experiences.
