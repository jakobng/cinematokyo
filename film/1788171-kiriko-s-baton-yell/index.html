キリコのタクト YELL

Kiriko's Baton: YELL

2026 · 監督: 雑賀俊朗 · 128分 · ドラマ

第76回NHK全国学校音楽コンクール中学校の部課題曲として制作され、第51回日本レコード大賞で優秀作品賞を受賞した「いきものがかり」の楽曲「YELL」に着想を得て、「カノン」「レディ加賀」の雑賀俊朗監督が描く音楽ヒューマンドラマ。鈴木京香が、タクトを振ることで生徒たちを輝かせる「魔法」を持つと言われた伝説の高校音楽教師・原田貴理子を演じる。 小さな音楽出版社に勤める記者の北村翔は、雑誌「音楽ジャーナル」の特集企画「伝説の音楽教師」で、全国合唱コンクールに数々の優勝をもたらし「優勝請負人」と呼ばれた原田貴理子を取り上げることになる。しかし貴理子は突然教職を退き、消息を絶っていた。実は北村にとって貴理子はかつての恩師。当時の合唱仲間とともに記憶をたどりながら、北村は貴理子が姿を消した理由に迫っていく。 伝説の音楽教師と呼ばれる原田貴理子役を演じた鈴木京香は、指揮やピアノのレッスンを重ねて撮影に臨んだ。そのほか、北村役を藤原大祐、北村に取材を命じる編集長役を小泉今日子が演じ、榊原郁恵、小西博之、宅麻伸、名取裕子らが共演。監督・脚本は「カノン」「レディ加賀」の雑賀俊朗。「YELL」のミュージックビデオの撮影地でもある神奈川県厚木市の市制70周年記念事業のひとつとして制作され、厚木市内でオールロケを敢行した。

A music human drama directed by Toshiro Saiga of "Canon" and "Lady Kaga," inspired by the song "YELL" by Ikimonogakari, which was produced as the set piece for the junior high school division of the 76th NHK National School Music Competition and won the Excellent Work Award at the 51st Japan Record Awards. Kyoka Suzuki plays Kiyoko Harada, a legendary high school music teacher said to possess the "magic" to make students shine by waving her baton. Sho Kitamura, a journalist working at a small music publisher, is assigned to cover Kiyoko Harada—known as the "victory contractor" who led numerous national chorus competition championships—for a feature in the magazine "Music Journal" titled "Legendary Music Teachers." However, Kiyoko suddenly retired from teaching and disappeared. In fact, Kiyoko was once Kitamura's beloved teacher. Together with his former chorusmates, Kitamura retraces his memories and closes in on the reason Kiyoko vanished. Kyoka Suzuki, who played the legendary music teacher Kiyoko Harada, underwent conducting and piano lessons in preparation for filming. Also appearing are Daisuke Fujiwara as Kitamura, Kyoko Koizumi as the editor-in-chief who assigns Kitamura the story, and Ikue Sakakibara, Hiroyuki Konishi, Shin Takuma, and Yuko Natori in supporting roles. Directed and written by Toshiro Saiga of "Canon" and "Lady Kaga." Produced as part of the 70th anniversary commemorative projects for Atsugi City, Kanagawa Prefecture—also the filming location for the "YELL" music video—the film was shot entirely on location in Atsugi.
