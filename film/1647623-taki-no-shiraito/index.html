滝の白糸（1952）

Taki no Shiraito

1952 · 監督: 野淵昶 · 98分

A shinpa play adapted from a novel by Kyoka Izumi, brought to the screen by director Akira Nobuchi, who came from a theater background. Screenwriter Yoshikata Yoda, known for his work with director Kenji Mizoguchi on melodramas, reworked the material, and Machiko Kyo gives a powerful performance as a woman devoted to love. Shiraito (Machiko Kyo), a water-trick performer who leads her troupe in a male-dominated world, falls for Murayama (Masayuki Mori), a struggling student who aspires to become a lawyer. To keep her promise to send him money for his studies, she even takes work with the loathsome Toragoro (Kozaburo Ramon) and earns money desperately, only to face Murayama ironically after he successfully passes his civil service exam.
