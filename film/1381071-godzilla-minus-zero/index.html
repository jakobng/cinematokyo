ゴジラ－0.0

Godzilla Minus Zero

2026 · 監督: 山崎貴 · 135分 · サイエンスフィクション / ホラー / アクション

第96回アカデミー賞でアジア映画として初の視覚効果賞を受賞するなど、世界的に大きな話題を集めた「ゴジラ－1.0」の続編。山崎貴が引き続き監督・脚本・VFXを担い、前作から2年後の世界を舞台に、再び姿を現したゴジラとの新たな戦いを描き出す。 戦争によって「無（ゼロ）」となった日本を、ゴジラが「負（マイナス）」に叩き落としてから2年。もがき苦しみながらも復興を遂げ、ようやく取り戻したはずの日常に、再びゴジラが襲いかかる。人々はこの戦いを終わらせるべく、総力を結集して新たな作戦に乗り出す。 キャストは、前作でゴジラと壮絶な死闘を繰り広げた主人公・敷島浩一役の神木隆之介、ゴジラに襲われながらも奇跡的に一命をとりとめたヒロイン・敷島典子役の浜辺美波をはじめ、山田裕貴、佐々木蔵之介、安藤サクラ、吉岡秀隆ら前作の顔ぶれが再集結。さらに新たなメンバーとして、気象台から大型災害対策事務局に出向した天才気象予報官・青山真琴役で長澤まさみ、戦争で心に深い傷を負った生物学者・村上寛治役で田中泯、医師・緒方役で國村隼が出演。

The sequel to "Godzilla Minus One," which became a global phenomenon and made history as the first Asian film to win the Academy Award for Best Visual Effects at the 96th Academy Awards. Takashi Yamazaki returns as director, screenwriter, and VFX supervisor, depicting a new battle against Godzilla, who reappears two years after the events of the previous film. Two years after Godzilla reduced Japan—already brought to "zero" by war—to "minus," the nation has struggled and rebuilt, and just as people have finally reclaimed their everyday lives, Godzilla strikes again. To end this battle, the people unite all their strength for a new operation. The cast reunites familiar faces from the previous film, including Ryunosuke Kamiki as Koichi Shikishima, the protagonist who fought a desperate life-or-death struggle with Godzilla, and Minami Hamabe as Noriko Shikishima, the heroine who miraculously survived Godzilla's attack, alongside Yuki Yamada, Kuranosuke Sasaki, Sakura Ando, and Hidetaka Yoshioka. New additions include Masami Nagasawa as Makoto Aoyama, a genius meteorologist transferred from the meteorological observatory to the Large-Scale Disaster Countermeasures Bureau; Min Tanaka as Kanji Murakami, a biologist deeply scarred by the war; and Jun Kunimura as Dr. Ogata.
