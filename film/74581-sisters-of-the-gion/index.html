祇園の姉妹

Sisters of the Gion

1936 · 監督: 溝口健二 · 69分 · ドラマ

かいせつ 京都・祇園の花街で生きる対照的な芸妓姉妹を透徹した眼差しで描いた傑作。古風で情に流され易い姉と現代的でドライな妹は…。従順な姉に反発し男を手玉に取ろうとする妹を演じた、当時19歳の山田の貫禄から目が離せない。

A commentary: A masterpiece that gazes with a penetrating eye at two contrasting geisha sisters living in Kyoto's Gion pleasure district. The older sister, old-fashioned and easily swayed by emotion, and the younger sister, modern and pragmatic... You can't take your eyes off the 19-year-old Yamada, who plays the younger sister rebelling against her submissive elder sibling and trying to toy with men, exuding a remarkable presence.
