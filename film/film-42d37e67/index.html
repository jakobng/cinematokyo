ハナのサンタ　奇跡の贈り物

2026 · 監督: 川畑秀雄 · 96分

声を失った少女と、余命宣告を受けた青年の出会いと交流を描いたヒューマンドラマ。 別々の児童養護施設で育ったトシオとマリナは、飲食店のアルバイトで出会って恋に落ち、やがて結婚して娘ハナが誕生する。しかしハナの8歳の誕生日、トシオはケーキを買いに行く途中で交通事故に遭い、帰らぬ人となってしまう。電話越しに父の最後の声を聞いたハナは、ショックのあまり声を失う。一方、幼い頃に父を亡くした29歳の青年ジントは、医師から余命1年を宣告される。死ぬ場所を探すうちに事故に遭い記憶を失った彼は、千葉の森の奥にある「再生の館」に辿り着き、ハナとその母マリナに出会う。止まっていた3人の時間は再び動き出し、クリスマスの日にある奇跡が起こる。 企画・原作・脚本・音楽・監督を川畑秀雄が担い、俳優・千葉真一への感謝を込めて本作を構想。千葉真一の旧邸宅である千葉県君津市の千葉真一記念館で撮影をおこなった。余命わずかな青年ジントを福地展成、声を失った少女ハナを新井笑琳、ハナの母マリナを宝生真里奈が演じる。声優の松本梨香が主題歌を担当。

A human drama depicting the encounter and interaction between a girl who has lost her voice and a young man who has been told he has limited time to live. Toshio and Marina, who grew up in separate children's foster homes, meet while working part-time at a restaurant, fall in love, and eventually marry and have a daughter, Hana. However, on Hana's eighth birthday, Toshio is hit by a car while on his way to buy a cake and does not return. Hana, who heard her father's last voice over the phone, loses her voice from the shock. Meanwhile, Jinto, a 29-year-old man who lost his father at a young age, is told by a doctor that he has one year left to live. While searching for a place to die, he is in an accident and loses his memory, eventually reaching the "Hall of Regeneration" deep in a forest in Chiba, where he meets Hana and her mother Marina. Time that had stopped for the three begins to move again, and a miracle occurs on Christmas Day. Hideо Kawabata handled planning, original story, screenplay, music, and direction, conceiving this work out of gratitude to actor Shinichi Chiba. Filming took place at the Shinichi Chiba Memorial Hall in Kimitsu, Chiba Prefecture, Chiba's former residence. Fukuoka Nobunari plays Jinto, the young man with little time left; Arai Emirin plays Hana, the girl who lost her voice; and Hosho Marina plays Hana's mother Marina. Voice actress Rica Matsumoto performs the theme song.
