The fourth feature film from Saint Laurent Productions, established by SAINT LAURENT for film production. Directed and written by Jim Jarmusch, this highly anticipated work was selected for the official selection of the 2025 Venice International Film Festival, screened in competition, and won the top prize, the Golden Lion. It has also drawn attention for its star-studded cast including Tom Waits, Adam Driver, Mayim Bialik, Charlotte Rampling, Cate Blanchett, Vicky Krieps, Sarah Greene, India Moore, Luca Sabat, and Françoise Lebrun. The story unfolds in America, Dublin, and Paris. This three-part anthology film depicts, with Jarmusch's signature lightness and abundant humor, the bothersome yet precious time shared by an estranged parent and child, sisters reuniting after a long separation, and siblings in each of the three cities. "Father": set in the northeastern United States, the long-overdue time between an estranged father and son; "Mother": set in Dublin, the time between a mother and her daughters at the annual formal family gathering; "Sister Brother": set in Paris, the time when siblings who lost their parents share memories. Set in different countries, each depicts the moment when people bound by blood face each other again after a period of blank space between them.
