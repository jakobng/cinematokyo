セックス・ピストルズ　破壊の設計図

2025 · 監督: アンドレ・レリス · 96分

—1970s London, England— The birth of 'punk rock'— 'That's so punk!'— Its spirit began here. — The Sex Pistols — surely everyone has heard that name at least once. They were a 'punk' rock band that suddenly appeared in late 1970s London, completely rejecting the stagnant rock scene. Today, 'that's so punk' is used in everyday conversation, but that spirit was born here. The original bassist and ill-fated man, Glen Matlock, tells the whole story in this shocking alternative narrative. Featuring live footage and interviews from the era, as well as rare live performances by Glen Matlock. This masterpiece documentary depicts the interplay between truth and falsehood in show business, and between artistic expression and commercialism, while posing the universal question: 'Who is the true winner in life?'
