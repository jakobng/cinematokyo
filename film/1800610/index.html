あなたの息子ひき出します！

「神様のカルテ」「法廷遊戯」の深川栄洋監督が企画・脚本・監督を務め、着想から10年の歳月を経て自主制作で完成させたヒューマンドラマ。引きこもり状態にある人々の自立支援に従事する女性の奮闘を通し、現代社会の歪みと人間の再生を描き出す。 都会から離れた街に幼い息子と2人で暮らす小林典子は、社会から孤立している引きこもりの人々を支援する活動をしている。つながりを必要とする彼らと一心に向き合う典子のもとには、他に行くあてのない人や相談が集まってくる。ある日、いつものように依頼を受けた彼女は、長い間引きこもったままの男性・三浦雅彦と出会う。当初は一切の関係を拒む雅彦だったが、典子の地道な働きかけで少しずつ周囲と言葉を交わしはじめる。このまま順調に外の世界へとつなげることができるかに思えたが、雅彦には決して引き出したくない過去があった。 深川監督の妻で映画制作のパートナーでもある俳優・宮澤美保が主人公・典子を演じ、お笑いタレントの平野ノラ、モデル・俳優ののせりん、オーディションで見いだされた子役・小野光南翔が共演。さらに、吉行和子、生瀬勝久、かとうかず子、江口のりこ、柄本明、天海祐希、大森南朋ら豪華キャストが脇を固める。シンガーソングライターの石崎ひゅーいが主題歌「手品」を書き下ろし、俳優としても出演。

A human drama planned, written, and directed by Eiji Fukagawa of "The Chef of South Polar" and "Courtroom Play," completed as an independent production after ten years from its conception. Through the struggles of a woman engaged in supporting the independence of people living in social withdrawal, it depicts the distortions of modern society and human rebirth. Noriko Kobayashi, living with her young son in a town far from the city, works to support hikikomori people who are isolated from society. People with nowhere else to go and those seeking advice gather around Noriko, who faces them wholeheartedly in their need for connection. One day, after receiving a request as usual, she meets Masahiko Miura, a man who has been withdrawn for a long time. At first, Masahiko rejects any relationship, but through Noriko's steady efforts, he gradually begins to exchange words with those around him. Just when it seems he might be able to connect with the outside world smoothly, Masahiko has a past he absolutely does not want brought out. Miho Miyazawa, an actor who is also director Fukagawa's wife and filmmaking partner, plays the protagonist Noriko, with comedian Nobora Hirano, model and actor Nose Rin, and child actor Konan Ono, discovered through an audition, appearing alongside her. The supporting cast is rounded out by Kazuko Yoshiyuki, Katsuhisa Namase, Kazuko Kato, Noriko Eguchi, Akira Emoto, Yuki Amami, and Nao Omori. Singer-songwriter Hyui Ishizaki wrote the theme song "Tejina" and also appears as an actor.
