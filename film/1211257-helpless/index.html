Helpless

2013 · 監督: 青山真治 · 75分 · ホラー

高校卒業を目前に控えた健次は、仮出所で帰って来た片腕のやくざ・安男に再会した。組の仲間が、組長が死んでしまって組はつぶれたと話しても、安男は一向に信じようとしないばかりかその仲間を撃ち殺してしまう。自分だけにつらい思いをさせて出所したら雲隠れか、と怒った安男は、健次に妹のユリと大切なショルダーバッグを預け、組長の消息を追いかける。ユリを預かった健次は安男との待ち合わせ場所である山中のドライブインに行くが…。

Kenji, on the verge of graduating from high school, is reunited with Yasuo, a one-armed yakuza who has returned on parole. Even when his gang comrades tell him that the gang has disbanded because the boss died, Yasuo refuses to believe it and even shoots one of the comrades dead. Angry that he was the only one who suffered and that they went into hiding after his release, Yasuo entrusts his sister Yuri and an important shoulder bag to Kenji, and goes off in search of the boss's whereabouts. Kenji, now looking after Yuri, heads to a roadside diner in the mountains—the meeting place with Yasuo—but...
