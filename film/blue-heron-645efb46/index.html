Blue Heron

2025 · 監督: ソフィー・ロムヴァリ · 91分

世界各地で絶賛と共感！ 新鋭ソフィー・ロムヴァリが描く、ある家族を巡る 今年もっとも胸を締めつける物語

In the summer of the late 1990s, eight-year-old Sasha moves to Vancouver Island, Canada, with her family of Hungarian immigrants. Surrounded by rich nature, Sasha gradually finds her place while spending time with her older brothers and new friends. However, her eldest brother Jeremy repeatedly engages in 'problem behavior,' and the family is reaching its limit. As the parents consult experts about Jeremy's problems, they are forced to make a difficult choice. Twenty years later, Sasha, now a film director, begins tracing her family's past with a camera in hand—
