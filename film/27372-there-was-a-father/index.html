There Was a Father

1942 · 監督: 小津安二郎 · 88分 · ドラマ

Shuhei Horikawa, a middle school teacher in Kanazawa, lost his wife and lived with his elementary school-aged son Ryohei. However, when a student drowned during a school trip, Shuhei felt responsible and resigned from the school. He returned to his hometown in Shinshu, but left Ryohei, now a middle school student, in a dormitory and went alone to work at a factory in Tokyo. Ryohei, who graduated from Tokyo Imperial University and became a teacher, reunites with his father at a hot spring inn after a long time and tells him he wants to quit teaching and live together. However, Shuhei admonishes his son, saying, "You must not abandon your current work."
