台風クラブ

Typhoon Club

1985 · 監督: 相米慎二 · 115分 · ドラマ

A masterpiece of youth cinema that vividly depicts middle school students who fall into a state of turmoil, showing inexplicable emotional excitement as a typhoon approaches. Directed by Shinji Somai, known for "Sailor Suit and Machine Gun" and "Shonben Rider." In a local city near Tokyo, five students sneak into their private middle school's pool at night to swim. They notice a male student who arrived earlier and play a prank on him, but it goes too far, nearly drowning him. The next morning, the news reports a typhoon approaching. From then on, something gradually begins to go wrong among the students...
