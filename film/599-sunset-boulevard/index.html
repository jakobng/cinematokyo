Sunset Boulevard

1950 · 監督: ビリー・ワイルダー · 110分 · ドラマ

A film that synchronizes two eras: the silent era of Hollywood, the movie capital, and the 1950s. Billy Wilder stated his intention: 'I wanted to make a film about Hollywood, set in Hollywood.' Unsuccessful screenwriter Gillis stumbles into the home of Norma, a great silent film star. She longs for a comeback to the glamorous world and asks Gillis to polish her script, but... Gloria Swanson's tense performance as Norma is superb. Cecil B. DeMille, known for directing 'Cleopatra' and others, appears as himself.
