うそがほんとに　ほんとがうそに

2026 · 監督: 金聖雄 · 104分

ドキュメンタリー映画監督・金聖雄が、「SAYAMA みえない手錠をはずすまで」「袴田巖　夢の間の世の中」「獄友」「オレの記念日」に続いて撮りあげた「冤罪」シリーズ第5弾。無罪が確定した袴田事件の袴田巖さんと姉・ひで子さん、いまだ再審裁判の扉が開かない狭山事件の石川一雄さんと妻・早智子さんの15年を記録し、ふたつの事件の「うそ」と「ほんと」に迫る。 2024年9月26日に無罪判決を受けた袴田巖さんと、第3次再審請求中の2025年3月11日に無念のまま亡くなった石川一雄さん。石川さん夫妻との出会いから冤罪をテーマにした映画を撮り続けてきた金監督が、権力にあらがい前を向いて歩み続けるふたつの家族にカメラを向ける。理不尽を強いられながらも、おおらかな心で生きる彼らのささやかな日常を通して、日本の司法の残酷さを浮き彫りにしていく。 俳優の北村有起哉が語りを担当。

The fifth installment in documentary filmmaker Kim Seong-woong's 'false accusation' series, following SAYAMA: Until the Invisible Handcuffs Are Removed, Iwao Hakamada: The World Between Dreams, Prison Friends, and My Anniversary. It records 15 years of Iwao Hakamada, acquitted in the Hakamada case, and his sister Hideko, as well as Kazuo Ishikawa, whose retrial for the Sayama case has yet to begin, and his wife Sachiko, exploring the 'lies' and 'truths' of the two cases. Iwao Hakamada received a not-guilty verdict on September 26, 2024, while Kazuo Ishikawa passed away in regret on March 11, 2025, during his third request for retrial. Director Kim, who has continued making films on the theme of false accusations since meeting the Ishikawas, turns his camera on two families who keep moving forward in defiance of authority. Through the modest daily lives of these people who live with generous hearts despite being forced to endure injustice, he highlights the cruelty of Japan's judicial system. Actor Yukiya Kitamura provides the narration.
