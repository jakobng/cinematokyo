夜のまにまに

2023 · 監督: 磯部鉄平 · 116分

大阪を舞台に、真面目で物憂げな青年と型破りで行動的な女性が織りなす恋の行方を描いた青春恋愛映画。 恋人の咲と別れたばかりのフリーター・新平は、映画館で出会った佳純と意気投合し、夜の街で一緒に過ごす。その後、新平の職場であるカフェで佳純が働き始める。突然の再会に驚く新平だったが、佳純から恋人の浮気調査を手伝ってほしいと頼まれて探偵の真似ごとをする羽目に。新平は強引な佳純に振り回されながらも、徐々に彼女にひかれていく。 「きみはいい子」「ちはやふる」シリーズの加部亜門が新平役、「猫は逃げた」の山本奈衣瑠が佳純役でそれぞれ主演を務め、「卍」の黒住尚生、「最後の審判」の永瀬未留、「凪の憂鬱」の辻凪子、「東京遭難」の木原勝利が脇を固める。監督は「コーンフレーク」「凪の憂鬱」の磯部鉄平。

Set in Osaka, this youth romance depicts the relationship between a serious, melancholic young man and an unconventional, proactive woman. Shinpei, a part-time worker who has just broken up with his girlfriend Saki, hits it off with Kasumi after meeting her at a movie theater, and they spend the night wandering the city together. Later, Kasumi unexpectedly begins working at the same cafe as Shinpei. Surprised by the reunion, Shinpei ends up playing amateur detective when Kasumi asks for his help investigating her boyfriend’s infidelity. Despite being swept up in Kasumi’s assertive pace, Shinpei gradually finds himself drawn to her. Amon Kabe (*Being Good*, *Chihayafuru* series) stars as Shinpei, with Nairu Yamamoto (*The Cat Escaped*) playing Kasumi. The supporting cast includes Hisao Kurozumi (*Manji*), Miru Nagase (*The Last Judgment*), Nagiko Tsuji (*Nagi’s Blues*), and Masatoshi Kihara (*Tokyo Disaster*). The film is directed by Teppei Isobe (*Cornflakes*, *Nagi’s Blues*).
