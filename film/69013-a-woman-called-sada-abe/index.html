A Woman Called Sada Abe

1975 · 監督: 田中登 · 76分 · 犯罪 / ドラマ / ロマンス

"The outside light bothers me" "His smell is soaked into the futon too..." Sadame closes the storm shutters to keep Kichizō's scent from escaping. How long has it been since the two of them were alone in the small room of the teahouse? Their union seeks ever more unknown pleasures. Sadame, choking Kichizō, says happily, "Kichi-san is twitching inside me." But when Kichizō, whose health is beginning to fail, suggests returning home for a while, Sadame's pure love for him becomes directly linked to pure desire. "I won't let anyone else touch you anymore. No one..."
