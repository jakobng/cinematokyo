うるわしの宵の月

In the Clear Moonlit Dusk

2026 · 監督: 竹村謙太郎 · 119分 · ロマンス / ドラマ

講談社「デザート」連載のやまもり三香による人気少女漫画「うるわしの宵の月」を実写映画化した青春ラブストーリー。「今夜、世界からこの恋が消えても」の道枝駿佑が主演、「カラダ探し THE LAST NIGHT」の安斉星来がヒロインを務め、同じ高校でそれぞれ「王子」と呼ばれる男女が織りなす初恋の行方を描く。 高校1年生の少女・滝口宵は中性的な美しさとクールな振る舞いで「王子」と呼ばれているが、内心ではその完璧な王子像に複雑な思いを抱いていた。そんなある日、高校の先輩で同じく「王子」と呼ばれる完璧なルックスの男子生徒・市村琥珀と出会う。それぞれ自分の内面と周囲からのギャップに葛藤する2人は次第に惹かれ合い、“自分らしさ”と向き合いながら少しずつ距離を縮めていく。 琥珀と宵の関係を揺り動かす3人目の「王子」こと大路拓人役で水沢林太郎、琥珀の幼なじみ・よしこ役で与田祐希、宵の父・誠一役で藤木直人、琥珀と宵の恋の行方を見守る友人役で星乃夢奈、鈴川紗由、大倉空人、阿佐辰美が共演。「交換ウソ日記」の竹村謙太郎監督がメガホンをとり、「翔んで埼玉」「かぐや様は告らせたい　天才たちの恋愛頭脳戦」など数々のヒット作を手がけてきた徳永友一が脚本を担当。

A live-action film adaptation of the popular shoujo manga "Uruwashi no Yoi no Tsuki" by Miyuki Yamamori, serialized in Kodansha's "Dessert." This youth love story stars Shunsuke Michieda ("Even If This Love Disappears from the World Tonight") and features Seira Anzai ("Karada Sagashi THE LAST NIGHT") as the heroine, depicting the first love between a boy and girl at the same high school who are each called "Prince." First-year high school student Yoi Takiguchi is called a "Prince" for her androgynous beauty and cool demeanor, but internally she harbors complicated feelings about that perfect prince image. One day, she meets Kohaku Ichimura, an upperclassman at her school who is also called a "Prince" for his flawless looks. The two, each struggling with the gap between their inner selves and how others perceive them, gradually find themselves drawn to each other, closing the distance between them as they confront what it means to be true to themselves. The cast includes Rintaro Mizusawa as Takuto Oji, the third "Prince" who shakes up the relationship between Kohaku and Yoi; Yuki Yoda as Yoshiko, Kohaku's childhood friend; Naohito Fujiki as Yoi's father Seiichi; and friends who watch over the romance between Kohaku and Yoi played by Yuna Hoshino, Saya Suzukawa, Sorato Okura, and Tatsumi Asa. Directed by Kentaro Takemura ("Koukai Uso Nikki"), with screenplay by Yuichi Tokunaga, who has worked on numerous hits including "Tonde Saitama" and "Kaguya-sama: Love Is War."
