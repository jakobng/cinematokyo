ロルナの祈り

Lorna's Silence

2008 · 監督: 尚-皮耶·達頓 · 105分 · 犯罪 / ドラマ

The Dardenne brothers, directors of 'The Son' and 'The Child,' craft a moving human love story centered on an immigrant woman who enters a sham marriage to obtain citizenship. With a bold structure, they suspensefully portray the conflict that arises when unexpected feelings develop within the false marriage, and the protagonist's growing desperation as she is cornered by her inescapable ties to the criminal underworld. Starring newcomer Arta Dobroshi, with Jérémie Renier from 'The Child.' Lorna, a woman from Albania, comes to Belgium. Dreaming of opening a bar with her fellow countryman lover, she enters a sham marriage with a drug-addicted young man, Claudy, arranged by a shady broker, Fabio, to gain citizenship. Initially finding him annoying, Lorna gradually develops special feelings for Claudy as he struggles to quit drugs and earnestly seeks her affection...
