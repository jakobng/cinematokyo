ゾンビ1/2 〜Right Side of the Living Dead〜

2026 · 監督: 太田えりか · 67分

人類はゾンビの脅威に勝利し、世界に残ったゾンビはただ一人。 その“最後のゾンビ”新宮龍馬（しんぐう・りょうま）こそ、本作の主人公。 彼はゾンビに噛まれても理性を保ち続ける“半ゾンビ”で、社会に受け入れられながら会社員として平凡な日々を送っていた。 しかしある日、インターンの羽座目（うざめ）くんを思わず噛んでしまったことで、ゾンビパンデミックが勃発！ 事態は憧れの先輩・える子、ゾンビ研究者の洞吹田（ほらふきだ）博士を巻き込み、「癒しの半ゾンビハウス」で暗躍する謎の男・飛衣輪（ひいりんぐ）も現れ・・・。

"Zombie 1/2: Right Side of the Living Dead" is the second production in the M CINEMA series presented by MMJ. Featuring an introverted office worker who has become the world's only zombie as its protagonist, 24-year-old rookie director and zombie geek Erika Ota delivers a brand-new sensation in zombie horror-comedy like you've never seen before! Humanity has triumphed over the zombie threat, leaving only one zombie left in the world. This "last zombie" is the protagonist of our story. Having maintained his sanity even after being bitten, he lives an ordinary life as an office worker, accepted by society as a "half-zombie." However, after he instinctively bites an intern named Uzame, a new zombie pandemic erupts! Dragging in his crush, Eruko-senpai, and the zombie researcher Dr. Horafukida, a mysterious man named Hiiringu—who operates in the shadows of a facility called the "Healing Half-Zombie House"—also appears... Can our hero save the world and everyone in it—!?
