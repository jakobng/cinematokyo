ヨーロッパ企画20周年ツアー 第37回公演「サマータイムマシン・ブルース」

サマータイムマシン・ブルース

2018 · 107分

Directed by Katsuyuki Motohiro of the "Bayside Shakedown" series, this youth sci-fi comedy is a film adaptation of the popular stage play by the theater group Europe Kikaku. Set during a sweltering summer vacation, the story follows a group of college students who trigger a series of chaotic events when they use a time machine to retrieve an air conditioner remote before it broke. In the clubroom of a university's Sci-Fi Research Society, five male students and two female members of the photography club are languishing in the oppressive heat. Their air conditioner's remote control broke the previous day, leaving them to suffer through the summer. Suddenly, a time machine appears before them. What starts as a joke turns into a discovery: the machine is real. Caught up in the excitement, three members decide to travel back to yesterday to fetch the working remote. However, a junior from the year 2030 suddenly appears before those left behind, leading the group to realize the grave dangers of altering the past... The screenplay was penned by Europe Kikaku’s own Makoto Ueda. Eita, who rose to prominence in "Water Boys," makes his film-starring debut as Komoto, the student at the heart of the commotion. Juri Ueno, known for "Swing Girls" and "Turtles Are Surprisingly Fast Swimmers," plays Shibata, the photography club member and object of Komoto's affection.
