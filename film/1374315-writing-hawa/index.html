ハワの手習い

Writing Hawa

2025 · 監督: Najiba Noori · 84分 · ドキュメンタリー

教育の機会を奪われ、13歳で結婚させられた母・ハワに娘である監督がカメラを向ける。歳を重ね、多くの挫折を経てもなお、彼女は諦めなかった…。

A documentary directed by Najiba Nouri, an Afghan female journalist, who turns the camera on her mother Hawa, who was denied education as a child and forced into marriage at age 13. Hawa lives in Kabul, the capital of Afghanistan, caring for her husband, who is 30 years older and has dementia. Hawa was only 13 when she was forced into marriage; her father kept her from education, and her mother gave her no advice about childbirth. After giving birth to six children, she ensured her daughters received education so they wouldn't follow the same path. Her second daughter, Najiba, lives independently as a journalist. Determined to support her mother, Najiba points the camera at her and listens to her complaints about her husband, secret love, and 'things she wanted to do.' Hawa starts a folk craft business and learns to read and write, but just as her modest dreams begin to take shape, her granddaughter Zahra, who lives with her eldest daughter's ex-husband, flees from her domineering father. Hawa takes in her granddaughter and learns to read and write with her, but Taliban attacks and occupation advance daily, putting Zahra and the family sheltering her in mortal danger. When the Taliban regained power in August 2021, moves to suppress press freedom intensified, and Najiba entrusted the camera to her brother Ali and left her homeland. The film won the Citizens' Prize at the Yamagata International Documentary Film Festival 2025 and numerous awards at film festivals worldwide.
