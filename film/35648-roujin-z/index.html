老人Z

Roujin Z

1991 · 監督: 北久保弘之 · 80分 · アニメーション / ドラマ / サイエンスフィクション

In the near future, to cope with an aging society, the Ministry of Health and Welfare develops a sixth-generation computer with self-replicating functions, the elderly care machine 'Unit Z001.' However, this computer absorbs the elderly selected as samples and goes on a rampage, causing a huge uproar involving volunteer female college students and the media.
