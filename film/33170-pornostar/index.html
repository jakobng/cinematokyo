ポルノスター

Pornostar

1998 · 監督: 豊田利晃 · 98分 · 犯罪 / ドラマ

Kamijo struggles to scrape together tribute money for the yakuza while harboring ambitions of one day surpassing them. Then, a man named Koya appears—a man who kills yakuza without so much as a flicker of expression. Koya fills a sports bag with knives and goes around stabbing anyone who calls himself a yakuza, one after another.
