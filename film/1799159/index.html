愛し合ってるかい？ 忌野清志郎が教えてくれた

2026 · 監督: 相原裕美 · 124分

愛と反骨を胸に日本の音楽シーンに変革をもたらした忌野清志郎の軌跡をたどり、その比類なき表現の真髄に迫ったドキュメンタリー。 ロックバンド「RCサクセション」のメンバーとして1970年にデビューし、「雨あがりの夜空に」「スローバラード」「い・け・な・いルージュマジック」など多くの名曲を世に送り出した忌野清志郎。その圧倒的なライブパフォーマンスでも数々の伝説を生み、日本のロック史に多大な功績を残した。1991年のRCサクセション活動休止後も、ソロや「THE TIMERS」での音楽活動に加え、映画・ドラマへの出演、絵本の執筆、さらにはサイクリストなど、多岐にわたる表現活動を展開。常に時代と向き合いながら、生涯にわたって走り続けた。 本作では、膨大なアーカイブから厳選されたライブ映像やレコーディング風景、社会への鋭いまなざしや世間を騒がせた数々の出来事など、忌野清志郎の唯一無二のパフォーマンスと“信念を貫く姿勢”を多面的に掘り下げるとともに、清志郎自らが創作の源泉について語る姿などを通して、ひとりの人間としての実像を浮かび上がらせていく。監督は「トノバン　音楽家　加藤和彦とその時代」の相原裕美。

A documentary that traces the footsteps of Kiyoshiro Imawano, who brought transformation to the Japanese music scene with love and rebellious spirit in his heart, and delves into the essence of his unparalleled expression. Debuting in 1970 as a member of the rock band RC Succession, Kiyoshiro Imawano produced numerous famous songs including "Ame Agari no Yozora ni," "Slow Ballad," and "Ikenai Rouge Magic." He also created numerous legends through his overwhelming live performances and left a tremendous legacy in Japanese rock history. Even after RC Succession went on hiatus in 1991, he pursued diverse expressive activities including solo work and music with THE TIMERS, as well as appearances in films and dramas, writing picture books, and even cycling. Always facing the times, he kept running throughout his life. This film, through carefully selected live footage and recording scenes from a vast archive, his sharp gaze toward society and the many incidents that caused public uproar, explores multi-faceted aspects of Imawano's one-of-a-kind performances and his "stance of sticking to his convictions," while also bringing to light his true image as a human being through scenes of Imawano himself speaking about the sources of his creativity. The director is Hiromi Aihara, known for "Tonovan: Musician Kazuhiko Kato and His Era."
