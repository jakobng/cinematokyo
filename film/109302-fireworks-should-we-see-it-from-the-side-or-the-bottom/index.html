打ち上げ花火、下から見るか？横から見るか？（1993）

Fireworks, Should We See It from the Side or the Bottom?

1995 · 監督: 岩井俊二 · 50分 · ロマンス / ドラマ

小学校最後の夏休み、プールの掃除当番ため登校していた典道、祐介、なずな。祐介は、典道に水泳の競争で勝った方がなずなに告白するという話をもちかける。「Love Letter」「花とアリス」の岩井俊二が当初TVドラマ用に制作した本作は、TVドラマとして初の日本映画監督協会新人賞を受賞する快挙を成し遂げ、のちに同監督作品「Undo」とともに劇場公開された。主演は奥菜恵、山崎裕太。

Summer vacation. Norimichi, an elementary school student, is invited by his classmate Nazuna—who is set to transfer schools after the summer due to her parents' divorce—to go to the fireworks festival with her that night. However, Nazuna is planning to run away from home, and Norimichi finds himself being dragged along. Meanwhile, Norimichi's friends, including Junichi, have decided to go to the festival to see for themselves whether fireworks look round or flat when viewed from the side. Nazuna remains determined to carry out her plan to run away.
