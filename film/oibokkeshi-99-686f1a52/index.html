OiBokkeShi 99歳の看板俳優

. 超高齢社会の「老い・ぼけ・死」の未来にやさしい光を灯す。 . “99歳の看板俳優”として生き切った男の、最初で最後の主演映画。 「老い・ぼけ・死」。誰もが向き合うテーマに、“演劇”と“介護”という領域を越えて活動を続けてきた劇団「老いと演劇」OiBokkeShi(おいぼっけし)。俳優で介護福祉士の菅原直樹と、認知症の妻を介護していた当時88歳の岡田忠雄との出会いから、その活動は生まれた。約10年にわたる二人の活動を記録し続けてきた膨大な映像資料を、ドキュメンタリー映画として構成。老老介護に悩んでいた一人の高齢者が、演劇に出会い、生きがいを見いだし、最期まで舞台にこだわり続けた軌跡を映し出す。演劇で「いま、ここ」を共に楽しむ二人の姿は、超高齢社会の「老い・ぼけ・死」の未来にやさしい光を灯す。 . .

Lighting a gentle light on the future of "aging, dementia, and death" in a super-aging society. The first and last starring film of a man who lived out his life as a "99-year-old star actor." "Aging, dementia, and death." The theater company "OiBokkeShi" has continued its activities across the fields of "theater" and "caregiving" on themes everyone must face. Its activities were born from the encounter between actor and care worker Naoki Sugawara and Tadao Okada, then 88 years old, who was caring for his wife with dementia. The vast amount of footage that continued to record the two's activities over about ten years has been assembled into a documentary film. It depicts the trajectory of one elderly person troubled by elderly-to-elderly caregiving who encountered theater, found a reason to live, and remained committed to the stage until the very end. The sight of the two enjoying "here and now" together through theater lights a gentle light on the future of "aging, dementia, and death" in a super-aging society.
