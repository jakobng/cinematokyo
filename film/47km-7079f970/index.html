自画像 47KMの死

2015 · 監督: ジャン・モンチー · 70分

中国湖北省の農村に通い続け、村の人々と自身の変化を記録してきたジャン・モンチーの「自画像 47KM」シリーズ第5作。村と彼女を結ぶ存在だった祖父の死をきっかけに、監督が村を覆う無数の死と向き合った記録となった。 2010年、大学を出たばかりのジャン・モンチー監督は、老人たちへのインタビューのため父の故郷である湖北省の村「47KM村」を訪れ、以来毎年通い続けてきた。だが、村と自分をつないでいた祖父が亡くなり、彼女は村にとどまる意義を見失う。死とはどういうものなのか。監督は老人たちにカメラの前で語ってもらう。農薬を飲んで自ら命を絶った老人、批判闘争でつるし上げられて死んだ人々。穏やかに見えた日常は、実は無数の死に囲まれていた。それでも監督は、村の若者たちに希望を見いだそうとする。 シリーズは第1作「自画像 47KM」から第10作「自画像 47KM 2020」まで10本を数え、監督は2020年から村に家を建てて暮らしている。日本初上映となる本作は、全10本をまとめて上映する「ジャン・モンチー監督 47KMシリーズ特集上映」（2026年11月7～20日＝ポレポレ東中野）で公開。

The fifth installment in Zhang Mengqi's "Self-Portrait 47KM" series, in which she has continued to visit a rural village in Hubei Province, China, recording the changes in the villagers and herself. Triggered by the death of her grandfather—who was the bond connecting her to the village—the film becomes a record of the director confronting the countless deaths that cover the village. In 2010, fresh out of university, director Zhang Mengqi visited the village in Hubei Province called "47KM Village," her father's hometown, to interview the elderly, and has continued to visit every year since. But when her grandfather, who connected her to the village, dies, she loses sight of the meaning of staying in the village. What is death? The director has the elderly speak before the camera. An old man who took his own life by drinking pesticide; people who were denounced and died in struggle sessions. The everyday life that seemed peaceful was in fact surrounded by countless deaths. Even so, the director tries to find hope in the village's young people. The series counts ten films, from the first "Self-Portrait 47KM" to the tenth "Self-Portrait 47KM 2020," and the director has lived in a house built in the village since 2020. This work, receiving its first screening in Japan, is shown at the "Director Zhang Mengqi 47KM Series Special Screening" (November 7–20, 2026, at Pole Pole Higashi-Nakano), which screens all ten films together.
