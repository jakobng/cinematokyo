オルモと夏のステレオ

Olmo

2025 · 監督: フェルナンド・エムケ · 84分 · コメディ / ドラマ

1970年代のアメリカ・ニューメキシコ州を舞台に、14歳の少年オルモと、寝たきりの父に翻弄される家族の最低で最高な1日を、ほろ苦いユーモアを交えながら温かいまなざしでつづった青春ドラマ。 1979年、ニューメキシコ州。14歳のオルモは親友ミゲルと遊びに行きたかったが、母も姉も出かけてしまい、病気で寝たきりの父の世話をしなければならなくなる。そんな中、想いを寄せている幼馴染のニーナからパーティに誘われた彼は、どうにか役目を逃れようと奮闘する。そんなオルモの小さな企みは、すれ違いを抱える家族も巻き込んで、混乱に満ちた忘れられない一夜となっていく。 主人公オルモ役には、オーディションで選ばれたアイバン・ウタパを抜擢。監督・脚本は、2004年の長編デビュー作「ダック・シーズン」で注目を集めたメキシコ出身のフェルナンド・エインビッケ。製作には「ニューオーダー」のミシェル・フランコ監督、製作総指揮にはブラッド・ピットが名を連ねる。2025年・第75回ベルリン国際映画祭パノラマ部門に出品。

Plan B Entertainment, the production company behind numerous Academy Award-winning films, has produced many acclaimed and award-winning works including "The Big Short," "If Beale Street Could Talk," "Beautiful Boy," "Nickel Boys," "Mickey 17," and "F1." "Olmo and the Summer Stereo," born from Plan B Entertainment teaming up with director Michel Franco, winner of the Silver Lion at the Venice International Film Festival for "New Order," depicts the "worst and best day" of the López family—fourteen-year-old Olmo and his bedridden father—with bittersweet humor and a warm gaze. Screened in the Panorama section of the 75th Berlin International Film Festival, the film has drawn critical acclaim: "The film we need right now" — Deadline; "A single day's events etched forever in the heart" — The Moveable Fest; "A new masterpiece of coming-of-age cinema" — IndieWire. 1979, New Mexico, USA. Fourteen-year-old Olmo is stuck. He would rather be hanging out with his best friend Miguel, but today he has been tasked with caring for his sick, bedridden father. Then his crush and childhood friend Nina invites him to a party, and he struggles to somehow escape his duties. Before long, his little scheme draws in a family full of misunderstandings, and Olmo steps into an unforgettable night filled with mischief and chaos—
