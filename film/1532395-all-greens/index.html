万事快調〈オール・グリーンズ〉

All Greens

2026 · 監督: 児山隆 · 119分 · コメディ / ドラマ

A film adaptation of the coming-of-age novel "All Greens" by Namiki Dō, who was a 21-year-old university student at the time and won the 28th Matsumoto Seichō Prize by unanimous decision, generating significant buzz. Park Soo-mi dreams of becoming a rapper but cannot find her place at school or at home. Yaguchi Miruku is the ace of the track team, sociable, and near the top of the school hierarchy, yet harbors problems at home and loves movies. Iwakuma Mako is a cynical, sharp-tongued character who relies on her beloved manga as the foundation of her identity. In a rural town with no future in sight, these three high schoolers living gloomy days conclude that the only way to achieve their dreams and escape this town is to strike it rich, so they form a club called "All Greens" and begin a certain forbidden extracurricular activity... Minami Sara, who drew attention for NHK taiga dramas "The 13 Lords of the Shogun" and "Dear Radiance" as well as the film "Even If You Don't Love Me," plays Park Soo-mi, while Deguchi Natsuki of "Akabane Honeko's Bodyguard" plays the other protagonist, Yaguchi Miruku. Yoshida Mizuki of "Look Back" takes on the role of Iwakuma Mako, who forms "All Greens" alongside the two. The director and screenwriter is Koyama Riki of "Let's Meet at Sarugaku-cho."
