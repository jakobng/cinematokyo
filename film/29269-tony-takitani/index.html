トニー滝谷

Tony Takitani

2004 · 監督: 市川準 · 77分 · ドラマ

村上春樹の同名短編小説を、「トキワ荘の青春」「つぐみ」の市川準監督がイッセー尾形と宮沢りえの共演で映画化したラブストーリー。 トロンボーン奏者の息子として生まれたトニー滝谷は、生後すぐに母親を亡くし、孤独を抱えたまま成長する。デザイン会社勤務を経てイラストレーターとして独立した彼は、ある女性と恋に落ち結婚する。しかし幸せな日々もつかの間、妻は事故で他界してしまう。孤独を実感した彼は亡き妻によく似た女性をアシスタントとして雇い、買い物依存症だった妻が遺した大量の服を彼女に着てもらうことで妻の死に慣れようとするが……。 主人公・トニー滝谷とその父をイッセー尾形、トニー滝谷の妻と彼女に似た女性を宮沢りえが演じ、西島秀俊がナレーションを担当。坂本龍一が音楽を手がけた。2004年・第57回ロカルノ国際映画祭で審査員特別賞、国際批評家連盟賞、ヤング審査員賞を受賞。2026年には4Kリマスター版でリバイバル公開。

A love story adapted from Haruki Murakami's short story of the same name, directed by Jun Ichikawa of 'Tokiwa: The Manga Apartment' and 'Tsugumi,' starring Issey Ogata and Rie Miyazawa. Tony Takitani, born as the son of a trombonist, loses his mother shortly after birth and grows up carrying loneliness. After working at a design company, he becomes an independent illustrator, falls in love with a woman, and marries her. But their happy days are short-lived; his wife dies in an accident. Feeling his loneliness, he hires a woman who closely resembles his late wife as an assistant and tries to get used to her death by having the assistant wear the vast collection of clothes his shopaholic wife left behind... Issey Ogata plays the protagonist Tony Takitani and his father, Rie Miyazawa plays Tony's wife and the woman who resembles her, with Hidetoshi Nishijima providing narration. The music is composed by Ryuichi Sakamoto. The film won the Special Jury Prize, the FIPRESCI Prize, and the Youth Jury Award at the 57th Locarno International Film Festival in 2004. A 4K remastered version was re-released in 2026.
