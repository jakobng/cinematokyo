宮古島物語ふたたヴィラ かんかかりゃの願い

監督: 上西雄大

The third chapter of the "Miyako Island Story: Futatavilla" series, a human drama exploring the bonds and rebirth of people set against the backdrop of a small hotel on Okinawa's Miyako Island. Directed, written by, and starring Yudai Uanishi (known for "Hitokuzu" and "Goro's 400 Million Yen"), the film centers on a small villa called "Futatavilla," where it is said that staying will grant the reunion one truly desires. A couple who lost their daughter to bullying, a man pursued by debt and guilt, and friends burdened by their pasts—each carrying their own pain and prayers—are drawn to the island as if guided by the wind, eventually discovering a glimmer of new hope. The cast includes Katsuya Shibayama, chairman of Resort Life Co., Ltd., which operates hotels in Miyako Island and throughout Japan, alongside returning regulars Yudai Uanishi, Chieko Matsubara, Ai Furukawa, Mika Tokutake, and Ruby Moreno. Guest stars include Ryo Tamura and Tamotsu Ishibashi.
