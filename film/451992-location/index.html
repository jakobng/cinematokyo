ロケーション

Location

1984 · 監督: 森崎東 · 99分 · コメディ

Be-yan, a cinematographer for pink films who returns home in the morning after a grueling shoot, discovers his wife Natsuko, who attempted suicide, sleeping as if dead after taking sleeping pills. He hurriedly carries her to the hospital and somehow she pulls through, but that day was supposed to be the first day of location shooting for a new pink film starring her. Unable to do otherwise, he starts filming with a stand-in, but it doesn't go well, and Natsuko, who finally returns to the set, also says she can't do it anymore, throwing the shoot into utter chaos.
