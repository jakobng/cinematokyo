アダムの原罪

Adam's Sake

2025 · 監督: ローラ・ワンデル『Playground／校庭』 製作：ジャン=ピエール＆リュック・ダルデンヌ兄弟 · 78分 · ドラマ

骨折で入院した4歳児と、面会を制限された移民のシングルマザー。彼らに寄り添う看護師の葛藤と決断の一夜を映し出す、圧巻のヒューマンサスペンス。

The second feature from Belgian director Laura Wandel, who gained attention with her 2021 debut 'Playground', is a human suspense drama depicting one night's events surrounding a 4-year-old boy hospitalized in a pediatric ward and his mother, told from the perspective of a nurse trying to support them. Four-year-old Adam is admitted to a pediatric center with a broken left arm. Malnourished and underdeveloped, his bones are brittle. The court rules that Rebecca, an immigrant single mother, has not been providing Adam with proper nutrition and issues an order restricting her visits. Nurse manager Lucie, herself a single mother, tries to support Rebecca, who fears losing custody and being separated from her son. But Rebecca's reckless actions and pressure from superiors and colleagues push Lucie into a corner. Léa Drucker ('Close') plays nurse Lucie, torn between her desire to save the mother and child and the judicial system the hospital must follow. Anamaria Vartolomei ('Happening') plays isolated single mother Rebecca. The film is produced by Belgian masters Jean-Pierre and Luc Dardenne. It opened the Critics' Week at the 2025 Cannes Film Festival.
