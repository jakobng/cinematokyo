Raíz

Through Rocks and Clouds

2024 · 監督: Franco García Becerra · 83分 · ドラマ / ファミリー

A Peruvian film that, through the eyes of a boy living under the vast Andes Mountains caring for alpacas, depicts the prayers embedded in the lives of the people there and the conflict surrounding mining development. In a small Peruvian village surrounded by the majestic Andes Mountains over 4,000 meters high, 8-year-old Feliciano makes it his daily routine to take his alpacas grazing with his loyal dog Rambo. His family makes a living by shearing and selling the wool from the alpacas they care for. In the year before the FIFA World Cup, Feliciano dreams of the Peruvian national team qualifying and advancing to the finals in Russia, listening excitedly to radio broadcasts. He believes this peaceful daily life will last forever, but soon a conflict arises between the villagers and mining companies threatening nature, and one day a major incident engulfs the entire village. Around the same time, a young alpaca named Ronaldo, whom Feliciano especially adored, disappears. The director is Franco García Becerra, for whom this is his second feature film. The role of the protagonist Feliciano is played by Alberto Merma, selected through open auditions and workshops. The film won the Special Prize in the Generation Kplus section and the Special Jury Prize in the Ibero-American Film section at the 74th Berlin International Film Festival in 2024.
