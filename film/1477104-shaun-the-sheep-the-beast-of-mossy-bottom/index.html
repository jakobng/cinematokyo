The farm where Shaun and his friends live is busy preparing for Halloween. One day, the Farmer wrecks his precious pumpkin patch, and what a disaster! To fix the problem, Shaun develops a mysterious potion—and the situation spirals into an even bigger commotion. The Farmer goes missing, and a shaggy monster appears! Can Shaun and the flock find the Farmer and save the farm?!
