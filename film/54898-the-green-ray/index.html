Le Rayon vert

The Green Ray

1986 · 監督: Éric Rohmer · 99分 · ドラマ / ロマンス

The fifth film in Éric Rohmer's "Comedies and Proverbs" series. Through lively dialogue and beautiful imagery, it depicts the journey of a lonely woman seeking love and happiness on vacation, winning the Golden Lion at the Venice International Film Festival. Delphine, who works as a secretary, is looking forward to a vacation in Greece, but her female friend who was supposed to go with her cancels at the last minute. She goes to the South of France with other friends but can't fit in and returns to Paris alone. Later, visiting the beach at Biarritz alone, Delphine hears about the "green ray" from a Jules Verne novel—a flash of light visible only for an instant just before sunset. Starring Marie Rivière of "The Aviator's Wife" and "Autumn Tale."
