1970年代は台湾史における重要な転換点でした。十大建設による経済成長、戒厳令体制、中華民国と米国の断交、 フォークソング運動、そして西洋思想の流入。 私たちは当時の人々がどのように愛し、信じ、葛藤していたのかを誠実に描こうとしました。 理想と現実が絶えずぶつかり合う、純朴でありながら矛盾を抱えた時代。その背景を持つラブストーリーとして本作を完成させました。 A once-in-a-lifetime encounter. That youth changed an era. In 1977, Taiwan under martial law. Amid an era colored by the folk song boom, momentum toward democratization was quietly rising. In the Hakka town of Zhongli, Fan Xianying, who has dyslexia but burns with passion for photography, Li Hongguo, a university student who loves music, and Jin Haoxi, a taekwondo instructor who came from Korea, meet by fate. The three are then swept up in the turmoil of the Zhongli Incident, which became a turning point in Taiwan's democratization movement. Amid a shifting society, the young people cultivate love, friendship, and family bonds while searching for their own beliefs and future. This moving film vividly depicts the youth of young people who lived seeking freedom, set against the backdrop of the Zhongli Incident, which actually occurred in 1977 and became the origin of Taiwan's democratization movement. Produced by Taiwan's GrXStudio, which has worked on 'Gold Leaf,' 'Copycat Killer,' and the 'The Girl in Red' series. Korean star Jinyoung and Taiwan's new-generation stars Moon Lee and Edison Song co-star. Within the nostalgic scenery of 1970s Taiwan, a story of love and hope is woven.
