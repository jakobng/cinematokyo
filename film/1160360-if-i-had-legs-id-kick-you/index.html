ワンオペレーション

If I Had Legs I’d Kick You

2025 · 監督: メアリー・ブロンスタイン · 113分 · ドラマ

A24が現代人を蝕む"ワンオペ問題"に切り込む衝撃作！ 監督の実体験から生まれた、限界寸前の日常を描く衝撃作！ 『ミッドサマー』『エブリシング・エブリウェア・オール・アット・ワンス』など数々の話題作を送り出してきた気鋭スタジオのA24が製作。本来は複数人で担うべき育児や家事、仕事を一人で背負う"ワンオペレーション"という現代社会の構造的問題を、スリラー、心理ドラマ、ダークコメディが融合した予測不能な物語として描き、主人公の日常とアイデンティティーが崩壊していく悪夢を、観る者に追体験させる衝撃作。 家事と育児、セラピストの仕事に追われるリンダは、原因不明の病を抱える幼い娘の世話に追い詰められていた。顧客の患者への対応、娘の担当医からの叱責、出張中の口うるさい夫からの電話──休まる暇もない日々の中、自宅アパートの天井が突然崩落し、娘とともに海辺の格安モーテルへ移り住むことに。誰にも頼れない孤独のなかで限界を迎えた彼女は、思いもよらぬ事態へと飲み込まれていく──。

A shocking work from A24 that cuts into the "one-operation problem" afflicting modern people! A shocking work born from the director's own experiences, depicting a daily life on the verge of collapse! Produced by A24, the cutting-edge studio behind numerous talked-about films such as "Midsommar" and "Everything Everywhere All at Once." This shocking work depicts the structural problem of modern society known as "one-operation"—where childcare, housework, and work that should be shared among multiple people are shouldered by one person alone—as an unpredictable story blending thriller, psychological drama, and dark comedy, forcing viewers to relive the nightmare of the protagonist's daily life and identity collapsing. Linda, overwhelmed by housework, childcare, and her work as a therapist, is driven to the brink caring for her young daughter who suffers from an unexplained illness. Between responding to her client patients, being reprimanded by her daughter's doctor, and phone calls from her nagging husband who is away on business—with no moment of rest—the ceiling of her apartment suddenly collapses, and she moves with her daughter to a cheap seaside motel. Having reached her limit in isolation with no one to rely on, she is swallowed by an unforeseen situation—
