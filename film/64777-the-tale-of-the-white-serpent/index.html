白蛇伝

The Tale of the White Serpent

1958 · 監督: 、割れる結末の解釈と次回作を語る · 79分 · アニメーション / ファンタジー

日本初の長篇カラーアニメーション映画。アニメーションの海外配給に商機を見た東映社長・大川博のもと、白蛇の精と人間の青年の愛を描く中国の説話が題材に選ばれた。原画担当は森康二と大工原章というベテランふたりのみで、6万5千枚におよぶ動画を仕上げたスタッフの多くは経験1年未満の新人であった。本篇前に復元デモ（2分）を上映。

The white snake spirit Bai Niang, unable to forget Xu Xian who cared for her when she was young, appears before him as a beautiful woman. The fish spirit Shao Qing acts as their go-between. The two quickly fall in love. However, this soon comes to the attention of the monk Fa Hai, who, concerned for the life of the young man bewitched by a demon, decides to use his magical powers to obstruct their love. Yet their romance seems to be progressing smoothly, as they spend dreamlike moments strolling through the garden of a beautiful mansion in full bloom. Xu Xian's close friends, the panda and Mimi, also playfully roam the mansion with Shao Qing, eventually pulling down a decorative dragon from a pillar. The dragon moves on its own, capturing the panda, Mimi, and Shao Qing, flying through the night sky and dropping them into a treasure hall. Shao Qing gives the jewels she finds there to Xu Xian and Bai Niang, but this becomes the cause of their separation.
