すべて真夜中の恋人たち

All the Lovers in the Night

2026 · 監督: 岨手由貴子 · 139分 · ドラマ / ロマンス

小説家・川上未映子が2011年に発表した初の恋愛小説「すべて真夜中の恋人たち」を、「あのこは貴族」の岨手由貴子が監督・脚本を手がけて映画化したラブストーリー。「ケイコ　目を澄ませて」の岸井ゆきのが主演を務め、人との関わりを避けて生きてきた校閲者の女性が、年上の物理教師と交流を深めるなかで、自らの孤独や感情と向き合っていく姿を描く。 フリーの校閲者である入江冬子は、同業者が集まる飲み会に参加する。しかし人と関わることが苦手な彼女は、周囲の会話のペースについていくことができない。数少ない友人で仕事相手の石川聖に普段の様子を聞かれても、親しい人も趣味もない毎日について話すだけで、誕生日ですら真夜中にひとり散歩をして過ごすのだという。そんな彼女の日常は、年上の物理教師・三束との出会いをきっかけに、少しずつ変わりはじめる。 主人公・冬子を岸井ゆきの、物理教師・三束を浅野忠信、冬子の友人・聖を森田望智が演じ、深川麻衣、塩野瑛久が共演。冬子の独白である語りを松坂桃李が担当。2026年・第79回カンヌ国際映画祭「ある視点」部門出品。第39回東京国際映画祭「ガラ・セレクション」部門上映作品。

Fuyuko, a freelance proofreader who has lived her life avoiding involvement with others. Hijiri, an editor and one of Fuyuko's few friends. Mitsutsuka, a high school physics teacher who comes to know Fuyuko by chance. Fuyuko, who has lived carrying loneliness, begins to confront her feelings for the first time through meeting Mitsutsuka. She begins seeing Mitsutsuka once a week, but she still only knows his surname... Fuyuko is played by Yukino Kishii, who won the Japan Academy Prize for Best Actress for 'Keiko, Me wo Sumasete' among numerous other acting awards. Mitsutsuka is played by Tadanobu Asano, who won the Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actor for 'SHOGUN' and is active worldwide. Hijiri, Fuyuko's few friends, is played by Mio Morita, who will star in next year's NHK morning drama 'Meguru Swan.' Mai Fukagawa and Akihisa Shiono round out the supporting cast, while Tori Matsuzaka handles the narration of Fuyuko's monologue, supporting the story. The joy and despair of love. Secret feelings you want to share with someone, yet want to keep for yourself alone. What is depicted is the loneliness carried not only by the protagonist Fuyuko but by Mitsutsuka, Hijiri, and everyone. A contemporary yet universal theme is presented quietly and delicately. A supreme romance film has been born, one that makes you hold your breath as you watch the small ripples of the heart. She had nothing. And that was fine. Fuyuko Irie, a freelance proofreader, is attending a gathering of fellow professionals. Fuyuko, who never drinks and avoids interacting with people, cannot keep up with the pace of the surrounding conversation. When her few friends and work associate Hijiri Ishikawa asks her 'Fuyuko, what do you always do?', she can only talk about a daily life with no close people or hobbies. She says that even on her birthday, December 24th, she does nothing special and simply takes a walk at midnight as her yearly routine. Meanwhile, Hijiri, while saying she is much the same, reveals that she has multiple partners she shares meals and sex with. Fuyuko spends her empty days alone, while Hijiri fearlessly speaks her opinions to anyone. One day, Fuyuko happens to meet an older physics teacher named Mitsutsuka. From there, Fuyuko's daily life begins to change little by little...
