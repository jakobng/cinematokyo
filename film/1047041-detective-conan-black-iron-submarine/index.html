名探偵コナン 黒鉄の魚影（サブマリン）

Detective Conan: Black Iron Submarine

2023 · 監督: 立川譲 · 89分 · アニメーション / アクション / 犯罪 / 謎

Pacific Buoy, an ocean facility built off the coast of Hachijo Island near Tokyo to connect the security cameras of police forces around the world, is nearing full operation. Engineers from around the globe have gathered to connect it to the network under the jurisdiction of Europol, the European police organization. There, testing is also underway on a certain "new technology" that applies facial recognition systems. Meanwhile, Conan and the Detective Boys are visiting Hachijo Island for whale watching at Sonoko's invitation. Then Conan receives a call from Subaru Okiya (Shuichi Akai) informing him that a Europol employee has been killed by Gin in Germany. Sensing something ominous, Conan sneaks aboard the security boat carrying Inspector Hyoue Kuroda and other Metropolitan Police officials heading to guard Pacific Buoy, and infiltrates the facility. Inside, where preparations for the system's activation are steadily progressing, a female engineer is kidnapped by the Black Organization...! Furthermore, the USB memory stick she carried, containing certain information, falls into the organization's hands... A sinister whirring of propellers echoes beneath the sea. And a dark shadow creeps toward Haibara, who is staying on Hachijo Island...
