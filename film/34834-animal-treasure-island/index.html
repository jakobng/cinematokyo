どうぶつ宝島

Animal Treasure Island

1971 · 監督: 森康二 · 78分 · ファミリー / アニメーション / アドベンチャー / コメディ

Young Jim, who dreams of foreign lands and sailing, is left to mind the old inn Benbow Tavern in a port town tonight. But a suspicious cat captain shows up and leaves behind a mysterious map. It seems to indicate the location of treasure hidden by the famous pirate Captain Flint. Jim sets out to sea with his best friend, a mouse named Gran, in his homemade barrel ship, the Pioneer. Their destination: the legendary Treasure Island on the map! However, the Pioneer is sunk by a pirate ship, and the two are quickly taken prisoner. The pirate ship is named the Pork Sauté. Its captain, Silver, was once one of Flint's men, and he too has his eye on the treasure of Treasure Island. Jim and Gran escape and land on Pirate Island. But they are caught by Silver's gang, have the map stolen, and are sold off to slave traders. In the stone prison where they are confined, they find a girl named Cathy already there. It turns out Cathy is Captain Flint's granddaughter and is also looking for the map. Jim and Cathy initially clash but eventually open their hearts to each other and decide to cooperate and head for Treasure Island. Now, the curtain rises on a great adventure across the sea!
