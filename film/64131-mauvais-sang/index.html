汚れた血

MAUVAIS SANG

1986 · 監督: Leos Carax · 119分 · ロマンス / 犯罪 / ドラマ / サイエンスフィクション

In a Paris plagued by STBO—a mysterious disease contracted through loveless sex—Alex is a young man who has lived with a sense of nihilism and isolation since childhood. Even in the company of his lover, Lise, the emptiness remains. Stricken by unexplained abdominal pains and weary of his existence, he is consumed by the need to change his life. It is then that his estranged father is killed by a train. Marc, an old friend of his father, finds himself saddled with the dead man's massive debts and devises a plan to steal and traffic the STBO serum. He seeks out Alex as the essential recruit for the job. Alex joins the heist, but when he meets Marc’s lover, Anna, he feels a profound sense of destiny, and the story begins to unfold.
